# D2 笔记05：Butterfield《The Peace Tactics of Napoleon 1806-1808》(1929) archive.org全文缓存 downloads/pages/83caafca03de.md（行号为缓存行）

## 普鲁士1806
- L380-392（p.5）：1806-10-05普使向塔列朗递最后通牒，10-08到期；10-12拿破仑作最后和平呼吁，普王称信未及时收到；10-14耶拿；普王随后请停战，拿破仑拒绝（Corr. XIII No.11,031："Any suspension of arms which would give time for the Russian armies ... would be too inimical to my interests"；"the fourth coalition in fifteen years"）
- L655-700（pp.11-12）：Lucchesini带回的条件：普放弃莱茵—易北间省份与对德关系；不干预德意志、承认莱茵邦联扩张；保留马格德堡和老马克；重心东移——"to concentrate upon her rôle of buttress against the powers of eastern Europe"；Butterfield解释："He had wanted to keep Prussia as an ally, as a client state; she had been useful to him because she could check or frighten Russia; but she had chosen to adopt rather the rôle of a German power ... she had been willing to leave him his influence in south Germany, his Confederation of the Rhine, but she had come to issue with him over the question of the leadership of the north."；"the exclusion of Prussia from the Germanic system"（Bailleu II 577）；巨额赔款、对英闭港、保证奥斯曼完整，若俄攻之则与法共同防卫
- L716-728（p.13）：11月18日得知俄军推进，拿破仑"considered this whole treaty as inadmissible and immediately cancelled all its arrangements"
- L730-760（pp.13-14）：10-26普王派Zastrow至夏洛滕堡；10-28与Duroc重谈；10-30接受新基础（须禁俄军入境、若法俄战须随法作战）；11-06 Graudenz会议：全体认为不可再战，Haugwitz认为稍有犹豫拿破仑就会加价（Ranke, Hardenberg V 396）
- L775-790（p.14）：11-07普王致拿破仑信称已阻止俄军前进；Haugwitz致Lucchesini："Prussia must not be made too weak to act as a check against Russia"；自称"for twelve years has consistently worked to cement the relations between France and Prussia"；盼塔列朗出面，抵制"that terrible principle of the destruction of Prussia to guarantee the future repose of France"
- L1255-1300（pp.25-26）：致元老院咨文：在全面和平、殖民地归还、土耳其独立获保证前不撤出波兰与柏林；只在"do not permit anybody to arrogate to himself any rights of superiority over us"基础上议和；同时宣布大陆体系；Butterfield："a series of thorough but conditional and temporary conquests in Europe, which were to be the pledge of a general peace. Napoleon would not subdue Prussia for ever or annex the state finally into his Empire, but he would occupy the territory until his enemies should find it to their own advantage to redeem it."
- L1344-1356（p.27）：拿破仑"had even talked of deposing the house of Brandenburg and enthroning another reigning family in its stead"（1806年秋的取消普王朝之议——Butterfield叙述）；后决定把占领作军事与外交筹码；"Prussia was not allowed to make peace, was not allowed even to accept defeat"
- L1359-1400（pp.27-28）：11-16 Zastrow、Lucchesini签停战，第6条须11-21前换文；Duroc奉命稍有异议即返；11-21 Osterode会议：军人认为交要塞不算大损失；Stein（与Hardenberg联络）指出停战与塔列朗11-16照会矛盾，普不能履约驱俄，要塞将一去不返；Beyme补充东普问题；"The armistice was rejected. The king placed his fate and future in the hands of Russia."（Ranke, Hardenberg V 398；FO 64/71 G. Jackson）
- L1505-1515（p.31）：国王拒批"with regret, not with indignation"；普鲁士是反法同盟中"scarcely more than a mere passenger"

## 奥地利1806-07
- L4640-4700（pp.107-108）：1806夏人们以为奥地利会先于普鲁士再战；弗朗茨被迫放弃神圣罗马帝国称号；卡塔罗纠纷；Larochefoucauld："he is absolutely Russian and his policy is diametrically opposed to France"（7-23，AAE Autriche 379）；截获Stadion训令称拿破仑"only seeks to augment his sway for the advancement of his family and his minions"；奥真正方针：与英俄秘密往来、避免引起法国怀疑，暗中积蓄
- L4682-4692（p.108）：1806-08-13 Stadion向Adair密告：防御体系、不阻法军过境、不承认那不勒斯新安排、不再割地、受攻则避免会战退入匈牙利（Adair to Fox, FO Austria 7/80）
- L4712-4760（p.109-110）：同时Stadion向Larochefoucauld表示愿与法更紧密，指示梅特涅（新任驻巴黎）消除不信任；原因：乌布里条约使奥地利恐慌；"This nervousness of Austria is the key to her conduct for the whole of the ensuing year"
- L4762-4775（p.110）：英国不催促、不收买奥地利（Howick 11-14）
- L4786-4800（p.110-111）：拿破仑在对普分歧加深时改变语气："It is absolutely necessary to remove every difficulty with Austria ... by fixing our boundaries in Italy"；乌布里条约被拒后提出对奥同盟
- L4822-4840（p.111）：1806年9月中旬拿破仑向维也纳提议以1756年条约为基础结盟，规定奥斯曼完整与萨克森独立，矛头指向彼得堡与柏林；若拒绝则与普结盟；（Talleyrand to Larochefoucauld 10-12）
- L4843-4870（p.112）：维尔茨堡选侯（Elector of Würzburg）作为另一次试探的中间人；耶拿后Stadion转向法使；Larochefoucauld与塔列朗试图让拿破仑免除奥地利对普战争义务、专注于对俄；拿破仑不许例外，"played with the idea of a partition of Prussia as an inducement to the Austrians"
- L4870-4900（p.113）：11月初俄军西进后拿破仑换Andréossy（军人）为使，要求奥裁军；同时重申同盟、保证奥属波兰省份，令Andréossy"bring up lightly the question of the exchange of Galicia for Silesia"（Handelsman, Napoléon et la Pologne pp.215-18）；奥停止武装但不回应
- L4912-4940（pp.113-114）：耶拿后奥地利"haunted by the fear that if she makes a false move Napoleon will turn aside to finish with her"；对盟国推说军备未齐、财政枯竭、需恢复；"the Austrian Emperor will lay the responsibility for his decision at the door of the Archduke Charles who is resolutely opposed to war, while the Archduke Charles will say that the decision is not his, that he is not Emperor"；赢一仗还要再赢两仗才宣战；拿破仑问"What is the House of Austria after?"
- L4990-5010（p.115）：Pozzo di Borgo带沙皇信来维也纳，要求立即参战、不单独媾和、普若背离不尊重其中立
- L5164-5170（p.119）：Pozzo："their hearts are frozen with fear, it is the master-motive of their conduct"
- L5200-5205（p.120）：12-30 Stadion承认"it was morally impossible in the present state of things to look forward to neutrality as a permanent system"
- L5206-5220（p.120）：拿破仑的全面和平声明（11-16照会）给了维也纳"a rôle and a purpose"；12-06 Adair报告奥将提出全面和平调停
- L5232-5245（p.121）：英使Stuart担心调停延缓俄国努力；Budberg同意但俄不停止军事行动
- L5250-5270（p.121）：Stadion答Pozzo：调停"was the only one which could be reconciled with the differences of opinion here, particularly between the Archduke and the Emperor"（Pozzo 1807-01-14/26，转Vandal I 40）；以大军展示支持调停；拿破仑拒则奥必转而反法；若接受，谈判中"gradually drift from mediation into war"
- L5285-5310（p.122）：Merfeldt 2月中报告Budberg欢迎奥调停；普鲁士派Krusemarck提出类似建议但奥地利以大军为后盾地位更好
