# Cobbett’s Weekly Political Register. London, Saturday, 30th July, 1803.

Published: 2021-03-16. Book chapter. 0 citations.

## Abstract

There are also enclosed certain copies calculated for posting, one of which is intended to be placed on the church door and another in some such public part of the parish, as the people may deem best fitted for making it known among the Parishioners. Of what the French would, in such case, do here, the people may form some judgement from what they have done in all those countries, where the remissness of the government, together with the pusillanimity of the people, have given them the predominance. In the long and black catalogue of French cruelties towards the people of other countries, those of the First Consul, and of the generals and soldiers immediately under his command first present themselves to our attention. The people are one of those nations; they are the people whom they are now preparing to invade: awful, indeed, is the warning, and if the people despise it, tremendous will be the judgement.
