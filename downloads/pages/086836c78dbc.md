# Spanish Silver and International Banking during the Napoleonic Wars

Ler História. Published: 2026-01-01. 0 citations.

## Abstract

Between 1806 and 1808, the Dutch bank Hope & Co. and the English bank Baring were involved in silver shipments from Veracruz (Mexico) to Europe. This case study illustrates how the relationship between bankers and governments developed during the Napoleonic Wars. These international bankers acted as diplomatic agents between Spain, France, England, and the United States. The direct result of their actions was that they provided both the English and the French with the financial resources to keep their war machines running. However, this came at the expense of countries like Spain and Mexico. Indirect consequences of their actions included the boost they gave to American trade, the decline of the Spanish Empire, and discontent in Mexico. The research is based on Dutch and English archives, with new information uncovered in the Prize Papers of the High Court of Admiralty.
