I am an assistant professor at the University of Florida’s [Hamilton Center](https://hamilton.center.ufl.edu/). My research focuses on political economy and economic history.

I also serve as an Associate Editor at [Public Choice](https://www.springer.com/journal/11127).

I live with my wife Mary and my three sons, Émile, Julien & Léo.
