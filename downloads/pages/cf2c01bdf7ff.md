Index

Naval History of Great Britain 1793 - 1827 by William James published 1837

- [Vol I 1793-1796](https://sites.rootsweb.com/~pbtyc/Naval_History/Vol_I/Contents.html) - Including the Battle of the 1st June 1793 and much else.
- [Vol II 1797-1800](https://sites.rootsweb.com/~pbtyc/Naval_History/Vol_II/Contents.html) - Including the French Expeditions to Ireland, The Mutiny at Spithead, etc., Sir John Jervis off Cape St.-Vincent, Battle of Camperdown, and the Battle of the Nile.
- [Vol III 1800-1805](https://sites.rootsweb.com/~pbtyc/Naval_History/Vol_III/Contents.htm) - Inc. Battle of Copenhagen and much else
- [Vol IV 1805-1808](https://sites.rootsweb.com/~pbtyc/Naval_History/Vol_IV/Contents.htm) - Inc. Battle of Trafalgar and much more besides
- [Vol V 1808-1811](https://sites.rootsweb.com/~pbtyc/Naval_History/Vol_V/Contents.html) - Transcripts of Naval Actions from around the World
- [Vol VI 1811-1827](https://sites.rootsweb.com/~pbtyc/Naval_History/Vol_VI/Contents.html) - Inc. War of 1812, Battles of Algiers (1816), and Navarin (1827), and Burmese War of 1824
