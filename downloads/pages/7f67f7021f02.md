INDEX TO THE DIRECT ROADS FROM LONDON.

The First Column contains the Name of the Place

The Second, the Name of the County.

The Third the Distance from London, in Miles and
Furlongs.

The Fourth, the Days on which the Markets are held.

The Fifth, the Page in which the Place is to be found.
A typical entries are:-

Abbey Holme / Cumb. / 310 4 / -- / 321

Aldstone Moor / Cumb. / 302 6 / Sat. / 320
