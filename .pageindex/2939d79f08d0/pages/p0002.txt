# Thierry Lentz: four questions on the 1812 Malet Affair

On 23 October 1812, with Napoleon and his army far away and under the cosh in Russia, General Malet attempted to overthrow the Napoleonic regime. In this, four-question interview, Thierry Lentz – author of [La conspiration du général Malet](https://www.napoleon.org/en/magazine/interviews/thierry-lentz-four-questions-on-the-1812-malet-affair/%22https:/www.napoleon.org/en/magazine/just_published/files/480355.asp%22), published by Perrin – addresses some of the key issues surrounding Malet’s outlandish coup d’état and in the process corrects one or two misinterpretations. (January 2012)
