   O n thefirstof February, 1697, Peter issued an ukase extend­
ing his blessing to the importation of tobacco. Imports of Brit­
ish tobacco mounted to 87,000 pounds in 1698, and bounded
to a peak of 1,450,000 pounds in 1700.9 Trade between Amer ­
ica and Russia had begun.
   This trade suffered from the beginning from competition
with cheap, smuggled Dutch tobacco, but the trade was impor­
tant enough for Americans to quickly protest to Queen Anne's
government in 1705 at the news that Englishmen and English
machines were going to Russia to process raw tobacco there.
T h e Americans feared that the Muscovites, once in possession
of the skills to transform tobacco from the raw leaf to a smok­
able product, would use the nearby Circassian or even home­
