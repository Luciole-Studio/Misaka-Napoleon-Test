the example of the "Champlin" and "Good Intent." Those two
were swift dispatch schooners, built and rigged for speed, not
applebowed merchantmen built for spaciousness and rigged for
slow dependability. T h e average stranded American merchant-
m e n could never hope to elude British cruisers in the Baltic.
T h e underwriters of Boston, w h o certainly knew the odds,

m a n hom e from Russia.31
