                       Postscript: Levett Harris

his constant companion and colleague during four and a half
gray years in Russia:

  Harris is one of those mixed characters w h o , with some very
  good qualities and great address, has not the firmness to
  withstand the temptation of profitable venality. His situation
  at St. Petersburg and the situation of the times, gave him both
  temptation and opportunity such as perhaps was never pre­
  sented to any other public officer of the United States; and he
  availed himself of it precisely to the extent which he thought
  he could do and escape detection. H e mad e a princely fortune
  by selling his duty and his office at the most enormous
  prices.2
