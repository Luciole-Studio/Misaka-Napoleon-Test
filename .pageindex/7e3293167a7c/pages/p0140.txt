   T h e chief opponents of Girard in his losing battle to make a
profit in European trade in 1809 were the Danes and Norwe­
gians. These peaceful Scandinavians, egged on by French
scheming and driven by English brutality, scourged the waters
around Norway and Denmark. In the process of bringing in
every vessel in 1809 that wasn't lucky, fast, or hiding behind a
British man-of-war, these corsairs of Frederick V I , king of
Denmark, captured sixty-one American vessels.10
