It was not an easy oath to keep in those days, when the Constable's only assistants were citizens taking their turn as Watchmen and very unwilling to obey the summons. The streets were lit, only on moonless winter nights, by one smoky lantern hung before each tenth door. Housebreakers and footpads abounded, and to these was added the yet more dreaded scourge of the Scourers, Mohocks, or other bands of dissolute young men about town, flushed with wine, primed for mischief, and equally ready to roll a decent citizen in the gutter, and to draw sword on him if he resented the outrage.
