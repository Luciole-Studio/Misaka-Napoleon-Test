There were, however, two events of greater importance, with which Saunders had nothing whatever to do, that rendered memorable his tenancy of Lloyd's Coffee House. The first was the addition of the "Memorandum" to the printed form of marine insurance policy; which was almost certainly the result of a meeting or meetings at Lloyd's Coffee House, but which can best be discussed in connection with the settlement of "Lloyd's Policy" thirty years later. The second was nothing less than the first appearance of Lloyd's Register Book.
