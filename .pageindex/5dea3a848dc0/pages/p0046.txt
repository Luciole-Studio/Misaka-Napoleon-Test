In a few cases, the ‘new’ regiments had merely been renamed: for example, the Húsares de Maria Luisa became the 1er Regi­ miento de Húsares de Extremadura, and the Tercios de Tejas (a force that had been raised for service in Texas, but had not yet been sent to America) the Cazadores de Sevilla.
