                                                           Notes on Contributors xv


Margrit Schulte Beerbühl is Professor in the Department of Modern History
II at the University of Düsseldorf. Her publications include Deutsche Kaufleute
in London: Welthandel und Einbürgerung 1660–1818 (2007) and ‘Trading with
the Enemy. Clandestine Networks during the Napoleonic Wars’, in Quaderni
Storici (2013). She has also co-edited Cosmopolitan Networks in Commerce and
Society 1660–1914 (2011) and Transnational Networks: German Migrants in the
British Empire 1670–1914 (2012). The Forgotten Majority: German Merchants in
London, Naturalization, and Global Trade 1660–1818 is forthcoming (2014)
and she is currently working on the Hamburg speculation bubble of 1799
and its global effects.
Robert Mark Spaulding is Professor of History at the University of North
Carolina at Wilmington. His research focuses on international con-
trol of the Rhine between 1789 and 1848 and his publications include
‘Changing Patterns of Rhine Commerce in the Era of French Hegemony,
1793–1813’, Vierteljahrschrift für Sozial- und Wirtschaftsgeschichte (2013)
and ‘Revolutionary France and the Transformation of the Rhine’, Central
European History (2011).
Alexandre Tchoudinov is Professor of History at the Institute of Universal
History in Moscow. His Gilbert Romme and Pavel Stroganov: History of an
Extraordinary Alliance (2010, in Russian) won the Anatole Leroy-Beaulieu
prize in 2010. His other publications include Englishmen’s Reflections on the
French Revolution: E. Burke, J. Mackintosh, W. Godwin (1996, in Russian), The
French Revolution: History and Myths (2007, in Russian), Le Précepteur franco-
phone en Europe (XVIIe–XIXe siècles) (2013) and Écrire l’histoire par temps de
guerre froide: Soviétiques et Français autour de la crise de l’Ancien Régime (2014).
Jann M. Witt is historian of the Deutscher Marinebund (German Naval
Association), curator of the Marine-Ehrenmal (German Naval Memorial) in
Laboe and temporary lecturer in naval history at the Marineschule Mürwik
(German Naval Academy). His publications on maritime and naval history
include Horatio Nelson – Triumph und Tragik eines Seehelden (2005), Piraten –
Eine Geschichte von der Antike bis heute (2011), Von Schwarz–Rot–Gold zu
Schwarz–Rot–Gold. Eine kurze Geschichte der deutschen Marinen von 1848 bis
heute (2011), Seefahrtsgeschichte Schleswig-Holsteins in der Neuzeit (2012) and
William Bligh – Die ‘Bounty’ war sein Schicksal (2014).
