CHAPTER XIV.

PEBSIA.

Persia comprises the greater portion of the Iranian plateau

rising on the north from the Caspian Sea
eogrp iy. ^^ Turkistan, on the east and west from the

Indus and Tigris valleys and on the south from the Arabian Sea and
Persian Gulf. The altitude of the plateau varies from four to eight
thousand feet above sea level. There are two great deserts, and a
great deal of the land is uncultivated. The northern frontier is
nearly thirteen hundred miles long, and was fixed by treaty between
Russia and Persia. The eastern frontier is nearly one thousand
miles in length. The Persia-Afghan boundary was fixed in 1872 by a
British Commission, and again in 1871 and 1902. That part of the
frontier adjoining Baluchistan was fixed in 1896. The western
frontier, seven hundred and fifty miles in length, has never been
properly demarcated.
