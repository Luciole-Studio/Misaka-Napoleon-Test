During the same month three companies of British mounted
infantry were raised and concentrated at Rangoon, the strength
being 15 officers and 300 rank and file. The men were drawn
from the 2nd Battalions of the West Riding Regiment, Essex
Regiment, and Durham Light Infantry and were mounted on
Burmese ponies. They embarked at Rangoon on the 24th Janu-
ary and arrived in South Africa on the 13th February. The
