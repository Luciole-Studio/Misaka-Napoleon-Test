and employment on the railway, as guards, ticket-collecters,
etc., where they would be dealing with the troops of foreign
contingents. Orders were consequently issued, towards the end
of February, for the despatch of half a battalion of the Koyal
Welsh Fusiliers from Hong-Kong to North China, to relieve the
Australian contingent ; they arrived at Tientsin on the 23rd
March and at Peking on the 25th ; the Australians at the latter
place leaving the following day for Tientsin, en route to Australia.
