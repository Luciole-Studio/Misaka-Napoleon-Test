CHAPTER IV.

THE INDIAN CONTINGENT IN EGYPT, 1882.

In consequence of the military revolt in Egypt, headed by
Arabi Bey, it was decided in July 1882 to despatch a British expedi-
tion to that country,* and that one division of the force should be
furnished by India.

In accordance with this plan, a scheme was drawn up by the
Commander-in-Chief in India (Sir Donald Stewart) for the despatch
to Egypt of a division consisting of two infantry brigades with
divisional troops. This scheme was approved by the Viceroy,
and the selected troops were warned for active service on the 6th
July. The composition of the force was as follows : —
