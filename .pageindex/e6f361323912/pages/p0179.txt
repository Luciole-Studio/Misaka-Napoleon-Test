At the beginning of February, 1904, the Boundary Commission
entered the Subaihi country. As several outrages had been committed
by this tribe and their temper was uncertain, a strong supporting
column was left at Musaimir, and a further column of 600 British
and Indian infantry, with two guns, was despatched to
Khatabia.

Some unimportant skirmishes followed, and Captain Warneford,
the Political Officer, was murdered by an Arab police naik in Govern-
ment employ.

A force of British and Indian infantry with a section of guns
remained at Dhali ' with the Political Officer.

In July the Kotaibi were again reported to be inclined to give
trouble, but, although a force was sent from Aden, hostile measures
proved unnecessary.
