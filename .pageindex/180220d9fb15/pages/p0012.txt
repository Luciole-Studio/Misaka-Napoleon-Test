The Role of the State in Providing Labor for the Plantations At the time plantations were opened up in the Central Highlands, labor, not land, was the scarce factor of production. A striking characteristic of the Kandyan Kingdom was its sparse population. According to the British Census Report of 1824, the population of the entire Kandyan Kingdom (which comprised roughly three-fourths of the whole island) was 256,835. The population of Kandy, the capital city in 1818 was only 3,000. 57
