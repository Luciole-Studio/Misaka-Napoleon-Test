Tocqueville foresaw the danger:

    The French Revolution, which abolished all privileges and
    destroyed all exclusive rights, has still let one survive—that of
    property.
       Landowners should be under no illusions about the strength of
    their situation, nor should they imagine that the right of property
    is an insurmountable bulwark because it has not yet been
    overcome….
       Soon, without any doubt, the struggle of political parties will
    be between those who own and those who do not own. The great
    battlefield will be property…7
