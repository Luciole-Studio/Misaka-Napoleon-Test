THE FRENCH AND EUROPE
What did French men and women know about Europe at the end of
the ancien regime? The question offers a convenient starting-point for
a discussion of the quarter of a century in which French arms and
Napoleon’s political ingenuity constructed and imposed on the greater
part of the Continent their particular model of the modern nation-
state.
   A wealth of indications can provide us with at least an
impressionistic response to so apparently simple a query. For the
overwhelming majority of this profoundly rural society the ‘foreigner’
was any ‘stranger’ from distant parts. Frequently he was the product
