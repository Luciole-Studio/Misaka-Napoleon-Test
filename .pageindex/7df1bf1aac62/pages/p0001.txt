## T1. 中国贸易对**英国国库**的价值（Milburn v2，行 71800–71830、82440–82470）
17 年（1792-3 至 1808-9）公司对华贸易在伦敦的销售总额 **£57,896,274**，其中：
- 茶 £55,160,230｜南京布 £848,425｜中国绸 £16,498｜瓷器 £82,001｜其余（多为生丝）£1,788,863

同期"国家净得"（dear gain to the nation）**£30,739,208**，构成：公司库付关税 £265,524＋运费与滞期 £10,886,017＋商业费用（按销售额5%）£2,894,815＋公司余额 £16,692,852。
另：
> "The above amount of duties is not the entire revenue derived from the trade with China, the greatest part being paid by the purchasers on clearing the goods for home consumption. The customs and excise duties received during the same period were as follow: Net duties, after payment of drawbacks, &c. and deducting the above £265,524 — £2,980,956; Excise duties on teas, which include the duty on private trade — £29,309,643; forming a total of £32,290,599, which, added to the above £30,739,208, forms a beneficial total to the nation, of £63,029,807, on an average of 17 years, **£3,707,636 per annum**."

百年账（行 82445–82470）：
> "Making a total of duty during the above period of £45,110,653. From 1794 to 1810 inclusive, the Excise duties collected on teas amounted to 29,309,643. From 1794 to 1810 inclusive, the Customs collected on the China trade were £3,246,480, four-fifths of which may be estimated to have arisen from tea — 2,597,184. Forming a total in the 100 years, 1711 to 1810 inclusive, of £77,017,480, a sum exceeding the amount of the national debt of England in 1756."
加上糖税归因：
> "it may be fairly stated that, immediately and remotely, the revenue is at present benefited by tea to the extent of **£5,000,000 sterling per annum**."

**读数（对 B2/B5/P8 直接有用）**：1800 年代英国政府岁入约 £60–70m 量级（见 B2 卡 BoE millennium 序列），则**茶—中国贸易为英国国库提供约 3.7–5.0m/年，占 5–8%**，而且这是**拿破仑的大陆体系在物理上完全够不着的一条税基**。任何"封锁使英国财政窒息"的推演，都必须先解释如何切断这条线——而切断它需要海军而非关税法令。
口径警告：£3.7m 的"beneficial total"混合了关税消费税（真国库收入）与公司利润、运费（非国库收入）。**只有 £32.29m/17 ≈ £1.9m/年是硬国库收入**；£5m/年是 Milburn 含糖税归因的宽口径。本卡两个口径并列，**不取中间值**。
