# IN1 贸易与汇兑笔记

主源：William Milburn, *Oriental Commerce*（London 1813），两卷。
本地：`downloads/IN1_milburn_v1.txt`（archive.org `in.ernet.dli.2015.181513`）、`downloads/IN1_milburn_v2.txt`（`dli.ernet.26367`）。Milburn 时任东印度公司驻华/海事部门职员，数据多取自公司账册与海关，属**近官方来源**，非独立审计。

## T1. 中国贸易对**英国国库**的价值（Milburn v2，行 71800–71830、82440–82470）
17 年（1792-3 至 1808-9）公司对华贸易在伦敦的销售总额 **£57,896,274**，其中：
- 茶 £55,160,230｜南京布 £848,425｜中国绸 £16,498｜瓷器 £82,001｜其余（多为生丝）£1,788,863

同期"国家净得"（dear gain to the nation）**£30,739,208**，构成：公司库付关税 £265,524＋运费与滞期 £10,886,017＋商业费用（按销售额5%）£2,894,815＋公司余额 £16,692,852。
另：
> "The above amount of duties is not the entire revenue derived from the trade with China, the greatest part being paid by the purchasers on clearing the goods for home consumption. The customs and excise duties received during the same period were as follow: Net duties, after payment of drawbacks, &c. and deducting the above £265,524 — £2,980,956; Excise duties on teas, which include the duty on private trade — £29,309,643; forming a total of £32,290,599, which, added to the above £30,739,208, forms a beneficial total to the nation, of £63,029,807, on an average of 17 years, **£3,707,636 per annum**."

百年账（行 82445–82470）：
> "Making a total of duty during the above period of £45,110,653. From 1794 to 1810 inclusive, the Excise duties collected on teas amounted to 29,309,643. From 1794 to 1810 inclusive, the Customs collected on the China trade were £3,246,480, four-fifths of which may be estimated to have arisen from tea — 2,597,184. Forming a total in the 100 years, 1711 to 1810 inclusive, of £77,017,480, a sum exceeding the amount of the national debt of England in 1756."
加上糖税归因：
> "it may be fairly stated that, immediately and remotely, the revenue is at present benefited by tea to the extent of **£5,000,000 sterling per annum**."

**读数（对 B2/B5/P8 直接有用）**：1800 年代英国政府岁入约 £60–70m 量级（见 B2 卡 BoE millennium 序列），则**茶—中国贸易为英国国库提供约 3.7–5.0m/年，占 5–8%**，而且这是**拿破仑的大陆体系在物理上完全够不着的一条税基**。任何"封锁使英国财政窒息"的推演，都必须先解释如何切断这条线——而切断它需要海军而非关税法令。
口径警告：£3.7m 的"beneficial total"混合了关税消费税（真国库收入）与公司利润、运费（非国库收入）。**只有 £32.29m/17 ≈ £1.9m/年是硬国库收入**；£5m/年是 Milburn 含糖税归因的宽口径。本卡两个口径并列，**不取中间值**。

孟加拉鸦片出口值（Sicca Rs）：1795-6 13,08,360｜1799-1800 28,80,593｜1802-3 39,43,951｜1803-4 41,88,225｜1804-5 64,12,283｜1805-6 58,66,888。
1805-6 去向（Sicca Rs）：**中国 32,94,570**｜槟榔屿及以东 21,25,209｜苏门答腊 3,20,748｜马尼拉 27,661｜科罗曼德尔 61,968｜白古 15,110｜阿拉伯与波斯湾 15,755｜马拉巴尔 15,515。
1795-6 全部东向仅 11,63,835，其中对华仅 2,51,450。

**读数**：1795-6→1805-6 十年间**对华鸦片输出由 2.5 lakh 增至 32.9 lakh Sicca Rs（约13倍）**；鸦片专卖收入在 1804-5、1807-8 达 70–80 万英镑，约当孟加拉领地岁入的 8–9%（对照 S2.3 孟加拉 1805-6 预算岁入约 £8.76m）。这是**战时唯一高速增长且不依赖欧洲市场的税源**。
口径冲突：Hansard 1806 韦尔斯利陈述的 1803-4 孟加拉鸦片税目为 Sicca Rs 3,989,200（按 2s/SR ≈ £498,650），Milburn 同年为 £463,161——**两口径差约 7%，并列保留，不平账**。

## T3. 孟买—中国棉花与"汇款机器"（Milburn v1，行 43063–43105）
> "The export of cotton from Bombay to China has in some seasons amounted to 80,000 bales, each half a candy, or about 375 lbs. making in the whole 30 millions of pounds."
> "The East India Company have within these few years become participators in the trade from Bombay to China ... **They reserve to themselves two thirds of the chartered tonnage of their ships destined from Bombay to China**; and in the event of their not occupying it, it is disposed of by open competition ... on condition that the freight, together with the proceeds of the goods, **shall be paid into the Canton treasury, for the latter of which bills are granted on England at the current rate of exchange**."
> "The cotton trade from this side of India to China is but of recent date. It commenced about 35 years ago."（＝约1778年起）
> 竞争：孟加拉与马德拉斯商人以更洁净的棉在广州卖出更高价，威胁孟买。
> "the specie thrown in by this commerce, aids the circulation at Bombay very considerably, amounting on an average of five years, 180〔2〕 to 1806, to Sicca Rupees 27,03,907 per annum."

**读数（本卡最关键的机制之一）**：印度的对华输出（棉花＋鸦片）→ 广州库房 → 公司开出**伦敦汇票** → 私商把在亚洲赚到的钱汇回英国，同时公司得到在广州购茶的现金。这条链条使公司**不必从英国运白银去中国**（与 Hansard 1806 韦尔斯利的自述互证：见 notes_finance S2.5）。
→ 推演含义：在"英国被逐出欧洲"的情景里，**这台汇款机器不依赖任何欧洲港口**；它依赖的是（a）广州口岸开放、（b）印度洋航线安全、（c）印度本身的岁入与生产。法国要打断它，必须在印度洋而非欧洲动手。
