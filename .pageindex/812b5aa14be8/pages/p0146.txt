 20. Javier Mina’s father was a man of some local standing, judging from his political prominence (he was the deputy for Otano in valleywide meetings) and his wealth. In 1802 the elder Mina agreed to take over the considerable
debt of María Theresa de Ilundáin, Espoz y Mina’s mother; in 1807 he purchased an obra pía confiscated and sold by the state; and during the war, the elder Mina was able to lend grain to various individuals and communities.
Indeed, Javier’s father appears to have been a war profiteer. APN, Pamplona, Peralta, legajo 51, nos. 11, 143; legajo 54, nos. 53, 54, 63.
