 39. Report of 1 June 1811, AAT, C8, 378. The French report of this action by Cafarelli is incomplete, since the general reports only the casualties (130) among the Thirty-second Fusiliers. Cafarelli gave a brief account of the
battle. The most interesting fact to emerge in his report is that the English prisoners, instead of taking advantage of the attack to escape, shamelessly retrieved French guns and fired upon the guerrillas. For casualty estimates
I relied on Andrés Martín, Historia de los sucesos militates, 1:128-32, whose figures are much lower than those given in the other sources. Mina and the battalion diaries place the French losses at 800 killed and 600 captured.
Casualties among the Navarrese are known only for the second and third battalions, which lost 13 killed and 49 wounded.
