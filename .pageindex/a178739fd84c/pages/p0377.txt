   Of the eight foundered British-built vessels, one, the Malabar, had been
 an East-Indiaman. Seven of the others were sloops, the largest of which did
not exceed 324 tons ; and it is even doubtful whether three of those were
not wrecked. The remaining vessel was the Leda frigate ; which vessel,
according to one account, upset in a heavy squall, according to another
account, struck on a sunken rock, and, according to a third, filled in con¬
sequence of having her side stove by some of her guns that had broken loose
in a severe gale of wind: in fact, the fate of the Leda is still involved in
mystery.
