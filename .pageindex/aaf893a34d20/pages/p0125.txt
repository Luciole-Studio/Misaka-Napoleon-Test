             The nuns of Jesús also owned a group of fields in Villaverde and Pajares, the next town to the north. The holdings in Villaverde
       consisted of thirty-three arable plots, a meadow, a cortina, and a house. The predicted rent for the plots in Villaverde is 21.125 fanegas
       of wheat. [14] The predicted rent for its holdings in Pajares is 13.05 fanegas of wheat, for a total in both towns of about 34 fanegas.[15]
