            This limited evidence indicates that the tenants paid more rent than the rule stated in the catastro. In La Mata the rule proved too
       high; the more complex calculation reported for Villaverde gives results that are probably too low. Under the circumstances, I shall use an
       estimate for rent 20 percent above that predicted by the rule in the catastro. Table 8.8 lists the property of outside owners and the
       estimate of the rent they would have collected, 1,050 fanegas of wheat. The total harvest on this property, using the yields shown in
       Table 8.2, would be 3,225 EFW. Increasing this amount by 5.6 percent, according to the correction derived from the tithe returns, one
       can predict a total harvest of 3,406 EFW. The rent would then be about 31 percent of the total harvest.
