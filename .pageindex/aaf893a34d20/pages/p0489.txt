Rural Change and Royal Finances in Spain




                                                                                     6.
                                                The Desamortización of Carlos IV. Page 2 of a deed of deposit issued by
                                               the notary López Fando of Madrid. It represents the royal obligation to the
                                               Cofradía Laical de Santa María Magdalena of the city of Jaén to cover the
                                             sale of an olive grove of nine trees to Cristoval Carrillo for seventeen hundred
                                            reales, as can be seen by the manuscript writing in the blank space provided for
                                            that purpose. The king promises to pay the Cofradía fifty-seven reales per year.
