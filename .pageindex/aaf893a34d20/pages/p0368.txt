       contained less than 250 hectares, a figure that has been considered the minimum size of a large property or latifundio in twentieth-

             The evidence from censuses permits one to assign the decline in population of most despoblados and alquerías to one of three
       periods: prior to the census of vecinos of 1534, between 1534 and the census of vecinos of 1712, and the period between 1712 and
       1826, for which we have the catastro (ca. 1751–53), the census of 1786, and Miñano's dictionary with figures for 1826. Table 18.3 shows
       the distribution among these three periods of those places for which some census data are available. Since the first census is for 1534,
       we can know only whether a place was at its demographic low point by that date. We do not know that it had ever been larger, and in
       view of the doubts of Cabo Alonso on this question, one should leave it open. Some certainly had been larger at some previous time.
