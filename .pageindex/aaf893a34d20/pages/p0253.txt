             Besides the share of the harvests taken by outside owners and administrators, the main payments leaving the economy were the
       tithes and other religious charges and the royal taxes. Because Lopera belonged to the jurisdiction of the Order of Calatrava, all the
       regular tithes on grain and olive oil belonged to the king as grand master. The crown had rented them out, however, in a contract

              We are now in a position to draw up the balance sheet for the income of the town (Table 12.12). The net total is approximately
       19,700 EFW. Divided among a population of 1,260, this provided 15.6 EFW per capita, well over the 12 EFW threshold. If properly
       distributed among the vecinos, the income would give them all a comfortable living. But how well was it divided?
