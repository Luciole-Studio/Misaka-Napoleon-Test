       The data collected for this study permit one to look at the despoblados in several new ways. Most of the following discussion is limited to
       the despoblados for which there are individual surveys of the catastro, but, unlike previous studies, it also considers the alquerías. Table
       18.1 shows what percentage of the catastro units in each zone fell into these two categories. Those with the greatest concentration of
       despoblados and alquerías were the four plains zones, SA to SD, and the hilly zone SF that juts between zones SB and SC. The following
       analysis is limited to these five zones.
