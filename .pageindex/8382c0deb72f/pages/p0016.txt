About the author

[David Schimmelpenninck van der Oye](https://independent.academia.edu/DavidSchimmelpenninckvanderOye)

addFollowmailMessage

David recently retired as Professor of Russian history at Brock University in St. Catharines, Canada, and is a Fellow of the Royal Society of Canada. His research focuses on 18th- and 19th-century Russian cultural, intellectual, diplomatic and military history, and he is the author of several acclaimed books, including Toward the Rising Sun: Russian Ideologies of Empire and the Path to War with Japan and Russian Orientalism: Asia in the Russian Mind from Peter the Great to the Emigration. David is currently working on a book about tsarist expansion into Central Asia titled, Russia’s Great Game: The Struggle for Primacy in Central Asia, and is co-editing a volume on The International History of Russia’s Great War for the Russia’s Great War and Revolution series.
