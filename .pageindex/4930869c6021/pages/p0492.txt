 5. NEW SELF-GOVERNMENT: THE JUNTAS

     * It seems doubtful that the words ‘Independence’ and ‘Revolution’ were taken over from the American War of Independence. Compared to her ally France, Spain’s intervention in that war, and its impact on the country, had been
minimal; moreover a Republic was not generally envisaged as a solution to Spain’s problems. In any event, what was missing in the Spanish case was an equivalent of the American Declaration of Independence which stated to what
political ends the Independence that had resulted from the Revolution was aimed at.
