The Indian project, as revived in 1807-8, was not the same 
as laid down in 1805. In 1805 the expedition was intended 
to be sent by sea via the Ope of Good Hope, still in Dutch 
hands. In 1807-8 the expedition was planned to follow the

Afghanistan. The basis of the plan was provided by the Treat) 
of Finkenstein with Persia, which will be noticed in the next 
section. But the project of an expedition by the sea route was 
not altogether abandoned. It was to be attempted, although 
on a smaller scale than what was planned in 1805 and only as 
subsidiary to the main expedition by land. Such combined 
attacks were deemed necessary to ensure success. On the 16th
