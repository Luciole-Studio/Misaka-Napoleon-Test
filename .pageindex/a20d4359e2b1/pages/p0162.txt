       15. Salva was a native of Mallorca who had supported the liberal cause in
 1810 and 1820. In 1820 he willingly took the required oath to the constitution

France, from where he returned in the 1830’s. His brother was elected a pro-

families, eleven from the middle classes, and thirty-two from the lower classes,
