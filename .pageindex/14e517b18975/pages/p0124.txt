  On May 19, the fleet carrying Bonaparte’s Army of the Orient
set sail in Toulon harbor and, taking advantage of a sudden
storm, evaded Nelson’s force and vanished into the open waters of
the Mediterranean. While Nelson vainly sought his foe in the
Atlantic, Bonaparte sailed directly east to Malta, which he took
from the Knights of St. John on June 9. Eight days later, after
replenishing his supplies and refreshing his men, he took his
fleet out of the harbor, and once again it disappeared into the wide
expanses of the Mediterranean. It was only in late June that Ruf-
