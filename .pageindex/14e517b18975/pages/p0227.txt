          57. BVA, Cevdet Maliye 2019; TKS, E 3967. Tax farms (Iltizam)
      were purchased at auctions (Mizad or Muzayede), at which those wishing
      to acquire them competed with each other, with the highest bidder being
      awarded the contract for the year. Ordinarily tax farm contracts were
      awarded only when the bidding pushed the sale price over an amount
      equal to five times the average annual profit (faiz) left to the previous
      tax farmer. When the Irad-i Cedid treasury took over an IItizam formerly
      farmed out by the regular treasury, it paid the latter the equivalent of
      this price. Once a tax farm was given to a tax farmer {Multezim), he was
      required to deliver a certain amount to the Treasury every year, and was
      allowed to keep the balance as profit for himself. The Irad-i Cedid treas¬
      ury now took over this function for the tax farms in question. See Stan¬
      ford J. Shaw, The Financial and Administrative Organization and
      Development of Ottoman Egypt (Princeton, N. J., 1962), pp. 34-40;
      Pakahn, II, 57-58; BVA Muhasebe 6094, fol. 128b.
