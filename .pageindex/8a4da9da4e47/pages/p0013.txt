preceding) the Day of giving Notice for raising such Sum or Sums
of Money, and that the Rate of Interest to be allowed to the Contri-
butors for such Sum or Sums of Money shall in no Case exceed Five
Shillings per Centum per Annum above the current Rate of Interest
produced by the Market Price of any such Redeemable Perpetual
Annuities or Annuities for Terms of Years existing at the Time, and
in which such Contracts shall be made.
