 11   Janissary is a word that covers a wide variety of soldiers: imperial Janissaries consisted of infantry and four regiments of cavalry, also referred to as Sipahis , not be confused with the sipahis , holders of timars , discussed later in the chapter. Some statistics combine them, others separate the two. Furthermore, a full range of auxiliary forces, such as sappers, miners, and artillerymen, were also members of the corps. Official Ottoman records kept them strictly separate, but foreign observers were less in tune to the nuances. Hence, we have the wide variance in numbers. The auxiliaries will be taken up in due course. Janissaries of all sorts served in all the garrisons and will also be discussed later.
