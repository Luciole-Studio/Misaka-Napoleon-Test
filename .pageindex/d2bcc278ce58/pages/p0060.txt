 *   The term Nizâm- ı Cedid has both a general and specific meaning: it serves as a synonym for the Selim III era, hence embodying his entire reform agenda, but it was also used to designate the new, modernised troops he attempted to introduce.

 3   The two versions are from the two significant historians of the late eighteenth century: Ahmed Vâs ı f Efendi (d. 1807) and Sadullah Enverî (d. 1795?), illustrating the diverse views in governing circles.
