PHILIP ZIEGLER was born in December
1929 and educated at Eton and New
College, Oxford, where he gained first
class honours in Jurisprudence. He then
joined the Diplomatic Service and served
in Vientiane, Paris, Pretoria and Bogota
before resigning to join the publishers
William Collins, where he was editorial
director for fifteen years. His books have
included biographies of William IV,
Melbourne and Lady Diana Cooper, a
study of the Black Death, and the official
biography of Mountbatten. He lives in
London with his wife and three children.
