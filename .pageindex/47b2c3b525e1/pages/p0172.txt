The crisis of these years was an historical event; the conflicts
that shaped it, and whose course it in turn shaped, perpetuated
themselves, partly in the upheavals of 1848 and 1860, but still
more in a divided and half-petrified society, and in that moral
divorce between Sicily and the continental Power that rules it
which has affected so much of the island’s history, even down
to the present day. No one who knows how much there is in
that history of bitterness and frustration can feel quite satisfied.
