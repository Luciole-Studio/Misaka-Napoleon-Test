  Government wish to get rid of me, why do not they give me poison, or have me

  arrive; but I solemnly declare, I will never go there alive’, etc.: Robert Fagan to
  Bentinck, 23 Dec. 1811, FO 70/44. ‘She said there was only one way to save their
  honour, that was [for the King] to put himself at the head of his Neapolitans and
  return to Naples. The King might perish but he would die like Tippoo Saib upon the
  breach’: Journal, 13 Jan. 1812.
