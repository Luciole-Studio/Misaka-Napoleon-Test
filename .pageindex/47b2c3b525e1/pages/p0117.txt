      THE KING'S RETURN:           THE QUEEN’S DEPARTURE               IOI
  On May 27th, three weeks before the Queen’s departure,
Bentinck sailed to Spain, though Castlereagh had instructed
him to remain in Sicily on account of the crisis:
   “When I reflect that the crisis to which this order has reference is past
—that the causes of all our past embarrassments have absolutely termi-
nated—that the Queen’s influence exists no more—that the King’s hos-
tility is neutralised—that full and unlimited powers have been delegated
to the Vicar General—that his administration is composed without
exception of popular characters—that this administration appears to enjoy
the Prince’s confidence, I am warranted in saying that the system against
which we have been contending for the last six years, and in support of
which the last struggle has been ineffectually made, is completely de-
molished.’

  The Constitution, too, was on the point of being sanctioned
at last. It was a confident parting flourish. But then it was meant
for official consumption.*
