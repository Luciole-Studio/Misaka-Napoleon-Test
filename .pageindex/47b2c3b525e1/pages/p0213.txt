   suggested to Castlereagh in December that the British Government should offer the

   refusal, until the arrival of Castlereagh’s instructions, to consider advancing money
  to the Queen.—There was no compassion lost on the Queen in London: ‘Whatever
  you have arranged with regard to the Lady in Sicily I am convinced is the best. . . .
  If we have not sent you any precise instructions regarding her, it has been because
  we felt we could securely rest in your discretion. . . . If it is necessary for her to be
  sent out of Sicily . . . for my own part, I shall be very glad .. .”: Bathurst to Bentinck
  (private) 25 Dec. 1812, B.P. Castlereagh, for his part, was particularly annoyed by
