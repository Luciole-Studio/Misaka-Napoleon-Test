relationships with the rest of the world (including its colonized economy of
Ireland) over a revealing chronology (say from the passage of Cromwell’s
navigation act of 1651 to its repeal two centuries later) continue to look
entirely remote. Indeed a line of distinguished scholars who have
grappled with the sources consider such accounts could not be
constructed within acceptable margins of error. 145
