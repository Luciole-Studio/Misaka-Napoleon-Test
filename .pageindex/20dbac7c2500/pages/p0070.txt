promoted the migration of shipbuilding to “out ports” up the coast and
around the Isles towards pools of cheaper labour in the North, Scotland
and Ireland. 163 Wars for American independence operated to raise the
shipbuilding and shipping industries of the United States along with those

and commerce. Competition continued and intensified after the Peace of
