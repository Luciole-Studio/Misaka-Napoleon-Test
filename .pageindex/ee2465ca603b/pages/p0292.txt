   The actual, as opposed to the established strength, was rather different
and excessive, especially in fourth-class seamen and boys. The ‘super¬
numeraries’ evidently included the Admiral’s staff—two aides-de-camp,
secretary, clerk and assistant clerk. Before sailing, there were apparently
626 men on board the ship and 11 in hospital. Duclos makes the total 593.
The Belle Poule had 274; L’Atalante, 248; and Semillante, 215.
