## S1. 1812《Papers respecting the negociation ... renewal of the East-India Company's exclusive privileges》
本地：`downloads/IN1_papers_negociation_1812.txt`（archive.org id `papersrespecting00east`，1812，HC印行）
sha256 见 SOURCES.md。OCR 为 18 世纪长 s（f/s 混），数字多处需还原。

### S1.1 董事会（Court of Directors）1812 年自述：印度债务由 800 万涨到约 3,200 万
行 359–366：
> "The situation of the Company is, at this time, very different from what it was at the last renewal of the Charter, in 1793. European war, with hardly any inter〔m〕ission, through the whole of the period that has since elapsed, has exceedingly encreased the expenses, and reduced the profits of the Company at home; and has likewise enlarged the scale of expenses abroad; where, moreover, wars with the Native Powers have been repeatedly carried on, to the vast accumulation of the Indian debt, now advanced from eight million sterling, at which it stood in 1793, to about thirty-two millions."

读数：**印度债务 1793 ≈ £8m → 1812 ≈ £32m**（董事会官方口径，非独立审计）。为 Wellesley 战争债务的总量级锚点。注意：这是"Indian debt"（在印度发行的 registered/bond debt），不含伦敦的 bond debt（见 S1.3）。

### S1.2 董事会 1812 年官方立场：法国陆路入侵印度属"欧洲战争、欧洲目标"，应由英国国库负担
行 396–414（同函第 5 点）：
> "To apportion duly between the Public and the Company the milita〔r〕y expenses of the Indian empire, is another measure now become unavoidable. For wars growing out of the Indian system, or out of sources purely Indian, the revenues and other means of the Company were long made to suffice, including even the charges of occasional attacks upon the Indian settlements of Euro〔p〕ean nations; but the influence of European war has, in the present protracted period of hostility, extended itself more and more to India, occasioning the expense of various distant expeditions, and the encrease of the military establishment, particularly in the European troops of his Majesty, which, from being twenty years ago only a very few regiments, now amount to above twenty thousand* men, and those of the most expensive description of troops composing the military force of British India: nor is at all improbable, that from the avowed design of France to invade our Indian possessions with great armies by land, it may be necessary still largely to augment our European force in that quarter. For a war of this description the Indian revenues, if unincumbered with debt, would be very inadequate. It would be an European war for European objects; a struggle between Great Britain and France, on the soil of India, for the maintenance and support of their power in Europe. For such an object, to which the national funds only are commensurate, the national funds undoubtedly ought to provide"

读数（**本卡最重要的反应函数锚点**）：
1. 公司官方承认 King's 欧洲兵在印已 **>20,000 人**（1812），且是"最昂贵的兵种"；
2. 公司官方承认：若发生法国陆路大军入侵，**即使债务全清，印度岁入也"very inadequate"**；
3. 公司据此要求把该类战费划归 **national funds（英国国库）**。
→ 卡片 falsifier（"1805–12公司已无法自付驻军→承压能力下调"）在 1812 年由公司自己的文件**部分证成**：不是不能付常规驻军，而是**不能付"欧洲级"战争**。区分必须保留。

### S1.4 私人贸易的汇款性质（1812-03-04 Robert Wissett 签署）
行 2400–2410：
> "The exports of individuals from Britain are: Bullion £194,576 / Goods £305,496 = £500,072. The imports of ditto into Britain £940,956. Difference, per annum £446,884 — being not a trade but a remittance, deducting the amount of the profit on exports."

读数：公司官方把英印私人贸易顺差解释为**汇款（remittance）而非贸易**——即印度财富向英国的单向转移机制的同时代自述。对"印度能否自养"一问关键：印度的"盈余"有一部分是被结构性抽走的（home charges 前身）。

### S2.5 Wellesley 方（Sir Arthur Wellesley 在同一辩论）的辩护数字

## S3. 1813-02-22 公司请愿（特许状续期）HC Deb vol 24 cc659–76
本地：`downloads/pages/db711396db6a.md`

### S3.1 财务自述（公司官方口径，1813年初）
> "at that time [1793] the capital stock of the petitioners amounted to the sum of 5,000,000l.; since that period ... the capital stock has been encreased by the sum of 1,000,000l. contributed by the subscribers at the rate of 200l. per cent."
> "sundry debts incurred in the defence and protection of the British possessions in India, bearing interest, were then due and owing by the petitioners, amounting to 7,000,000l. sterling, or thereabouts; since that period the said debt has been very much increased for the same purpose ... the petitioners have been obliged to provide out of their funds and credit at home, the means of paying bills of exchange drawn upon them since the year 1807, to the amount of 10,902,924l. sterling, in discharge of Indian debt, and the debt contracted for political purposes, now remaining due in India, according to the latest advices from thence, amounts to the sum of 26,000,000l. or thereabouts, over and besides the sum of 3,000,000l. reduced 3 per cent. annuities, and the sum of 1,400,000l. consolidated 3 per cent. annuities, on which the sum of 2,500,000l. sterling was raised ... and also over and besides the sum of 2,202,000l. or thereabouts, now owing by the petitioners upon bills of exchange not yet due, but payable in London"
> "the revenues of the territorial acquisitions in India ... in the year 1793 amounted to the annual sum of 8,000,000l. or thereabouts, and by the latest account and estimates ... amounts to the annual sum of 16,000,000l. or thereabouts, but the civil and military expenses of the government have proportionably increased"
> "the profits of the trade carried on by the petitioners since the year 1793 ... have amounted to the sum of 6,289,405l., over and above the commercial charges ... and beyond the payment of interest on their bond debt in England, and besides the dividends"
> "in 1793, the bond debt in England of the petitioners amounted to the sum of 3,200,000l. ... their bond debt in England now amounts to the sum of 5,409,325l., but the petitioners are entitled by law to issue bonds to the amount of 7,000,000l."
> "the annual interest upon the present amount of the Indian debt now amounts to the sum of 1,600,000l. ... the petitioners estimate that the annual sum of 1,500,000l. or thereabouts, will be necessary to be provided annually in London for the payment of such interest"
> 另：1812年法案筹措之 £2.5m 年金的利息与偿债基金合计 **£242,820/年**，法定由印度岁入承担但须在英格兰银行支付。
> 政治性开支在英国支付者（含对卡纳蒂克已故纳瓦布债权人付款）估 **£910,000/年**。
> 自1807年以来印度汇票总额 **£13,104,924**（已清 10,902,924，余 2,202,000，1814年3月前大部到期）——见同日另一请愿 cc676–9。
> 公司称公方欠其 **£2,294,426**（已扣其欠公方的 £1.5m 国库券）。

### S3.2 **公司自述的"自养"判断（对本卡最关键的一句）**
> "as, from the best estimates which can be made, there appears but little reason to expect (without a considerable reduction of the military expences of the petitioners in India) that there should be any sufficient surplus revenue to be remitted for those purposes, the petitioners apprehend that the punctual discharge of the pecuniary obligations of the petitioners ... will depend most essentially upon the trade to be carried on by the petitioners"

读数（**falsifier 的直接证据**）：1813年初公司自己认定——**在不大幅削减印度军费的前提下，印度岁入不会产生足以汇回伦敦的盈余；伦敦端义务的履行"最根本地依赖贸易"**。即：领地财政不能自养其伦敦义务（利息£1.5m＋政治开支£0.91m＋年金£0.24m≈£2.65m/年），只能靠茶叶等商业利润填补。

### S3.3 主权与"无国库负担"的自我宣称
> "the British dominions in India, without a rival, or any enemy, now consist of a very large proportion of the peninsula, besides very extensive provinces in the north of Hindostan, and contain, as it is supposed, above fifty millions of inhabitants, and have been acquired wholly at the expence and risk of the petitioners, without any charge whatever to the British exchequer, for, though land forces belonging to his majesty have been employed ... yet the whole of the expence of such forces of his majesty, whilst they have been employed in such service, as well as for their passage out and home, and in recruiting, has been defrayed"（by the petitioners）

读数：与 S1.2（1812 董事会要求把对法战争划归国库）并置，可见公司在**同一年**用两套论述：对内要钱时说"这是欧洲战争该由国库付"，对外辩护特许权时说"我们从未花过国库一分钱"。这是理解公司反应函数的关键——它的政治资产是"不花国库钱"，它的财政现实是"已花不起"。
