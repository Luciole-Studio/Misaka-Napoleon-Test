## The reigns of Alexander I and Nicholas I

## General survey

Alexander I Alexander I, portrait by Domenico Scotti, 1883.(more)

When Alexander I came to the throne in March 1801, Russia was in a state of hostility with most of Europe, though its armies were not actually fighting; its only ally was its traditional enemy, Turkey. The new emperor quickly made peace with both France and Britain and restored normal relations with Austria.
