

#### IV. Ottoman Empire

The Ottoman Empire had been able to institute a relatively efficient statistical service in the beginning of the 20th century, a quarter century before the end of the empire and had published a very limited amount of data.

- Güran, Tevfik ed., (1997) Osmanli Devleti’nin ilk Istatistik Yilligi 1897 [The first Ottoman Statistical Yearbook of 1897], Ankara. `italic "Osmanli Devleti’nin ilk Istatistik Yilligi 1897 / The first Ottoman Statistical Yearbook of 1897"`

Some of these data have been collected and amended by present day economic historians. The bulk of our information stems from French and English Consular archives and from the provincial almanacs (salname) which were of questionable precision. The original data on Ottoman foreign trade have been collected, amended and corrected by Sevket Pamuk (1987, 1995), using the relevant foreign trade statistics of the major importing countries. `italic "salname"`

Pamuk, Sevket (1987) The Ottoman Empire and European Capitalism, 1820-1913. Trade, Investment and Production, Cambridge. `italic "The Ottoman Empire and European Capitalism, 1820-1913. Trade, Investment and Production"`

Pamuk, Sevket ed., (1995) 19 yüzyilda osmanli diš ticareti [The Ottoman Foreign Trade in the 19th century], Ankara. `italic "19 yüzyilda osmanli diš ticareti"`
