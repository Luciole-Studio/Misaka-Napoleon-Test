our figure. Numbers of stallions were relatively small and it is clear that many mares were
used for work, although they would not have had the same power input as non-breeding
horses. In the Napoleonic period, some 72% of all horses were working in agriculture, some
70% at mid-century but falling to 55% by 1913.19 Overall this is undoubtedly a slight
underestimate of animals used for draught power as oxen were certainly employed, primarily
in the east of the country. There numbers were however small and no sure data exists on the
totals. They have not been included in our data.
