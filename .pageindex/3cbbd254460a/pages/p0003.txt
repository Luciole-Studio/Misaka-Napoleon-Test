Horses
The total number of horses was reported annually during the nineteenth century by county
agricultural societies for each parish and city within its area up until 1916. This information
was not reliably updated and it did not reveal what use the horses were put to. Large local
investigations were undertaken in 1916, not least to gain information of the number of
