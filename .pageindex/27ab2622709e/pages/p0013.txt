### C4｜1811 Whitbread、政府与Baring
- HC Deb 5 June 1811，vol.20，cc445–455，“Vote of Credit”。
- https://api.parliament.uk/historic-hansard/commons/1811/jun/05/vote-of-credit ；`downloads/pages/8f32cf6e5188.md`，本轮实读承重全文。
