After the Isis had been constructed, it was thought advisable to cut her in two, and add an additional port and space to her length ; and also, to take away her poop, forecastle, and quarter-deck, or at least as much of the latter as reached from forward to about a beam afore the mizenmast. This made the Isis a flush two-decker, with a short quarter-deck, or large roundhouse, merely intended as a roof to the captain's apartments, and increased her measurement to 1321 tons.
