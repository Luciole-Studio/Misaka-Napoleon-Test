The year without a summer
J. Luterbacher and C. Pfister
The 1815 eruption of Tambora caused an unusually cold summer in much of Europe in 1816. The extreme
weather led to poor harvests and malnutrition, but also demonstrated the capability of humans to adapt
and help others in worse conditions.
