45.AHN, Estado, Legajo 222G/142. One of a series of documents seized by Spanish patriots from the French consul’s office in Cádiz after the anti-Napoleonic rising and subsequently translated for the Junta Suprema’s benefit. Although written on hearing the news of Josef Bonaparte’s ascension to the Spanish throne, the desire for the suggested reforms would have pre-existed this news. In terms of representing Spanish feelings at the time, the document is authenticated by its comment on the popular hatred Napoleon had aroused in Spain. Unsigned and undated, the report was probably written by the French consular agent, Carriere, in Jerez de la Frontera.
