*Lacking military force, the junta proposed a legally accepted mode of achieving its ends. The fathers and mothers of all those who had refused to report were to be arrested until their sons turned themselves in. Meanwhile, a party of trustworthy locals would try to round up the deserters in the countryside, being paid by the head for each one taken.
