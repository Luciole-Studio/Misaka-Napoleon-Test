Far exceeding an agent’s normal duties, Rachel Calder saw this book through to publication for which I am truly grateful.

It has become almost an authorial platitude to thank wife and family for their support during the long years of researching and writing. In this case it is far from platitudinous. My wife, Aurora Bosch, patiently and lovingly supported long absences physical and mental over the past ten years and as a professional historian gave me invaluable advice. My son, Mark, in the antipodes, deprived himself of much needed sleep from his demanding work to read and criticize successive drafts; and my daughter, Jessica, provided me with some invaluable suggestions. To all I owe immense gratitude and love.
