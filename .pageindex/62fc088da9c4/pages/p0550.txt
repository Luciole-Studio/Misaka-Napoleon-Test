The Extraordinary Crimes Tribunal was about to judge an ex-Franciscan priest and companion of Mina when the latter took two French officers, a chief medical officer and five soldiers prisoner in Tafalla. Mina immediately proposed to exchange these for his companion and another guerrilla about to be tried with him. D’Agoult agreed. This ‘serious affair’, as the Bonapartist government termed it, subsequently led to a recommendation that the king order d’Agoult not to permit the recurrence of such events which disturbed public order.
