With the abolition of internal customs and suppression of locally fixed prices and embargoes of recently harvested grain for local supply and storage, the freedom of the agrarian market had become absolute. This decree suffered the same fate as the distribution of the commons, but it was certainly the most drastic socio-economic proposal put forward by the liberals.
