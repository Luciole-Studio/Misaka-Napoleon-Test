In 1812 the poor inhabitants most exposed to the fire were moved to a tented encampment out of range. ‘In all my life I have never known more continuous public gaiety than in Cádiz during the spring and summer of 1812.’ (Alcalá Galiano, 1886/1956, p. 402.)
