*Carlos IV’s main task was to hunt from nine to twelve and from two to five every day, in all kinds of weather, following his father’s example. In the evenings he would ask Godoy, ‘And what have my vassals been doing today?’ As late as 1803 he constantly ‘forgot’ that the British colonies in America had gained independence, although Spain, as France’s ally and enemy of England, had afforded aid to the American revolutionaries.
