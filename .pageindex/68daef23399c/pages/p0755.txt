Our  importations  from  these  islands  have  hitherto  been  insignificant, 
being  confined  to  small  quantities  of  fish  oil  and  timber.  Of  this  latter 
article  the  islands  arc  said  to  contain  a  very  abundant  supply  of  very 
desirable  qualities,  and  in  particular  that  spars  of  considerable  size  may 
be  obtained  for  the  use  of  our  navy.
