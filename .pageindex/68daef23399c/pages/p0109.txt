The  advantage  to  rate-payers  of  the  change  of  system  regarding  the 
rehef  of  the  indigent,  will  be  made  sufficiently  apparent  by  the  above

the  sacrifice  of  our  social  duties,  and  at  the  expense  of  all  the  better 
feelings  of  our  nature.  The  charge  of  heartlessness  has,  in  fact,  been 
so  often  brought  and  urged  against  the  authors  of  the  change,  that  it 
may  be  well  to  inquire,  however  briefly,  whether  that  charge  be  well 
founded  or  otherwise.
