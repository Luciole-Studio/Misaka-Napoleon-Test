for  by  the  fact,  that  no  shipments  took  place  from  this  country  to  America 
in  the  years  immediately  preceding,  during  which  time  we  were  at  war 
with  the  United  States.  The  exportation  of  1815,  the  first  year  of 
renewed  intercourse,  has,  however,  been  greatly  exceeded  of  late,  giving

Birmingham,  is  one  of  those  branches  of  industry,  the  progi-ess  of  which

The  imposition  of  this  duty  has  no  doubt  given  encouragement  to  the
