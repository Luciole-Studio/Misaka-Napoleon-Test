PROGRESS  OF  THE  NATION. 


[sec.    Yll. 


In  the  early  part  of  last  century  the  rates  of  })ostage  in  Scotland  were 
in  a  slight  degree  below  those  charged  in  England,  but  they  have  long 
since  been  equalized  ;  and  from  1812  an  addition  of  a  halfpenny  was 
charged  upon  each  letter,  whether  single  or  double,  or  heavier,  passing 
between  England  and  Scotland.
