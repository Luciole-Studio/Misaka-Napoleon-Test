1825,  the  commercial  intercourse  between  Great  Britain  and  Ireland 
having  at  the  end  of  that  year  been  assimilated  by  law  to  the  coasting 
traffic  carried  on  between  the  different  ports  of  England  ;  and  with 
the  exception  of  the  single  article  of  grain  (as  to  which  it  was  considered
