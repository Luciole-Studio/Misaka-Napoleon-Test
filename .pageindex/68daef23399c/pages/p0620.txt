period  was  one  of  war,  while  the  second  has  been  one  of  peace  ;  and  it

ports  of  destination  without  having  to  wait  for  convoys.     The  greater 
economy  of  time  thus  attained  has,  of  late  years,  been  vastly  augmented 
through  the  employment  of  steam-vessels.     Besides  these  circumstances
