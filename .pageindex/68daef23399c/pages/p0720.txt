Among  the  sources  of  Government  revenue  may  be  noticed  "  receipts 
of  Government  printing-office."  This  calls  for  some  explanation.  Up 
to  the  beginning  of  1838,  no  person  was  allowed  to  exercise  the  trade 
of  a  printer,  or  to  use  a  printing-press,  without  the  license  of  the  Govern- 
ment, which  would  grant  no  such  license  except  to  the  Government 
Commissariat  Department,  to  be  used  for  the  public  service  of  that 
department,  and  to  the  Church  Missionary  Society,  the  society  binding 
itself  to  submit  everything  printed  by  it  for  the  approval  of  the  Govern- 
ment, before  publication.  Two  presses  set  up  without- the  license,  one 
in  1810,  the  other  in  1827,  were  taken  possession  of  by  the  Government.
