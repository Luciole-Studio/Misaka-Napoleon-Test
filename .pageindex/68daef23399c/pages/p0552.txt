Similar  effects  had  followed  reductions  in  the  duty  at  former  periods. 
In  1746  a  reduction  equal  to  about  2s.  per  pound  caused  an  increase  in 
the  quantity  to  more  than  three  times  that  on  which  duty  had  been  paid 
in  1745.  In  1768  an  abatement  of  1.9.  per  pound  on  black  tea  caused

1773  the  shilling  duty  was  reimposed,  the  consumption  fell  back  to  its 
former  scale.
