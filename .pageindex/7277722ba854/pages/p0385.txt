Total of force was: Infantry, 4,533; Cavalry, 206; Artillery, 362;
Engineers, &c., 96 = Grand Total, 5,217.

N.B.--Of these troops on the field, Dilkes’s brigade _minus_ the
211 Coldstreamers, but _plus_ 260 of the 2/67th and Browne’s flank
battalion, formed the right column, while Wheatley’s brigade, _minus_
one wing of the 2/67th, but _plus_ the two Coldstream companies and
Barnard’s flank battalion, was on the left. All the guns were with the
latter column. Thus Dilkes’s command must have been about 1,950 strong,
Wheatley’s about 2,883. The cavalry were absent with Whittingham on the
coast-road till nearly the end of the engagement.
