  N.B.--Note that of all the 594 casualties in action during the period of
  Masséna’s retreat all but 65 were in the Light and 3rd Divisions. All
  the officers killed or wounded belonged to those divisions, except two in
  the 4th and 6th Portuguese Caçadores wounded at Redinha.
