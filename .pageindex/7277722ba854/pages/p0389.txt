

N.B.--The disproportionate number of wounded to killed among rank and
file, 464 to 55, or one to eight, while the normal proportion was about
one to five, suggests that some of the numerous ‘missing’ were really
killed. Note the excessive loss among officers, 61 to 699 rank and
file, one to eleven instead of the usual one to twenty.

There is some reason to suppose that the figures are incomplete, as
Martinien’s _Liste des officiers tués et blessés_ gives 19 killed and
46 wounded _by name_. We find in these tables the 1st Hussars with an
officer killed and three wounded, and the 70th Ligne with seven killed
and thirteen wounded, &c.
