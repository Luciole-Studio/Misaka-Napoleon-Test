

Places  printed  in  the  Roman  Character  are  those  through  which  Coaches  pas> 
in  the  Prosecution  of  their  Journey  to  some  Town  more  remote  from  the 
Metropolis. 

The  italic  Character  denotes  that  there  are  Coaches  whose  Journeys  terminate 
at  the  Place ;  the  References  to  such  Coaches  are  printed  within  Brackets 
[]:  where  other  Figures  follow  those  included  within  the  Brackets,  they 
relate  to  Coaches  which  pass  through  the  Place. 

m.  following  the  Figure  of  Reference,  signifies  the  Mail  Coach. 

The  Numbers  opposite  the  Name  of  each  Place  correspond  with  those 
annexed  to  the  Coaches  in  the  List  of  Inns  which  immediately  succeeds 
this  Index. 

EXAMPLE. 

Supposing  a  Conveyance  to  Abberford  to  be  wanted — see  in  this  Index  the 
Name  of  Abberford,  and  the  Numbers  opposite,  176.  395,  520; — turn  to 
either  of  these  Numbers  in  the  List  of  Inns,  where  will  be  found  the  Coach 
which  passes  through  that  Place ;  viz.  [176],  Aberdeen  and  Edinburgh  Coach, 
Bull  and  Mouth  Inn  ;  &c.  &c.
