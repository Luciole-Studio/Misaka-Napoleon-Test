here  it  is  joined  by  the  Trent  and 
Mersey  Canal,  and  proceeds  to 
Lower  Runcorn,  wheie  it  falls  84 
Feet  to  the  Mersey  by  10  Locks.  I 
Its  Line  to  Worseley  Mill  and  Leigh

The  Sankey  Navigation  is  from  the  River 
Mersey,  a  little  South  of  Sankey 
Bridges,  where  it  locks  into  that 
River  ;  in  its  Course  from  thence  it 
passes  near  Little  Sankey,  and  by 
the  Side  of  the  Sankey  River,  which 
is  used  as  a  Feeder  to  the  Canal,  and 
terminates  at  St.  Hellen.  This  Canal 
was  the  first  executed  in  theKingdom.
