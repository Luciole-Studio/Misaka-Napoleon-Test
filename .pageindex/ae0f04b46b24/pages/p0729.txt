Malls  are  made  up  in  London  the  First  Wednesday  of  every  Month  for 
Halifax,  Quebec,  and  New  York.  The  Packet  sails  from  Falmouth  soon  after 
the  Arrival  of  the  Mails  on  the  Saturday  Evening,  if  it  has  sailing  Orders  from 
the  Secretary  of  State  ;  and  goes  and  returns  by  the  Way  of  Halifax,  to  and 
ftom  New  York,  every  Month  ;  except  November,  December,  January,  and 
February,  during  which  Months  it  goes  and  returns  to  New  York  direct. 

At  Faxmouth,  for  Lisbon. 

The  Mail  for  Lisbon  is  made  up  every  Tuesday  Night,  and  sent  from  Fal- 
mouth generally  on  the  Saturday  Morning,  if  a  Packet  be  in  the  Harbour. 

At  Falmouth,  for  Madeira  and  the  Brazils. 
The  Mail  is  made  up   the  First  Tuesday  in  every  Month,  and  sails  on  the 
Saturday  following. 

At  Falmouth,  for  Gibraltar  and  the  Mediterranean. 
The  Mail  is  made  up  every  Third  Tuesday,  and  sails  on  the  Saturday  fol- 
lowing. 

At  Dover,  for  Calais. 
The  Mail  for  Calais  is  made  up  at  the  General  Po<t  Office  every  Tuesday, 
"Wednesday,    Thursday,    and   Friday  Night,    at   Twelve   o'Clock,    and  sails 
immediately  on  the  Arrival  of  the  Post. — Distance  Eight  Leagues. 
At  Harwich,  for  Holland,  &c. 
The  Mails  for  Holland,  Gottenburgh,  and  Hamburgh,  are  made  up  at  the 
General  Post  Office  every  Tuesday  and  Friday  Night,  at  Twelve  o'Clock,  and 
sails  immediately  on  the  Arrival  of  the  Post. 

At  Weymouth,  for  Guernsey  and  Jersey. 
The  Mail  is  made  up  every  Tuesday  and  Friday  Night  in  London.     Sails  on 
Wednesday  and  Saturday  from  Weymouth  about  Four  or  Five  o'Clock  in  the 
Afternoon;    Wind   and    Weather   permitting.      Returns    to  Weymouth   the 

Thursday  or  Friday  following. — Distance  35  Leagues. 

At  Whitehaven,  for  the  Isle  of  Man. 
The  Packet  Boat  carrying  the  Letters  to  and  from  the  Isle  of  Man,  sails  from 
Whitehaven  once  a  Week,  every  Tuesday,  and  returns  as  often.
