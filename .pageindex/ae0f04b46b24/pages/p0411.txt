Bridgenorth  is  a  Place  of  great  Trade  in 
the  Manufacture  of  Cloth,  Stockings, 
Iron  Tools,  &c.  The  Hill  on  which 
the  upper  Part  of  the  Town  stands 
rises  60  Yards  above  the  Bed  of  the 
River;  many  of  the  Houses  are 
founded  upon  the  Rock,  and  most 
of  the  Cellars  are  Caves  hewn  out  of 
it:  on  the  Proofs  of  the  Caves  are 
Gardens  with  Footpaths  over  them, 
so  that  Persons  may  walk  over  the 
Tops  of  several  of  the  Houses  with 
out  Danger ;  and  there  is  a  curious 
Walk  made  from  the  high  Part  of 
the  Town  to  the  Bridge,  being  a 
hollow  Way  hewn  20  Feet  through 
the  Depth  of  the  Rock.  The  Town 
is  governed  by  2  Bailiffs,  24  Alder 
men,  48  Common  Councilmen,  a 
Recorder,  Town  Clerk,  &c.
