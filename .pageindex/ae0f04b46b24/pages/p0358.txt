Worcester  is  situated  upon  the  River  Se 
vern,  and  is  much  famed  for  its  fine 
Manufacture  of  China,  and  its  an- 
cient Cathedral,  which  is  410  Feet 
in  Length,  and  78  in  Breadth,  and 
the  Tower  196  Feet  high.  The 
Nave  is  extremely  beautiful,  both 
in  Style  and  Proportions.  There 
are  several  handsome  Monuments, 
but  the  chief  is  that  of  King  John, 
standing  in  the  Midst  of  the  Choir. 
His  Majesty,  in  1789,  attended  the 
Music  Meeting,  in  Commemoration 
of  which  was  rebuilt  the  Window  in 
the  west  Front.
