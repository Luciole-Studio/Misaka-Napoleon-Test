Neivarb :  this  Town  was  incorporated 
by  Charies  II.  in  Gratitude  for  its 
Loyalty  to  his  Father,  who  granted 
it  the  Privilege  of  sending  Members 
to  Parliament.  It  is  governed  by  a 
Mayor  and  12  Aldermen.  The  Town 
Hall  is  a  magnificent  Stone  Building. 
The  Church,  esteemed  one  of  the 
finest  in  the  Kingdom,  was  erected 
by  Henry  IV.  Of  its  ancient  Castle, 
built  in  the  Reign  of  King  Stephen, 
there  now  remain  only  some  of  the 
Walls,  of  great  Thickness  and  Height. 
The  Town  is  neat,  and  many  of  the 
Buildings  are  new  ;  and  the  Market- 
place, though  not  spacious,  is  ex- 
tremely handsome.  In  consequence 
of  the  many  Inundations  from  the 
River,  which  often  rendered  the 
Town  impassable,  a  new  Turnpike 
Road  was  made  about  the  Year  1 770, 
and  raised  so  high  upon  Arches,  as 
to  be  passed  with  Safety  in  the  great- 
est Floods  :  it  was  constructed  under 
the  Direction  of  Mr.  Smeaton  ;  and 
near  the  Town  there  is  a  Bridge,  for 
the  same  Purpose,  made  mostly  upon 
dry  Land.
