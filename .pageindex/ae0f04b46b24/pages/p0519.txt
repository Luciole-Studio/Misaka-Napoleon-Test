Durham:  this  City  is  supposed  to  have 
been  first  built  in  995  ;  it  is  nearly 
surrounded  by  the  River  Wear,  and 
is  about  a  Mile  square.  The  Cathe- 
dral and  Castle  stand  upon  an  Emi- 
nece,  inclosed  by  the  Remains  of 
the  old  City  Walls.  The  Situation 
of  the  Cathedral  is  extremely  grand, 
the  Building  stretching  along  the 
Crown  of  an  Eminence  80  Feet  per- 
pendicular from  the  Surface  of  the 
River  which  washes  its  Base  :  before 
it  is  a  large  open  Area,  called  the 
Palace  Green,' on  the  north  Side  of 
which  stands  the  Castle,  first  erected 
by  William  the  Conqueror ;  it  for- 
merly contained  four  Tiers  of  Apart- 
ments, but  nothing  now  remains 
except  the  Mount  Vaults,  and  Part 
of  the  Shell.  In  a  deep  Vale  near 
the  River  are  the  Remains  of  Finch- 
all  Abbey,  founded  in  1196  for  Be- 
nedictine Monks.  At  Durham  is  the 
Palace  of  the  Bishop  of  the  Diocese.

Newcastle:  this  Place  has  long  been 
noted  for  its  Glass  Works,  particularly 
in  the  finer  Sort ;  and  in  the  Coal 
Trade  it  is  the  first  Port  in  the 
World,  and  has  sometimes  exported 
to  foreign  Parts  and  coastwise,  up- 
wards of  600,060  Chaldrons  in  a 
Year.  It  contains  4  Churches  ;  and 
that  of  St.  Nicholas,  the  Mother 
Church,  is  a  most  curious  Fabric 
erected  by  David  King  of  Scotland. 
The  Bridge  over  the  Tyne,  built 
about  1 775,  consists  of  9  large  elliptic 
Arches,  and  supports  a  Street  of 
Houses.  It  is  governed  by  a  Mayor 
Sheriff',  and  12  Aldermen,  and  has 
sent  Members  to  Parliament  ever 
since  the  Reign  of  Edward  I.
