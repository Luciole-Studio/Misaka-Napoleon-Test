Finally, the natality rate moved more slowly and began to drop in the 1740s, much earlier than anywhere else in Europe. This decline accelerated from the 1790s onwards. This ultimately accounted for the progressive slackening of the growth of the French population from 1840 onwards. All in all, the analysis of the long-term evolution of the French population suggests that warfare had a sizeable short-term impact but a much smaller effect on the long term.
