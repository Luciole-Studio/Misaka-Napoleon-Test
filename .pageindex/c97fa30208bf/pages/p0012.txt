On the eve of the French Revolution, industrial products accounted for some 53% of French domestic exports (TOFLIT18 data). This percentage was consistently lower than the ratio for Great Britain, which has led some historians to conclude that the French trade did little to foster industrialisation (Meignen 1973, 583-614; Léon 1974, 407-432). This is confirmed by the dependency rate of most French industries on external markets, which can be computed by using the value-added estimates from the ISMEA team (see Figure 3.6). This rate was relatively small and

declined further during the wars, which reinforces the argument about the rising importance of internal demand.
