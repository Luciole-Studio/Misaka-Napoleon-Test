# M1来源核读摘录4：Antipa–Chamley 2017伦敦—汉堡汇率

Pamfili M. Antipa & Christophe Chamley, *Monetary and Fiscal Policy in England during the French Wars (1793–1821)*, Banque de France Working Paper no.627 (2017) 的作者网站修订本，封面明确 October 2017，作者页面 https://people.bu.edu/chamley/Ec365-17/UKFR.pdf；全文 `downloads/M1_Antipa_Chamley2017_UKFR.pdf`（doc 21ef3d917a4a）。正文PDF/印pp.35–36明确英镑兑换汉堡 Flemish banco shillings，**2.5个月远期票据**（非即期汇率），‘the only series available for the entire period of the French Wars’;至1808年底约34 schillings/£，随后渐贬，伴伊比利亚战费与补贴财政压力；Figure 11 p36（影像已读）视觉上1809后低谷约1813的24–28 shillings/£，再回升。但 **34 为作者正文近似、1813 24–28为本卡从图视觉约读，绝不能作为逐月原始报价**。图像源‘Boyer-Xambeu et al., 1994, authors’ calculation’，参考文献p41为Marie-Thérèse Boyer-Xambeu, Lucien Gillard, Ghislain Deleplace, ‘Régimes monétaires, points d’or et serpent bimétallique de 1770 à 1870’, *Revue économique* 45(5):1139–74（1994）。这不是作者自行采集的另一条独立汇率观察，与可能Boyer-Xambeu源需去重。

重要原句（p35）：‘The price of bills also incorporated contemporaries’ expectations, as we consider the 2.5 months usance rate on Hamburg–the only series available for the entire period of the French Wars. As shown in Figure 11, the exchange rate fluctuated around 34 schillings (Flemish banco) per pound until the end of 1808.’ p36：‘The pound’s internal devaluation was further exacerbated by the advent of unfavorable war-related news, as these made British victory seem less probable and devaluation–rather than resumption at the pre-war parity–more likely.’ 这说明大陆胜利并非完全未定价，只是不同资产收益方向和幅度各异；长期纸镑贬值亦含黄金价格、金银比、贸易、财政预期、战争开支，不能读成英国政治被征服概率。

本源未提供可机读逐周报价，且英国纸币黄金agio不可错称‘汉堡agio’。对敦巴黎/阿姆斯特丹仍无实读直接报价。
