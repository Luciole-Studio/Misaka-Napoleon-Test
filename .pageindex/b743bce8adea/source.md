## B. 补给与海上活动：p.108
> Left by Sir John Duckworth to pursue his course, the French admiral reached, without further molestation, the latitude of the Cape of Good Hope. Here he captured an English merchant-vessel, but not "une corvette anglaise," for none was lost, and from the former learnt, to his regret, into whose hands the Cape had recently fallen. In this port he was to have refitted, preparatory to a cruise off the bank of Anguillas, where he had hoped to have intercepted the China fleet. Thus disappointed, M. Willaumez contented himself with cruising between the continents of Africa and South America, until the want of provisions, in the beginning of April, sent him to the port of St. Salvadore. After a stay here of 16 days, the French squadron weighed and set sail for Cayenne. There M. Willaumez separated his squadron into three divisions, and cruised between the last-named port and the ninth degree of south latitude. He, it appears, contemplated the destruction of the shipping in Carlisle bay, Barbadoes ; but declares he was prevented by currents, contrary winds, and bad weather. Perhaps, had M. Willaumez made the attempt, he would have found more formidable obstacles than these.

来源提示：附近注引 *Victoires et Conquêtes*, tome XVII, p.300；最后一句显然是James评议，不是法方报告。

## C. 加勒比海再集结：p.109

## D. 英法相遇的不同叙述：p.110至p.111
James先叙法舰似欲避战，接着给出法方记述的英译：
> Willaumez shortened sail to wait for it, and cleared for action. The English admiral did not think proper to engage, and kept his wind. The position of the two squadrons prevented the French admiral from closing the enemy, who had Tortola and St. Thomas to shelter himself in, and after having in vain offered him battle, he (Willaumez) resumed his route.

James随后叙述（p.110）：
> Deeming it, as he himself admits, no longer safe to remain in the Antilles, and having taken on board his squadron at Martinique a sufficient quantity of provisions to last until October, M. Willaumez steered towards the latitude of the Great Bahama bank.

注：附近法方叙述来自*Victoires et Conquêtes*，不是本次独立读到的原始报告。James随后称其“bounce”并推断命令禁止不占显著优势时交战，不能把该推断改称已核实的命令。

## E. Jérôme离队与搜索：pp.112–113
p.112：
> Doubtless the admiral had received from the emperor the most solemn charge to avoid every risk of placing his headstrong brother in the hands of his enemies. If so, there is less difficulty in accounting for the apparently shy conduct of Rear-admiral Willaumez
> After the squadron had cruised for some days longer upon the Bahama bank, in the listless and unprofitable manner already mentioned, the impatience of the admiral's protege could hold out no longer; and accordingly, on the night of the 31st of July, the Veteran contrived to part company.

p.113：
> Daylight on the 1st of August discovered to the French admiral the absence of the Veteran; and, fully sensible of the danger to which the fugitive prince would be exposed, M. Willaumez cruised in every direction to find him.
> Having returned from his unsuccessful search, M. Willaumez continued to cruise for the Jamaica fleet, until, at the expiration of some days, a neutral assured him that his hopes were at an end.

注：第一段的“Doubtless ... If so”是作者推测皇帝训令；其余是后出的事件叙述，均不冒充日志直接引文。

## F. 风暴、修复、交战：pp.114–117
p.114：
> On the night of the 18th of August, in latitude 22° north, longitude 63° west, a gale or hurricane overtook the squadron, scattering the ships in every direction, and dismasting and damaging the whole of them.
> When the gale subsided, the French admiral found himself entirely alone. Having contrived a substitute for her lost rudder, and erected jury-masts in lieu of those which had been carried away, the Foudroyant steered straight for Havana.
> The cannonade continued until about 1 h. 45 m. p.m. ; when, finding she was not able to cope with her antagonist, the Anson tacked, ceased firing, and made sail,

注：最后一句为9月15日Anson与Foudroyant交战。p.115说明法舰为80炮、英舰为44炮；James反驳英方Brenton称Anson将法舰逐入港的说法，并记西班牙增援舰在Anson停火时出港。此处胜负不能用于同级舰效能比较。

p.116：
> the British experiencing the gale in latitude 21° 25' north, longitude 62° west, the French, as has already been stated, in latitude 22° north, longitude 63° west.
> the Caesar and the other ships of their squadron, which had been separated by the gale,

p.117：
> The Foudroyant, after undergoing a refit at Havana, set sail on her return to France, and arrived in the road of Brest. The Cassard the only remaining ship of the French squadron, as soon as the gale had abated, bent her course towards Europe, and reached in safety the port of Rochefort.

## I. 同时代法国舰员信件的英译转引：pp.407–408
James引文引介：
> the particulars of which we will give in the words of one of her own officers, as extracted from the translated copies of several intercepted letters, with a sight of which we have been favoured.

p.407，Océan军官来信：
> This movement of the Regulus obliged us to cut our north-west cable.
> We were obliged to cut this cable also, hoist the foretopmast-staysail, loose the foresail, and steer so as to avoid the Palles, the bank of rocks on which the Jean-Bart was lost. At 10 we grounded ;
> Our engines played upon and completely wetted the poop : with spars we hove off the fire-ship,
> Two of our line-of-battle ships, the Tonnerre and Patriote, at this time fell on board of us.

p.408，接续同一引文：
> We lost 50 men at least, through their zealous exertions to disengage the fire-ships : they fell into the sea and were drowned ; but our boats saved a number of others.

注：作者未在此处给军官姓名、原信日期、档号；本次只读到James英译转引。50人是信中估计，不是独立核实的伤亡表。其后p.408 James推测Océan因殖民地补给载荷吃水甚深，用“perhaps”估计28或29英尺，不作确定测量值。

## J. 英方指挥争议与事后解释：pp.423–427
p.423，编者脚注（署“Ed.”，不是James正文）：
> Mr. James has asked this question as a landsman, and it is easily answered:
> by daylight, the French ships, if they cut their cables, might have ran without grounding into security ; whereas by night, the marks could not be seen, the danger could not be avoided.

p.424，James正文：
> Being deficient, as he undoubtedly was, in his force of small vessels, the admiral should have been more vigorous and decisive in his attack by the larger vessels.

p.425，James转引Gambier军法审判指控：
> did for a considerable time neglect or delay taking effectual measures for destroying them.

p.426，James转引判决：
> the court is of opinion that the charge has not been proved
> most honourably acquitted

p.427有O'Meara, *Napoleon in Exile*, II, p.292转引：属于圣赫勒拿回溯性谈话。本报告不以其“全舰皆可被俘”等话语作为1809当时能力的已证事实。
