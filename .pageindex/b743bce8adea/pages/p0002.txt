## D. 英法相遇的不同叙述：p.110至p.111
James先叙法舰似欲避战，接着给出法方记述的英译：
> Willaumez shortened sail to wait for it, and cleared for action. The English admiral did not think proper to engage, and kept his wind. The position of the two squadrons prevented the French admiral from closing the enemy, who had Tortola and St. Thomas to shelter himself in, and after having in vain offered him battle, he (Willaumez) resumed his route.

James随后叙述（p.110）：
> Deeming it, as he himself admits, no longer safe to remain in the Antilles, and having taken on board his squadron at Martinique a sufficient quantity of provisions to last until October, M. Willaumez steered towards the latitude of the Great Bahama bank.

注：附近法方叙述来自*Victoires et Conquêtes*，不是本次独立读到的原始报告。James随后称其“bounce”并推断命令禁止不占显著优势时交战，不能把该推断改称已核实的命令。

## E. Jérôme离队与搜索：pp.112–113
p.112：
> Doubtless the admiral had received from the emperor the most solemn charge to avoid every risk of placing his headstrong brother in the hands of his enemies. If so, there is less difficulty in accounting for the apparently shy conduct of Rear-admiral Willaumez
> After the squadron had cruised for some days longer upon the Bahama bank, in the listless and unprofitable manner already mentioned, the impatience of the admiral's protege could hold out no longer; and accordingly, on the night of the 31st of July, the Veteran contrived to part company.

p.113：
> Daylight on the 1st of August discovered to the French admiral the absence of the Veteran; and, fully sensible of the danger to which the fugitive prince would be exposed, M. Willaumez cruised in every direction to find him.
> Having returned from his unsuccessful search, M. Willaumez continued to cruise for the Jamaica fleet, until, at the expiration of some days, a neutral assured him that his hopes were at an end.

注：第一段的“Doubtless ... If so”是作者推测皇帝训令；其余是后出的事件叙述，均不冒充日志直接引文。

> When the gale subsided, the French admiral found himself entirely alone. Having contrived a substitute for her lost rudder, and erected jury-masts in lieu of those which had been carried away, the Foudroyant steered straight for Havana.
