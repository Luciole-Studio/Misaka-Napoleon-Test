p.400记Bergeret致海军部长信促使Willaumez被召回，3月16日降旗，17日Allemand升旗；并记Allemand调整原有三列锚位。

p.402：
> he directed the armed launches and boats of the fleet, 73 in number, to be assembled in five divisions, in order to be ready, at the close of day, to take their stations near the boom, for the purpose of boarding and towing away the fire-ships,
> Some very excellent regulations were drawn up for the guidance of these boats, as appears by a copy of them which afterwards fell into the hands of the British.

p.404：
> The wind, although in its direction as favourable as it could blow for the progress of the fire-ships, the whole of which had dropped to an anchorage about a mile nearer than the British fleet, was too violent to admit one part of the plan to be carried into effect—that of chaining the vessels together in divisions of four.

p.405：
> although the fourth and fifth divisions of the French boats had been ordered to the boom, there to wait until 2 a.m., nearly the whole of them, owing to the strength of the wind and tide, were obliged to put back.

## I. 同时代法国舰员信件的英译转引：pp.407–408
James引文引介：
> the particulars of which we will give in the words of one of her own officers, as extracted from the translated copies of several intercepted letters, with a sight of which we have been favoured.

p.407，Océan军官来信：
> This movement of the Regulus obliged us to cut our north-west cable.
> We were obliged to cut this cable also, hoist the foretopmast-staysail, loose the foresail, and steer so as to avoid the Palles, the bank of rocks on which the Jean-Bart was lost. At 10 we grounded ;
> Our engines played upon and completely wetted the poop : with spars we hove off the fire-ship,
> Two of our line-of-battle ships, the Tonnerre and Patriote, at this time fell on board of us.

p.408，接续同一引文：
> We lost 50 men at least, through their zealous exertions to disengage the fire-ships : they fell into the sea and were drowned ; but our boats saved a number of others.
