   The main fact remains that the Alsatian wine trade pros-
pered in spite of all those difficulties. According to a contem-
porary source,*® the Bas-Rhin’s harvest in 1808 totalled
546,560 hl, of which the whites of Wolxheim, Molsheim, and
Heiligenstein were especially good, as were the reds of
Marlenheim, Blaesheim and Neuwiller. Prices for the pro-
ducers were above average, and the harvest was fully bought
up. Indeed, with the exception of only a few years, the entire
run of Alsatian harvests from 1801 to 1818 was satisfactory,
and it contrasted well with the cycles before and after it.*?
