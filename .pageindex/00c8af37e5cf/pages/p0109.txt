   What is not in doubt is that the total output of the Haut-
Rhin’s weavers was well below the normal requirements of its
indienneurs. Desportes’s table no. 4 of 29 April 1806 gives
the annual production of the department’s twenty-two
largest calico-printing firms as 247,762 articles.!*? In other
