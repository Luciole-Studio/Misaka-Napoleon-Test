French market design was kept up well beyond the formative
years 1806 and 1807. Like Perier’s mission, the others were
meant to establish useful facts about the Empire’s inter-
national competitivity and offer practical advice on ways of
improving it. How two Rhenish departments fared under the
economic regime of 1807-13, and how far their fluctuations
were linked with the Blockade and the French market design,
are questions which the remaining chapters attempt to
answer.
