278                            APPENDIX B

Observations on Appendix B

All figures are reproduced without correction from the Chamber’s
minutes. The secretary’s arithmetical mistakes.unfortunately have com-
pound effects, since he adds across as well as down the various columns:
viz.
(1) The total for the regular down-river traffic of the Mayengais in
      1806   (column
                   8) adds      up to 23,262,    not   23,242,    so that the
      general down-river total in that column as well as the general total
      for that year in column 16 need to be corrected accordingly.
(2) The total for the regular down-river traffic of the Mayengais to
      Mainz (column 6) adds up to 59,432,-not 59,412, and the respec-
      tive general totals in columns 9 and 16 require due correction.
(3) The total for the regular up-river traffic of the Strasbourgeois from
      Mainz in 1807 should be 22,227, not 32,227 (column 2), if the
      figure of 32,431 in column 4 for that year is correct. Alternatively,
      the latter adds up on the existing entries to 42,431. Either way,
      corrections would be needed in the composite up-river total in
      column 2 (? 194,151, not 204,151) or to that in column 4
      (? 343,365,   not 333,365).   Either of these changes would        also
      affect the respective general totals in columns 9 and 16.
(4) The total for the regular up-river traffic of the Mayengais in 1807
   _ (column 8) adds up to 14,321, not 14,311, implying the same
    minor correction in the composite totals downwards and across.
(5) The total for the regular up-river traffic of the Mayencais from
    Frankfurt (column 5) adds up to 35,503, not 35,493. The com-
    posite totals in columns 8 and 9 require correction accordingly, as
    does the relevant general total in column 16.
(6) The figures in the first three columns of the bottom line add across
      to 1,683,632, not 1,673,632, although the latter is correct if the
      up-river and down-river totals are added down the line in column
      4. The general totals in that line under columns 9 and 16 disguise
      the same discrepancy.
