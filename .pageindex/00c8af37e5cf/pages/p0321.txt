8. Stadtarchiv, Frankfurt am Main

From the series entitled ‘Handel und Handelseinrichtungen, Messe und
fremde Fahrmarkte’ (MS inventory by Jung and Ruppersberg, 1919-
20), the following items:
Alte Bezeichnung
Ugb. C 38 Nr. 432: Frankfurt’s rights in Rhenish navigation and peti-
   tions for direct trade with Cologne, 1806-10.
Ugb. C 38 Nr. 435: id., 1805-10; matters affecting the Rhenish octroi,
   1808.
Ugb. C 38 Nr. 452: attempts to reform Frankfurt’s financial services,
   1807-9.
Ugb. C 38 Nr. 45>: foundation of anew chamber of commerce, 1807-9.
Ugb. C 38 Nr. 474: introduction of the French commercial code, 1808.
Ugb. C 38 Nr. 475: problems of the French commercial code discussed
   before the Fiirste-General-Kommission, 1808.
Ugb. H.[andel]      o.[hne]        B.[ezeichnung]     82 (fol. 1-9): lists of current
   prices of goods and bullion in Frankfurt during the Napoleonic
   period.
Ugb. H. o. B. 83: papers on the value of government bonds (1810,
   1817, 1820).
Neue Bezeichnung
S3/A 564: documents (some printed) on the French military occupa-
   tion of and customs purge in Frankfurt (Oct.-Nov. 1810), with the
   official decrees ordering the public burning of British goods.
