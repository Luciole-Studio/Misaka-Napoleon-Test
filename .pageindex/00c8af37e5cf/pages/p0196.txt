iv. Industrial expansion in the Haut-Rhin                               :

The Blockade and the French Continental market design
offered the Haut-Rhin manufacturers similar opportunities
for progress during the expansionary period. Though the
evidence (and especially the statistical evidence) is more
fragmentary than for the Bas-Rhin, it is apparent that the
main industrial enterprises of the other Alsatian department
strengthened between the summer of 1807 and the autumn
of 1810. Explicit signs of this began to appear in official
reports during the course of 1808, in spite of a brief indus-
trial slack during the latter half of that year, as elsewhere‘in
the Empire. With this one exception, industrial output was
on the upward trend at least until the last quarter of 1810,
when the first ominous warnings of a new crisis were given.
   Again the official records have to be approached with
caution.   The reports from Sainte-Marie-aux-Mines,       for
instance, speak of ‘progressive stagnation’ well-into 1808.7
