   Within such limitations, the statistical record for 1809
points to a signal expansion of the carrying trade through
Strasbourg. It is true that the cited report of 5 August
referred to a continuing decline in imports of colonial goods
during the first half of 1809. This is consistent with the
contemporary statistical evidence of Eichhoff, director-
general of the Rhenish octroi, as shown in Table 4.
