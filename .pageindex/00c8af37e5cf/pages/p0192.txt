   The statistics on Strasbourg’s trade during 1810 are not as
full as those for the preceding two years, and, such as they
are, show huge discrepancies. On 2 August, for instance, the
Chamber gave an assessment of 122,015 ‘old quintals’ (i.e.
50 kg units) for down-river and 18,549 for up-river traffic
during the first half of the year.® This is equivalent to
61,007.5 and 9,274.5 metric quintals respectively. If one
