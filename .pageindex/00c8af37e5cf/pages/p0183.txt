In its report of 20 July the Chamber in Strasbourg gave an
equally disappointing account of the local wine trade: the
spring sales had not matched those of the previous year.”
In 1809, it went on, there had been one minor bankruptcy
in the department; during the first half of 1810 alone there
had been five more serious ones.™ ‘En général’, the Chamber
concluded, ‘il y a stagnation dans les affaires’.
   The same refrain was taken up in later reports that year.
