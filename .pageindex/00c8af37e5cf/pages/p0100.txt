And this indeed is my general conclusion on the industrial
condition of the Bas-Rhin at that time, pace the Chamber’s
verbal protests to the contrary.

iv. Industrial recovery in the Haut-Rhin 1800-1806

The evidence of industrial headway in the Haut-Rhin during
the Consulate and early Empire is more convincing still,
although again there is no doubt that the manufacturers had
had their difficulties. The peace of Amiens had been too brief
to allow them any chance of re-establishing links with former
markets overseas. Far more seriously, the hostilities of 1805-7
had affected their inland trade. The imperial decree of 22
February 1806, which forbade whole classes of imported
cotton goods, was a common grievance among them. This
