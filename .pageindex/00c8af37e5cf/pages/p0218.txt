        INLAND ECONOMIC EXPANSION 1807-1810:
                 THE ALSATIAN EXAMPLE
        PART II: THE QUESTION OF CAUSAL LINKS


i. Positive connections

Alsatian prosperity during the expansionary period was
due to the working of the Continental Blockade and the
market design in two direct ways. The first is obvious:
in so far as the commercial and industrial headway depen-
ded on expansion in a protected and growing imperial
home market, it was closely related to the Blockade. Equally,
in so far as the expansion stemmed from a better perfor-
mance    in export markets          and in the ranges of goods in
which Britain had previously competed, Alsace benefited
from the Blockade. To the extent that the competition
of Britain and of the most industrially advanced Continental
rivals of France    was   blunted    or eliminated,      Alsatians   bene-
fited among other imperial exporters from the ‘markets
of replacement’. Where Alsatian economic expansion was
stimulated by the opportunities of the Continental market
design, especially in the Outre-Rhin and in Italy, it was
linked with the Blockade, of which that design was an
integral part.                                               ‘
