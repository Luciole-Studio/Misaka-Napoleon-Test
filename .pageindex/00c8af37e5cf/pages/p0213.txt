v. The beneficiaries of expansion in Alsace

However impressive their achievements of 1807-10 may
have been, the industrial magnates of the Haut-Rhin did
not quite match their commercial counterparts in Strasbourg
for personal wealth or annual trade earnings. Of the. depart-
ment’s      twenty-five          ‘most       distinguished’        manufacturers
and merchants named in the prefect’s list of 22 July 1810,1!”
seventeen specialized mainly in cottons (tozles peintes,
cotonnades, siamoises) and six of them were based in
Mulhouse. Eight of the full list were reported as having
an active export trade, chiefly to Switzerland, Germany,
and Italy. Yet if the various estimates of personal wealth
(fortune) are taken as even an approximate guide, Mulhouse
had only three millionaires: Dollfus-Mieg, Jean Hofer, and
Laurent Weber. Gaspard Dollfus (800,000 francs) and Paul
