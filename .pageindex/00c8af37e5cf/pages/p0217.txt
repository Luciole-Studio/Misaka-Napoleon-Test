The years 1807-10 were marked by relatively high output,
full employment, and some technical advances.!*° Its capital
assets in 1810 were estimated at 900,000 francs, which made
it the leading industrial enterprise of the Bas-Rhin and placed
it in the class of the top half-dozen or so commercial houses
in Strasbourg. In 1809 its turnover of trade was reckoned at
a million francs, compared with 530,000 in 1779. At the
later date its employment level was 988, as against 918 at
the earlier, so that technical improvements explain most of
the increase in production. But there had also been a pro-
nounced reorientation of the firm’s markets. Its exports in
1809    were   put    at   no   more   than   50,000    francs,   whereas
before the Revolution they had been nearer 500,000
annually. In this case, as in many others, the Continental
market design was largely dependent on home demand. The
next chapter looks in more detail at the far from facile links
between the market design and the effects of the Continental
Blockade.
