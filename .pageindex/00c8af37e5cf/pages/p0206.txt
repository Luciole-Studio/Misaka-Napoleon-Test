   The manufacturers of the Haut-Rhin also had important
trade links with towns on the west coast and in the south of
France. Jean-Jacques Hémardinquer’s survey of this network
is a useful short introduction.®? Whether directly or through
associates like Gros, Davillier & Cie of Bordeaux, the Alsatian
houses     concerned        dealt regularly with firms in Saint-Malo,
Niort, La Rochelle, and Rochefort, and with others in
Toulouse, Montpellier, and Beaucaire. For example, the
correspondence of Etienne and F. Charruyer of La Rochelle
between June 1807 and July 1813 shows that they made
sizeable orders of shawls (‘schals’) from Henri Schlumberger
& Koenig of Mulhouse.               They were        also regular clients for
check handkerchiefs (mouchoirs a carreaux ordinaires) from
the Mulhouse firms of Gaspard Dollfus & Cie and Huguenin
Painé. At Rochefort and Niort, the evidence of sales during
