tries’ which had any short-term effect was beet-sugar and that
its earliest advances were registered mainly in imperial Alsace.
Such progress came too late in the period for any obvious
causal link with the Blockade                     to be established, and as in the
case of other ‘new industries’ there were autonomous and un-
related reasons for entrepreneurial endeavour. Nevertheless,
for what it was worth, Montalivet’s Exposé of 1813 placed
the Bas-Rhin first among the imperial departments in the pro-
