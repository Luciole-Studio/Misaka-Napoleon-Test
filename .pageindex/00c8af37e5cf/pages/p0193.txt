    Further examples of this are easily found. In a retrospec-
tive report of 10 November 1812 to the mayor Bracken-
hoffer, the Chamber gave monthly trade figures for the whole
period from January 1809 to June 1812.®* These, which have
the merit of close detail and of being actual rather than in-
ferred estimates, show that during 1810 the Strasbourg
barges carried a total of 164,008 metric quintals down river
and 11,935 up river. The totals carried by the Mainz barges
that year were respectively 43,042 and 16,856 metric
quintals. If the figures cited in Table 6 are again taken as
the basis of comparison, by that reckoning 1810 was a better
year for Strasbourg’s river trade than any before 1809, and
indeed it falls not far short of this, the annus muirabilts.
