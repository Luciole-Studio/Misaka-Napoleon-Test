6. Archives Communales/de la Ville de Mulhouse
Sub-series FU: liasses nos. Cb 1 (industrial inquiry and statistics, 1808),
  _Cb 2 (id., 1811), Cb 6-7 (foundation of new industrial enterprises,
   1806-9), Cc 1 (brewing, 1769-1800).
Sub-series FHI: Jiasses nos. Ab 1 (grain harvest, Year XIII), Ad 1 (statis-
   tics on viticulture in the canton of Mulhouse, 1810-30).
Sub-series FIV: liasse lettered Aa (regulations on the fairs and markets
   of Mulhouse, Year VI-1818).
Sub-series FVUI; two dossiers of industrial and other statistics, along
   with ‘moral’ reports, mostly for post-Napoleonic period.
Sub-series HU: liasse lettered A (military administration during the

      1806).
