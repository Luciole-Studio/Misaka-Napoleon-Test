   The period of sustained growth in the Alsatian wine
industry and trade owed much to, and was itself an on-going
cause of, the increasing area brought under viticulture.*
The large imperial military market was another encourage-
ment. But perhaps the most favourable factor of all was the
growth of the home market through Rhenish annexations,
matched by the advantageous conditions for export to neigh-
bouring lands. Together, these made up for the rupture of
wine sales to Britain and America. Most of the imperial
export trade in wines and eaux-de-vie                          to the Outre-Rhin
