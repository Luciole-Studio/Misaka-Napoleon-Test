   One sign of better industrial heart in the Haut-Rhin
after Brumaire is the founding of new enterprises. According
to L’Huillier, of the 22 major firms specializing in printed
calicoes, 4 had been established between                 1799 and 1804, and
2 more were in the process of being launched at the latter
date.!8° According to a prefectoral list of 22 July 1810,
which names the 25 ‘most distinguished’ industrial and mer-
chant houses in the Haut-Rhin, 9 had been founded between
1795 and 1805.13! More significantly, of these nine, Daniel
