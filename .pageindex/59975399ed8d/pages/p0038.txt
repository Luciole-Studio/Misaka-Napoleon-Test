   In early February the disagreement between Craig and Elliot was brought
to an end by the steady advance of the French army, and the “existing
circumstances” mentioned by Craig were a reference to the king’s decision to
leave Naples and take refuge in Sicily. Napoleon had never had the least
intention of yielding to the requests of the queen. “The dynasty of Naples has
ceased to reign,” he had publicly pronounced at Pressburg, and his brother
Joseph was to seize the kingdom. On 9 February the king sailed for Palermo,
followed shortly by the queen and her entourage, and once in Sicily, on
Acton’s prompting, he visited Messina and allowed Craig to land.®? In the
third week in February British troops took over Messina. They were not to
leave it for another nine years.
     Julian Corbett describes these events as “the last stroke of the Trafalgar
campaign,” by which the British were able to avert the full consequences of
Ulm and Austerlitz.°? And there is no doubt that if the British had not been
able to command the straits from their firm base in Messina citadel, having
first removed all boats from the mainland as well as the coastal artillery, the
French, pursuing the routed elements of the Neapolitan army of Damas,
could have crossed with ease into Sicily and so gone on to threaten Malta and
the whole command of the Mediterranean.
