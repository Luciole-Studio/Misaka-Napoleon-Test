  18. Lynedoch MS, 3598 ff, 193-94, National Library of Scotland, Edinburgh.
The statement Nelson made to Hamilton, in a letter of 13 October, that Dundas had
earlier authorized the posting of Graham and his force to Valletta, but that he
(Nelson) had kept back the order, is, on the evidence, completely untrue. One can
only speculate on the reasons why Nelson told a deliberate falsehood. Presumably it
was for the consumption of the court, who might have resented losing the troops.
