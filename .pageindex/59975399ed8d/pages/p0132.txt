   Accordingly, a mixed force (half of it Sicilian) set sail from Sicily in the
middle of May under the command of General Macfarlane. When it arrived
in the Bay of Naples it found its presence largely superfluous, as an Austrian
army occupied the town and the British fleet lay anchored in the bay.?® A
fortnight later the king himself arrived back in his capital, having left behind
the hereditary prince to act as lieutenant general of the island. The death of
the queen the previous September had made his return considerably easier,
since memories of her vindictiveness in 1799 were still very much alive.
Needless to say, the king had not sought permission from parliament to leave
Sicily, as by the terms of the constitution he was in strictness bound to do.
The last chapter of Britain’s occupation and protection of Sicily was about to
begin.
