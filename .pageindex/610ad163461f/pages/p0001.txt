## 1. Popham 1803-11-26〈Secret Paper on South America〉（印 pp.288–293；行 13535–13760）
- 【事实·亲读】目标口径："any permanent influence of Great Britain, whether fixed by **absolute conquest, or conquest in the first instance, followed up by successful political intrigue**, (which I contend is authorized by all usages of war)"（印 pp.289–290）→ **1803 年英国海军将领层的方案确实是"先征服后政治操作"**，这是 falsifier 的正面证据，须与 1807-05 内阁备忘录并置：**1803（个人/军方层）征服在先 → 1807（内阁层）征服被判 hopeless**。
- 【事实·亲读】经济动机数量化："the annual export from that country to Spain… are **fifty millions of dollars a year** (the last Return from Cadiz made in 65)"（印 p.289）。
- 【事实·亲读】战略链："eventually annihilating the navy of Spain, by cutting off its greatest nursery for seamen and its principal sources for foreign timber; for if Spain is once confined to its European navigation and commerce, she will not be long considered a maritime power."（印 p.290）→ 与 P4/F3 海军差距卡的接口：**西属美洲是西班牙海军的人力与木材上游**。
- 【事实·亲读】太平洋据点清单："Baldivia, Valparaiso, Lima, Panama, and Acapulco"；拉普拉塔的理由包含**反法**："its presenting the most probable point for the enemy—I mean, **France**—not only to take possession of, but to furnish supplies to counteract all our operations, and this certainly with great facility, **if he once had possession of the Brazils**"（印 p.290）。
- 【事实·亲读】米兰达的自估兵力："calculated on being able to collect an army of **15 to 20,000 men**" 起于加拉加斯，沿圣菲（新格拉纳达）与基多扩张，终点是巴拿马地峡常驻据点，并与印度方面的远征通联（印 p.291）。
- 【事实·亲读】社会分类（英方情报口径）："The inhabitants may be considered under four heads: the positive Spaniard, the Creolian Spaniard, the Mulattoes, and the Indians. The Mulattoes are much connected with the Creolian Spaniards, who have ever been notoriously the most dissatisfied race in that country with the existing Government, even as much so as the Indians, who are its natural enemy."（印 p.291）
  【解读·须警惕】此为英方一厢情愿的社会学：把 mulattoes 与克里奥尔捆绑、把印第安人说成政府天敌。1812–14 委内瑞拉 llanero 与 pardo 大规模站到保王派一边（见 notes_venezuela）恰好证伪该假设；**红队可据此抽核"英国情报的社会误判"这一条**。
- 【事实·亲读】所需兵力极小："The General asks for **1,500 men**… issue letters of service for a **Mexican legion**, and you will have 2,000 recruits in a few weeks."；海军可由商人 Davison 自费提供 3–4 艘印度商船（1 艘 50 炮、3 艘 36 炮），条件是政府为其承保失败/被俘损失（印 pp.292–293）。
  → **本卡"英国扶植式干预的最小可行包"锚点：1,500 正规兵 + 私人船队 + 就地募兵。**
- 【事实·亲读】**法国反制条件（本卡最重要的 counterfactual 机制锚点之一）**："if the enemy, either from France or Spain, is able to land a European force on the coast, and by **any immediate sacrifices or concessions to the natives obtain their favour**, the enterprise from this country may have the greatest difficulties to encounter"（印 p.293）。
  → 英方自己承认：**先于英国向当地人让步者得当地人**。这为"法国/西班牙以自治与贸易让步先发制人"提供同期文书依据，而不是后见之明。

## 2. Velasquez（巴拉圭总督/Intendant）1806-08-01〈Private Annotations〉（印 pp.302–309；行 14277–14570）
西班牙官员写给马德里陆军大臣的内部报告，即**敌对方对 1806 年事件的即时叙述**，与英方文书构成独立互证。

- 【事实·亲读】招引英军者是本地人："four greedy merchants and not a few of the citizens, disgusted with the Government of the Marquess de Sobremonte, wrote to the English General, informing him of the defenceless state in which the Viceroy had left the town, and invited him to come"（印 p.302）；英商"who have been here under the name of Americans"提供了社会关系底账（印 p.303）。
- 【事实·亲读】占领极易：1806-06-23 登陆、06-27 入城，"with no more troops than 2,000 men… without loss, and without the least resistance"；城内正规军只有一个掷弹兵连与伤残兵（印 p.303）。
- 【事实·亲读】**翻脸机制**："The English had been invited under the persuasion that they would **declare the independence** of Buenos Ayres; but those who had so calculated were mistaken. Beresford called himself conqueror… by a proclamation declared **the King of Great Britain the sovereign of Buenos Ayres**. After this proclamation, the very same persons who called him hither took the resolution to drive him out ignominiously with their house-brooms."（印 p.303）
- 【事实·亲读】太迟的补救："When Beresford knew that it was difficult to defend the town, **he offered independence**; but, having been answered that it was too late…"（印 p.305）
- 【事实·亲读】**本地政权更替**：上层市民鼓动下层反对总督回城，并推举利涅尔斯掌军权，"declared at a meeting of the Audiencia on the **14th of August, 1806**, two days after conquest"（印 p.305）→ 1806 年 8 月已发生"美洲城市自行罢免总督的军权安排"，比 1810-05 早四年。
- 【事实·亲读】**武装平民**："The same higher class of the citizens, afraid of the re-establishment of the Viceroy by the Court of Madrid, roused Liniers, and… showed him the way of raising troops… it began about the middle of September. The populace, once armed, were told that they had a right to oppose the tyrannical Government which they had endured under former Viceroys… By this the mob was urged into a complete state of revolution."（印 p.306）
- 【事实·亲读】募兵抵制：蒙得维的亚来布宜诺斯艾利斯征兵，"The populace was against this step; and, if the recruiting officers had not hidden themselves, they would have been assassinated. **This is one of the causes of the loss of Monte Video.**"（印 p.306）→ **河东岸/河西岸对立在 1806–07 即已制度化**（BA 拒绝为蒙得维的亚流血）。
- 【事实·亲读】卡比尔多对海军军官的追责、利涅尔斯以私谊庇护 Concha、主教出面销毁文书（印 pp.307–308）；后 Audiencia 与 Cabildo 秘密召 Velasco 取代利涅尔斯、Elío 被任命为 Major-General（印 pp.308–309）→ **1807 年起"Elío 对 利涅尔斯"的对抗轴已成型，1808–10 蒙得维的亚成为保王据点有其 1806 根源。**
