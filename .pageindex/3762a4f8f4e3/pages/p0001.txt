 Acclaim for Swords Around a Throne `A fascinating tale, an excellent book." James L. Stokesbury, Army Magazine "Fresh, evocative, vivid, and dramatic ... this book reads like a grand novel in the old style, with the added charm of one of the world's most enduringly fascinating people at its center. It is a military buff's delight, but also offers large rewards for the general reader of history." -Wall Street Journal "In this thorough and immensely entertaining book ... no aspect of the life of the Grande Armee escapes Elting's attentions......Swords Around a Throne is unlikely to be surpassed." -Washington Post Book World "Written in a lively and ironic style, Elting's book offers a wealth of information and color on this vast undertaking, both new and familiar, which is likely to be of great interest to military historians, wargamers, and Napoleon buffs....A work rich in narration and anecdotal flair." -The Historian "The author is knowledgeable, articulate, and meticulously accurate....His graceful prose makes the intricacies of grand strategy clear, even to the casual reader, but does not neglect the minutiae of the daily grind of military life....Elting's analytical, often wry, style lends needed perspective." -Sea Power "Elting creates a fascinating picture of the people, their motives and their methods, that made up the metabolism of the Grande Armee and gave it its life blood .............................................................................................................................Perceptive and colourful." -London Times `An excellent and unique book....The author approaches a familiar topic from a fresh perspective....Scholarly without being ponderous or presumptuous, this work will be appreciated by scholars, students, buffs, and general readers." choice "The text is superb.
