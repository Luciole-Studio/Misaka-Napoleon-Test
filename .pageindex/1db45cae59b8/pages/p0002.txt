

## 4. 亚历山大 1812-04-01 信（圣彼得堡）[行8760–8930]
- 前信答复希望渺茫故沉默；计划已泄露至德累斯顿与巴黎。
- 与法决裂"seems inevitable"；指拿破仑要求俄国断绝一切中立国贸易、单方面开放法国奢侈品——"inadmissible and incompatible with the honour of Russia"。
- 军事计划：不指望进抵维斯瓦河/华沙，"We must therefore make the provinces the centre of our action"；问：复国宣言在开战即发还是先胜后发？**是否先组织立陶宛大公国并授两部宪法草案之一**（"organise the Grand-Duchy of Lithuania as a preliminary measure and give it one of the two constitutions of which I send you drafts"）。
- 深度防御论："the immense extent of territory which the Russian armies have behind them...Napoleon's difficulties would increase the further he proceeds"；"Once the war begins we are resolved not to lay down our arms."
- 瑞典已订攻守同盟。
- 驳Czartoryski"拿破仑同意俄皇兼波兰王"设想："chimerical"。

## 5. Czartoryski 1812-12 信（战后）[行8930–8966]
- 提议**米哈伊尔大公**（非康斯坦丁）为波兰王："a cause of incessant anxiety and terror to the Poles is the Grand-Duke Constantine, who is your heir-apparent"；"A King of Poland with 300,000 men under his orders would be able at any time to destroy what his predecessor may have established"——**波兰精英对无宪法保障的俄属王国的结构性恐惧，同期表述**。
- 对列强悲观："I have no hope in the Continental Powers"；寄望英国与摄政王。

## 6. 亚历山大 1813-01-13 信（Leypouny/Krasnopol）[行8966–9100]
- 复国意图不变，但坦承障碍：①**"opinion in Russia would be against them. The sacking by the Polish troops of Smolensk and Moscow, and the devastation of the whole country, has revived old hatreds."**；②此刻公布波兰计划会"throw Austria and Prussia entirely into the arms of France"。
- **拒绝米哈伊尔方案**："the idea of my brother Michael cannot be admitted. Do not forget that Lithuania, Podolia, and Volhynia are hitherto regarded as Russian provinces, and that no possible reasoning could persuade Russia to see them under the rule of another sovereign than the one that rules Russia. The name under which they would continue to form part of Russia is a difficulty that would be more easily overcome."——**俄国接受"名义波兰"但绝不接受西部省份脱离俄皇**：S5类比的硬约束。
- 下令俄军不占华沙（条件：无外军尤其无波军驻留）；军事：每步兵团后备1,000人、骑兵团2中队、炮兵预备连，春季入列；**18万新征兵进行中**；民兵全体在Tolstoi指挥下向沃里尼亚集结。
- "I am decided to push on the war...until a general peace is established."

## 7. Czartoryski 1813-04-23/27 华沙信 [行9100–9160]
- Radziwiłł转述普王：不反对波兰存在、认为波兰民族愿望正当，抱怨亚历山大避谈。
- 抗议总部下令**没收在波兰军中服役者地产**："people should not be punished for serving their country and obeying the orders of their sovereign, recognised as such by your Majesty."
- **立陶宛五省军政之弊**："suffering under an administration more unjust and arbitrary than any of those that have preceded it. No one's property, life, or honour is safe...the Government and the authorities...seem to think it their duty to persecute and plunder them."——1813年俄占立陶宛实况的同期波兰方证词（利益相关方，需与俄方文书对照）。

## 对本卡推演的直接价值
- B3复国版的**竞争约束**：俄国自己1811年就开出"德维纳—别列津纳—第聂伯"版波兰，条件是永属俄皇——法国复国版的出价必须超过此线才有吸引力差。
- 亚历山大兵力表（230k vs 155k）与实际编成（106.5k+134k+44k+80k）是1811年俄方**自估**，Czartoryski已质疑账面/实际比（100k纸面=65k实际）。
- 精英分裂结构：Poniatowski军中无可替代；Czartoryski线的沉默失败（他没把方案递交华沙政要=方案死于第一步）；西班牙2万波军人质机制。
- S5硬边界：俄国可让"名义"不让"疆土"；俄国舆论与军队意见是沙皇自己承认的否决者。
