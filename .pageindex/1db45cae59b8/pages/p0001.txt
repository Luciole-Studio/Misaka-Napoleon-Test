## 1. 亚历山大 1810-12-25 信（圣彼得堡）[行8107–8296]
- 背景：Czartoryski以维尔纳大学监督职辞职信（1810-11-H日）触发。
- "It seems to me that the time has arrived to prove to the Poles that Russia is not their enemy...it is not improbable that Russia will be the Power to bring about that event [restoration of Poland]...This has always been my favourite idea"。
- 提出8个问题：公国民情、是否会"seize with avidity any offer giving them the certainty (not probability, but certainty) of their regeneration"、是否接受任何来源的复国、党派分布、军中党派、军中最有影响的军官是谁。
- 战略逻辑："success...is based not on a hope of counterbalancing the genius of Napoleon, but solely on the diminution of his forces through the secession of the Duchy of Warsaw, and the general exasperation of the whole of Germany"。
- **兵力表**（亚历山大附表）：己方100,000俄军＋50,000波军＋50,000普军＋30,000丹军=230,000，可再增100,000俄军；法方60,000法军（德境仅46,000，加荷兰与法国内地）＋30,000萨克森＋30,000巴伐利亚＋20,000符腾堡＋15,000威斯特法伦等=155,000。
- 关键论据："The support on which the Poles can rely is limited to the person of Napoleon, who cannot live for ever."（波兰所恃仅拿破仑一人之寿命）
- "Such a moment presents itself only once; any other combination will only bring about a war to the death between Russia and France, with your country as the battlefield."

## 2. Czartoryski 复信 1811-01（"1%"=OCR，当为1月18/30日）[行8296–8459]
- 公国军民"sole wish and object is the restoration of Poland — the reunion of all its parts into a single national body, under a national and constitutional regime"；无实质党派分裂。
- 但列出转向俄国的障碍：①拿破仑已使波兰人相信非不愿乃力不足，与俄决裂之日即复国之时；②感恩与"brothers in arms"情感；③"the Russians are her bitter enemies——an idea considerably strengthened by the events of the late war [1809]"；④**西班牙2万波军人质**："there are 20,000 Polish troops in Spain, whom their friends and relatives in Poland would fear to sacrifice to the vengeance of Napoleon"；⑤子弟在巴黎就学=人质；⑥拿破仑不败神话。
- 开出四条件："(1) The restoration of the constitution of the 3rd of May 1791...(2) The reunion of the whole of Poland under one sceptre...(3) The re-establishment of outlets for trade, the closing of which has impoverished the country; and (4) A reasonable prospect of success in a war with Napoleon."——**第3条证明大陆封锁对波兰经济损害是精英层公开筹码**。
- 质疑俄军账面："I have so often seen in Russia 100,000 men on paper represented only by 65,000 effectives."
- **军中最有影响者=Poniatowski**："he is undoubtedly Prince Poniatowski, the Commander-in-Chief and Minister of War, whose personal character secures him an influence over his subordinates greater than that of any other chief."

## 3. 亚历山大 1811-01-31 信（圣彼得堡）[行8459–8700]
- 复国之国=俄国；**疆域=旧波兰全部含俄占省份（白俄罗斯除外），以德维纳—别列津纳—第聂伯为界**；官员、当局、军队全为波兰族；宪法未定（要看五三宪法文本），但"a liberal constitution will be offered"。
- **Sine qua non两条**：①"the kingdom of Poland shall for ever be united to Russia, whose Emperor shall in future bear the title of Emperor of Russia and King of Poland"；②公国显要人物签名担保民意一致。
- 俄军编成明细：第一军8步兵师×10,000＋4骑兵师×4,000＋15哥萨克团7,500=106,500；第二军134,000；第三军预备44,000＋已训新兵80,000；摩尔达维亚军可抽师、芬兰格鲁吉亚军与克里米亚军团不动。
- **加利西亚方案：以瓦拉几亚＋摩尔达维亚（至塞列特河）与奥地利交换加利西亚**，且须等奥同意——"The Kingdom of Poland would, therefore, in the first instance be formed of the Duchy of Warsaw and the Russian provinces."
- 萨克森王补偿：只有他来投才给。
- 两种前途分析：波兰随法→战场在波兰腹地，"after such a war, whatever might be its result, Poland would be only a vast desert"；波兰投俄→战场推到奥得河、复国先于战争、贸易复兴、"a public revenue based on the real wants of the country, and not, as now, applied solely for the maintenance of a too large army destined to serve the ambitious plans of Napoleon"（**俄方对公国财政军事化的同期指控**）。
- 承诺条件化："so long as I cannot be sure of the co-operation of the Poles I am decided not to begin a war with France."
- 保密：连首相（Chancellor Rumiantsev）都不知情；警告Czartoryski已被法国警务大臣监视。
- 亚历山大公开否认将称波兰王的传言（"rumours...prove that the plan would be highly approved by the Russians"——注意：此句意为俄人会赞成他兼波兰王，与后文1813年"俄国舆论反对"对照）。
