                           1 Archives Nationales de Paris (ANP)
The Archives Nationales remain, indisputably, the greatest and truly indis-
pensable source for the study of Napoleonic Italy. Their remarkable
character resides in not only the territorial breadth they offer, nor even the
insights into high policy they provide, but the wealth of local detail found in
sub-series F7 and F19. These series, especially, are essential to the study of
Italian local history in this period.
