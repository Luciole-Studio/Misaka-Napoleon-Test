    62   al-Rāf‘ī, ‘ Aṣr Muḥammad ‘Alī , p. 327.
 For the training of Ismail Pasha’s mamluks, see S/1/48/1/195 on 29 C 1239/1 March 1824; and S/1/48/1/215 on 19 B
 1239/20 March 1824. Although all mamluks were technically slaves, there were sharp differences among them. The
 leaders of the households are referred to as “Mamluks,” and it was these who were the target of the massacre of
 1811. Their followers who later joined Mehmed Ali’s service as well as other white slaves who were bought by him
 and by other dignitaries are referred to as “mamluks.”
