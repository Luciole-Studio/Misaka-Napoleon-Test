      The Berlinische Nachrichten was a local newspaper published in Berlin; its influence was limited to a small
area of Prussia and Germany. Moreover, this study only focused on recruiting notices, financial reports, and contri-
bution notices related to the Berliner Landwehr. Nevertheless, surveying articles from a local newspaper indicated
that the Berliner Landwehr was more substantially connected to the local situation than to the principles and ideals
of the Prussian military reforms. Prussian military reforms only created fundamental systems for the Landwehr
from the top down. During the Wars of Liberation, the municipalities and Prussian inhabitants determined whether
reforms were enforced.
