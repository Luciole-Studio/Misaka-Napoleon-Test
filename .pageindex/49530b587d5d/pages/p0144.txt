   By means of these massive inﬂuxes of money, the corsair cam-
paigns promoted the use of cash payments in the Maghreb, ﬁrst in
the corsair cities, and then in the hinterland, thus promoting the
growth of a merchant class and stimulating the development of an
exchange economy on a local and international level. But by increas-
ing the risk of diplomatic and military confrontations, the campaigns
seriously disturbed the very economic relations they fostered, as
is apparent in the war between Tripoli and the United States.
However, a time comes when commercial exchanges can become
more proﬁtable than privateering.
