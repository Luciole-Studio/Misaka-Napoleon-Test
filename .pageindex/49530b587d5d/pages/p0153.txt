Commercial Exchanges
The information we have is very uneven: quite good on relations
with Marseilles; incomplete, though still useful, for the other European
ports; but inadequate when it comes to the Ottoman Empire.

Trade with Europe
Although the facts we can provide here only refer to the ﬁrst six
months of 1788, they are fairly representative of trade between Tunis
and Europe.
