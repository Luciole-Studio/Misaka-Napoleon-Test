of the 1780s and the beginning of the 1790s, the activity of the
Barbary corsairs, assisted by diplomatic and economic developments,
was moving without too many shocks towards a progressive and
doubtless early demise.33 This situation was brought abruptly into
question in 1793, and the years that followed marked a return to
corsair activity in North Africa that was as spectacular as it was
unexpected.
