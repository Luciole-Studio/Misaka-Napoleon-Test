The career of the raïs Hamidou was pretty consistently successful,
with ﬁfteen positive years out of eighteen! Apart from 1798, an excep-
tional year for many corsairs, which provided him with six captures,
the following years usually produced two, with, of course, very uneven
proceeds. In 1803, the lone captured ship earned him only 160 riyâls,
whereas he netted 5,044 riyâls from another one, captured in 1806.
In total, over eighteen years, the raïs Hamidou captured, either by
himself or in the company of other corsairs, 31 ships, which, when
the shares were distributed, provided him with nearly 41,000 riyâls,
around 2,400 a year. Although it is impossible to evaluate today the
real value of his fortune, it is certain that he had one, the superb
villa, the Jenan Ali Raïs, he owned near the city on the road to Sidi
Ferruch bears witness to that.23
