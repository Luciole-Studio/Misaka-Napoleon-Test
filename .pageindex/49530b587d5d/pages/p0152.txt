   • The complete absence of the Ottoman ﬂag: the North Africans
were absent from commercial navigation as much by their commit-
ment to the corsair war as by the fact that their ships were not
allowed in the European ports. Added to this was the corsair activ-
ity of the Christians, notably the Maltese, which led the Ottomans,
for reasons of security, but also of eﬃciency, to make use of Christian
merchant ships practicing what was known at the time as the ‘mar-
itime caravan’, to which we will return later.
