Although the Algiers arsenal was very active, the small size of the
city as well as its layout made it impossible to build a single big
complex. As far as we can glean from the sources, there were at
least three sites: the shipyard reserved for the major constructions
was near Bab el-Oued, as were the Dâr al-barûd, the explosives fac-
tory, and the cannon foundries.
