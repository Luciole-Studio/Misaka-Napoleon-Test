# IN1 制度与政治笔记

## P1. 公司的宪制位置（1808 年请愿自述）
来源：`downloads/pages/9ea19769f461.md`＝HC Deb 26 April 1808 vol 11 cc68–79，公司请愿全文。
> "the petitioners are also in the possession of certain territories in the East Indies yielding a large annual revenue, the immediate government of which territories is exercised under the orders of the Court of Directors of the petitioners; but **the supreme superintendence, direction, and controul of all acts, operations, and concerns which in any way relate to the civil or military government and revenue of the said territories, has been for many years past, and is now vested in the Board of Commissioners appointed by his majesty for the Affairs of India**"
> "the petitioners' concerns are principally of two kinds, one of which regards the civil and military government of the said territory, its political relations, and the Indian debt incurred in respect thereof; the other of which regards the commerce carried on by the petitioners"

读数：公司自述其为**双重法人**：领地政府（受国王任命的控制委员会 Board of Control 最终支配）＋商业公司（自负盈亏）。1813 年请愿（notes_finance S3）补充：涉及战和与对土邦谈判的机密命令经**三人秘密委员会**下达，"without any privity of the petitioners, or of their court of directors, and without any discretionary authority on the part of the members of such secret committee"。
→ **本卡最重要的制度结论**：在战争与外交上，**东印度公司不是一个自主行为体，而是英国内阁的一个前置执行机构＋财务承担者**。推演"公司对法国威胁的反应"时，"公司的偏好"只在**钱、人事与商业特权**三条轴上有独立性；在"是否对法作战"上它只有**议价权而无否决权**。

## P2. 欧洲战争对公司账目的传导（1808 请愿第 3 条）
> "The deterioration occasioned in the affairs of the petitioners by a state of European war since 1793, under the following heads: 1st, **in freight and demurage, which, in the course of 14 years, have created an increase of expence to the petitioners by the sum of 7,000,000l. sterling**; 2d, in the increased cost of the manufactures of this country exported by the petitioners, to the annual amount, on an average of 13 years from 1793–4, of **about 1,690,000l. sterling** [增额]; 3d, in diminution of profits on the Indian investments homeward"
> 又：1803–1806 年运往印度与中国的货物与白银大幅超过回流；1803–04 到货的白银"great part thereof ... was absorbed by the expences of the war then carried on against the Mahrattas"。
> 1797–1807 十年间，本土向印中两地的垫款超过回流 **逾 £5,000,000**。
> 1808-03-01 公司欠国王关税与消费税 **£1,410,238**；1808-03-01→1809-03-01 预计现金缺口 **£2,433,185**。
> 公司为"依政府指令"对法属、荷属、西属岛屿及埃及的远征垫款，要求国库偿还。

读数（量化"欧洲战争税"）：公司自报的战争成本＝运费与滞期 14 年多支 £7m（≈£500,000/年）＋出口英货成本年增 £1.69m＋印度洋远征垫款。**这就是"英国被逐出欧洲"情景的镜像量级**：如果欧洲战争结束或英国退出欧洲战场，这些成本中的运费保险部分会显著下降；但远征垫款与领地债务不会。

## P3. 明托的法国威胁评估（1807，致控制委员会主席）
来源：`downloads/IN1_minto_in_india.txt`＝*Lord Minto in India: Life and Letters of Gilbert Elliot, First Earl of Minto, from 1807 to 1814*（London 1880，archive.org `lordmintoinindia01mint`），行 2480–2540。
> "If a force adapted to the purpose (of invading India) could be collected there [波斯湾口], gradually and in detail, either by sea or land, and the obstacles are not insurmountable either way, the trade of the Arabs and of the Persian Gulf in general is so considerable that tonnage would be found without difficulty to transport any number of troops, and the passage is short and easy to territories occupied by our professed enemies in India."
> "**We must not forget the nature of our tenure in this Empire, and that it would not require a great European army to disturb our security in India—20,000 French troops commanded by French officers, supplied with all the means which attend such armies, would not, I am firmly convinced, subdue us—but would create a struggle which must be attended with great present calamity, however successfully and honourably it might be overcome. For these reasons I should be sorry to see half the number of troops landed in India.**"
> "An absolute ascendancy at the Porte or a positive conquest of Turkey, a confederacy with Russia in Oriental schemes, would tend to the furtherance, I do not say to the accomplishment, of Bonaparte's scheme against India."
> 建议向波斯派使，且使节应"represent His Majesty and receive his instructions from his ministers"，但与印度政府保持密切通信。
