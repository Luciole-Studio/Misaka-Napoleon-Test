rather  than  incur  the  reproach  of  having  abandoned  her, 
and  preferred  indulging  in  repose,  I  shall  gladly  court  a 
glorious  death  in  her  service.  It  is  under  the  inspiration 
of  these  sentiments  that  I  humbly  beseech  England  and 
France  to  adopt,  in  my  regard,  a  determination  consistent 
with  justice,  equity,  and  their  own  interest."
