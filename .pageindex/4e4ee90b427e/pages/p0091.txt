But,  if  Mohammed  Ali  has  not  succeeded 
in  bringing'  his  army  to  a  very  high  state  of 
either  instruction  or  discipline,  he  has,  at  all 
events,  organized  a  body  of  men  fully  equal 
to  cope  with  any  enemy  against  whom  he  is 
for  some  time  likely  to  have  to  contend ; — 
a  force  to  which  the  country  is  indebted  for 
its  release  from  the  thraldom  of  the  Porte  and 
for  its  present  state  of  civilization  ;  the  com- 
munity at  large,  for  the  security  which  it 
affords  to  trade,  science,  and  industry ;  by 
whose  means,  in  fine,  an  order  of  things  has 
been  established,  which,  if  fortunately  followed 
up,  will  make  Egypt  once  more  a  rich  and 
powerful  nation.
