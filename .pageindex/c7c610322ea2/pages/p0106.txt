    D. Rodrigo had employed many erudite Brazilians in the process of de-
cision making. He had encouraged others to undertake state-sponsored
scientific expeditions in Brazil. He had been especially responsive to those
who were connected with the Minas conspiracy. The exiled Jose Alvares
Maciel, for example, forwarded a memorial on the iron mines in Angola. It
was favorably received and Maciel was given an official mission to investi-
gate the situation more closely.II3 The members of the Literary Society of
