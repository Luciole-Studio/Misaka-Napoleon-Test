Similarities elsewhere in Europe suggest themselves.61
    Among the middle classes in both Nordic kingdoms, general enthusiasm for
t e French Revolution was characteristically combined with strong loyalty to the
ruling monarch. This synthesis rested upon an implied social contract, justified
by democratic, or at least antiaristocratic, social reforms broadly approved by
middle-class opinion. For the present, this dynamic balance provided welcome
backing for the crown and a mandate for further reform. But it could in time
break down, giving cause for alarm among kings and their ministers.
