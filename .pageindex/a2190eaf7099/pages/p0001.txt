## B3英国海军（卡t_998052）
James 1837 edition *Naval History* 年初年度舰表，Paul Benyon的HTML转录而不是我亲阅原表影本；B3 `table_historical.md`：1813海勤Line I102/ordinary22/H124、1814 I99/ordinary19/H118、1815 I47/ordinary62/H109；1808账籍Line237非海勤126。1803–14 Built共74，1808–12共46。英国1812票定总员145000含31400陆战队；FB Gregory Fremont-Barnes 2007 p44总拨款£19,305,759（可含运输，不等实支）。`B3_response.csv` W/P高低配对H、A12、I、现役巡防和小舰、常备人数与按1812价费用；`model_assumptions.md`特别交代H/A12/I、M=650L+280R+110C+O，三型含舰上陆战队，不再加全体陆战队；K=£4–6m+£85–105*总员为纯模型，W£15–21.5m，P£9–15.3m。英国年造Line 4–8常态/8–12高压，历史据James，B3五年包低30、高40，退出现数反算，非实测退役。
Hansard 1817-02-17 vol35 cc408–409原决议六阴历月19,000含6,000陆战队，原句 `The number of men which he had to propose was 19,000, of which number the royal marines were 6,000.`；1820-05-17 vol1 cc459–460，23,000含8,000，13阴历月；1821-06-27 vol5 cc1345–1442，Hume转引Annual Estimates/Finance Accounts：1817总估£5.985m与实支£6.473m、1820总估£6.691m与实支£6.388m；其船工裁员意见前提为 `with the prospect of a long peace`；1830-03-01 vol22 cc1121–1143计划平均29,000含9,000，彼时实际>32,000，原句 `our naval force must partly depend upon that of other powers`；以上所读选段与转引级别保存于 `nodes/r_5b1357a9c6/cards/t_998052/sources/postwar/read_evidence_and_notes.md`，相应缓存列于该文件。1815-06-14 Vansittart `that expense now incurred for our armies would cease, and the supplies at present demanded for them could be applied to the service of our navy` cc806–807，Tierney反对不加条件认作永续转拨（B3 SOURCES.md、downloads/pages/156d03703582.md）。
