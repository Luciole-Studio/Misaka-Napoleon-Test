The aim of this series in early modern and modern European history is to publish
outstanding works of research, addressed to important themes across a wide geo-
graphical range, from southern and central Europe, to Scandinavia and Russia,
and from the time of the Renaissance to the Second World War. As it develops
the series will comprise focused works of wide contextual range and intellectual
ambition.
For a full list of titles published in the series, please see the end of the book.
