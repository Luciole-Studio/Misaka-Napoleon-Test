                       Other public revenues were obtained from a mosaic of fiscal instruments.
                   For instance, the colonial government received a number of ecclesiastical
                   fiscal transfers, including the media anata, bulas, and a percentage of the
                   tithes collected from peasants and landlords. These arrangements, dating
                   back to the sixteenth century, bespeak the fact that the Catholic Church
                   was closely linked to the Crown not only ideologically but also in fiscal
                   terms.
