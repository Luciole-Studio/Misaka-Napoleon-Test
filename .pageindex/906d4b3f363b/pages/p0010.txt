

## What were the implications of Napoleon's reliance on "unauthorized" seizures by his officers during his campaigns?

Napoleon's toleration of 'unauthorized' seizures by his officers during campaigns added a layer of complexity to the financial and diplomatic dimensions of his wars. While it allowed for more flexible requisitions, it caused disparities in wealth accrued by different officers, potentially undermining discipline and uniformity. Furthermore, it complicated post-war financial settlements with occupied territories, as seen in his correspondence, which humorously suggested that accounting for these would result in a net debt to Prussia rather than a benefit .

## How did the financial strategies implemented during the Napoleonic Wars illustrate the economic pressures faced by France?

The financial strategies during the Napoleonic Wars show France's need to alleviate economic pressure through external means. By employing requisitions, demanding contributions from occupied territories, and utilizing allies to bear the costs of garrisoning troops, Napoleon sought to reduce the financial strain on France's Treasury. The reliance on 'ordinary' and 'extraordinary' contributions from these regions illustrates these pressures, highlighting France's inability to sustain prolonged military campaigns purely from domestic resources. This dependency underscores the balance Napoleon attempted between military ambitions and economic stability .

## Evaluate how Napoleon's creation of the "Domaine Extraordinaire" reflected his approach to state and military finance.

The creation of the "Domaine Extraordinaire" exemplified Napoleon's pragmatic and centralized approach to military finance, reflecting his broader strategy of ensuring that military gains were translated into political and economic capital controlled directly by him. This autonomous financial entity allowed Napoleon to channel resources towards military rewards and grand state projects without legislative oversight, underscoring his view that military victories should self-finance further military and state endeavors. This reflects his adeptness in consolidating power and exerting financial control independently of conventional state structures .

## How did the Napoleon's system of financing military campaigns depend on the economic capacity of occupied territories?

Napoleon's system relied heavily on requisitions and contributions from occupied territories. The financial support needed to pay troops was extracted from seized local public finances, confiscated taxes, and additional contributions demanded from local authorities. This approach made the French army's payroll dependent on the capacity of these regions to pay their dues. Wealthier areas like Germany, Prussia, and Austria provided ample resources, whereas poorer regions like Poland, Russia, and Spain led to delayed payments due to their lesser economic capability .
