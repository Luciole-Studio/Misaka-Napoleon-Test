## Daily cost of maintaining a soldier under the Empire

 Thanks to Mollien, we know the daily maintenance cost for a soldier during the Empire (the Treasury minister was after all in the best position to make this kind of estimate). The cost amounts to 1.91 francs (pay, equipment, engineering, travel, and military accoutrements included).15

## Calculation of the savings generated through the use of allied soldiers

 I then simply multiplied the number of soldier days for the allied contingents by their daily maintenance cost to get the amount saved for each military unit. Using the same example of the Italian Royal Guard, 3,285,000 days equated to a saving of 6.274 million francs (3,285,000 x 1.91) for France.
