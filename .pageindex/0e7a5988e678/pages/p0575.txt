PONDICHERRY 

Is  situated  in  latitude  11°  54'  North,  and  longitude  79°  58'  East.  It  was  the  capital  of  the  French 
settlements  in  India,  and  the  residence  of  the  Governor-Greneral  and  superior  ( Council ; and  before  its 
fortifications  and  public  buildings  were  destroyed  by  the  English,  Pondicherry  was  by  far  the  largest, 
strongest,  and  most  beautiful  European  settlement  in  the  East  Indies. 

Pondicherry  is  built  in  a circular  form  on  the  borders  of  the  sea,  and  strongly  fortified.  It  is  divided 
into  two  parts  the  Black  Town  and  the  White  Town;  the  latter  spreads  along  the  sea^oast,  and  is  again 
divided  into  two  parts,  the  north  and  south.  The  tower  bearing  the  flagstaff  is  in  the  middle,  and  sepa- 
rates the  two  quarters.  The  Black  Town  is  separated  from  the  White  by  a ditch  running  through  the
