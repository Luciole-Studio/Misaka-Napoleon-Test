Are  an  article  of  trade  from  the  Arabian  and  Persian  Gulfs  to  India,  and  from  thence  to  China;  they 
are  esteemed  very  strengthening  by  the  Chinese.  In  chusing  them,  care  should  be  taken  that  they  have

they  are  generally  sold  by  tale:  each  fin  should  be  upwards  of  nine  inches  long;  all  under  that  size,  reckon
