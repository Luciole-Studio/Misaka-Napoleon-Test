ELEPHANTS’  HAIR. 

The  hairs  which  grow  on  the  tail  of  the  elephant,  arc  stilF  and  remarkably  smooth,  of  a glossy  black 
colour,  14  or  15  inches  long,  and  of  the  size  of  small  iron  wire;  they  are  not  hollow,  but  of  a horny  nature 
throughout  their  whole  substance;  they  are  very  tough,  and  will  in  general  bear  to  he  tied  or  doubled 
without  breaking,  for  which  reason  they  would  be  useful  for  making  beaids  to  fish  liooks,  though  some 
of  them  are,  on  the  contrary,  very  brittle;  the  greater  part  are  rather  Hat  tliaii  round,  and  make  neat 
ornaments  for  ladies’  rings,  broaches,  &c.
