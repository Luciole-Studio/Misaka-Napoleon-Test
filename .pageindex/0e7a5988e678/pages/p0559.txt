The  coast  from  Cape  Ramcn  to  Point  Calymere  comprehends  the  provinces  of  the  Marawars  and 
Tondiman;  the  principal  towns  on  the  coast  are  Tondy  and  Cottapatam,  frequented  only  by  small  coasters. 
On  Point  Calymere,  which  is  in  latitude  1(P  18'  North,  arc  two  remarkable  pagodas.  About  half  a mile  to 
the  northward  of  them  is  a small  river,  and  on  its  banks  stands  a large  village,  where  a considerable  trade  is 
carried  on  in  tobacco,  rice,  piece-goods,  &c.;  the  river  has  a bar,  so  that  only  small  vessels  can  enter.  The 
kingdom  of  Tanjore  commences  to  the  southward  of  Point  Calymere,  and  extends  to  the  northward  as 
far  as  the  Coleroon  River.
