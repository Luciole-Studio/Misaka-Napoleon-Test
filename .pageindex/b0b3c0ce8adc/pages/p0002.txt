

> 1810-08-05 Cadore致Armstrong正式声明，把"禁止对英贸易"的明确要求替换为较模糊的"conformably with [Macon's Bill] …, shall cause their rights to be respected by the English."

> Perkins裁断："Was it really correct to say that the decrees were 'revoked,' when their effect was not to cease until November and then only if one of two conditions was fulfilled? Quite obviously not"；Cadore本人随后声明"decision of H.M. is … conditional."

【卡内判读·承重】卡多尔信是**附条件的诱饵**而非撤令；但麦迪逊选择据此对英重启不进口。这正是"对法扣船怨恨"与"对英开战"并存的关键机制：法国给了一张**可被美国政府当作外交工具使用的纸**，英国没有给。
