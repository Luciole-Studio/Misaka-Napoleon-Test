The new Third Western Army under Vice Admiral Tshitshagov moved toward the
Beresina. Its strength and organization when it arrived on the scene is described in
the text. After crossing the Beresina, some formations (3 battalions, 8 squadrons,
6 Cossack regiments, and IVi batteries) remained there; others were left behind in
the further course of the pursuit.
    Colonel von Knorring's detachment (2 battalions, 8 squadrons, and Vi battery)
joined by another 2 gun section from the main army, marched into the Minsk area.
    When departing for the Beresina, Tshitshagov left behind Corps Osten-Sacken,
which continued to watch the border.      It   was eventually joined by the Ukrainian
Cossacks under Witte. Repninski's detachment was formed from these elements.
