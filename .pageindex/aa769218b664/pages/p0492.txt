The total Russian army at Borodino included 72,000 infantry, 17,300 cavalry, and
14,500 artillery, with 637 guns. The number of guns is generally given as 640, but
those lost at Shevardino on September 5 must be subtracted.

Two batteries cannot be accounted for. Probably, as was frequently the case on
campaign, they were sent back for replenishment or were disbanded to bring others
up to establishment. The troops sent to Riga had been part of Wittgenstein's corps.
