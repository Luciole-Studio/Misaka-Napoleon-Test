Thus, the total number of Russian troops at this time was 253,500 men present,
with an additional 32,142   men coming up. The present strength excludes 8000
militiamen who were soon to be withdrawn from the line, but it includes 15,000
men who garrisoned Riga and Bobruisk. The strength of those coming up excludes
11,000 men who no longer found use, but it includes 3000 men who went to Riga.
