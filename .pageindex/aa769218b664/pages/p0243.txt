228               1812: NAPOLEON^S RUSSIAN CAMPAIGN


10.   At the beginning of the campaign, Ney's corps had numbered 39,342 men.
      Now, exclusive of the seven battalions left in Kovno and Vilng, the corps had
      only 16,053 effectives; by September 3,   it   was further diminished to 11,278
      men. The Wuerttembergian infantry, 8200 men in the beginning, lost only 555
      men in the action; on 2 September, it was down to 1456 men, who were
      amalgamated into three composite battalions.
