Regarding the table above, several points should be noted:


    1.   The numbers deviate from Chambray only in that he does not extrapolate
         the strength of the Cavalry for IV and         V Corps and thus comes up with an
         extra 2500 infantry and correspondingly fewer cavalry.

    2.   The artillery, as of October 7, had 571 guns and 2325 artillery vehicles.
    3. VIII Corps, at this time,     numbered about 5000 men, with 34 guns.
