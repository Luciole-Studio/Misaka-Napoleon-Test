    As a result, the catastrophe shaping up on the horizon was becoming
inevitable, and it no longer allowed any margin for error. Napoleon had
already lost the campaign. The only real question that remained was what
price the Russians would exact. If the situation in the flanks deteriorated
any further, only the Russians could cheat themselves out of a total victory.
