# 本轮实际读取的相关原文摘录（不是Glover正文）

## S1：J. C. A. Stagg，The Political Essays of William Shaler（2002）
来源：https://oieahc.wm.edu/wp-content/uploads/stagg.pdf
分篇版本：https://oieahc.wm.edu/wp-content/uploads/stagg_essay2.pdf
读取方式：web_extract返回PDF文本；未获得本地PDF字节，未做影像校对。读取完整的Essay II分篇及总篇相关导言、尾注。总篇未逐段全文读完。

总篇注33＝Essay II分篇注2：
> The (Baltimore) Weekly Register, Oct. 19, 26, 1811, printed reports on Napoleon's efforts to rebuild the French navy at Antwerp since the Battle of Trafalgar, including the prediction that France would shortly put 150 ships of the line to sea; see Richard Glover, "The French Fleet, 1807-1814: Britain's Problem; and Madison's Opportunity," Journal of Modern History, 39 (1967), 233-52.

Essay II正文开头：
> It may be occupied by an expedition from France; it may be the object of the great armament in the Scheldt.

总篇注16：
> None of the first 4 essays reached Washington.

注意：Stagg在注33用“see”列Glover整篇，而非逐字引用Glover；150的口径是1811年报纸预言的出海战列舰数，不是1814实有或配员数。Weekly Register两期原件未阅。

## S2：Michael McTavish，Building a Warrior: Industry, Empire and the Royal Navy’s Iron Ships 1847–1860
Nipissing University学位论文，当前读到的题页未显示年份。
来源：https://utoronto.scholaris.ca/bitstreams/bda99cfd-9fc5-4a50-89c7-89b96aa97bd4/download
读取方式：PDF文本提取；实际阅读有关第11—13印刷页及注35—36，不称全文已读。

印刷p.12，正文：
> the last decade of the conflict saw the British and the French engage in a massive construction and reconstruction campaign focused on ships of the line.

注35引用Glover，但写作“229-35”；Glover实际文章始于233页，该页码范围有明显错误。注36为Ibid，谈未干燥木材和英国选材变化；本段未提供所需的1814存量、年度下水或船厂产能数字。不能作为逐数转引。

## S3：Haris Dajč（2024）
The British-French Naval Rivalry in the Ionian and Adriatic Basins 1807–1814 from an insular perspective，Povijesni prilozi 67，287–305。
DOI：https://doi.org/10.22586/pp.v43i67.32824
全文入口：https://hrcak.srce.hr/file/469145
读取方式：实际阅读提取文本有关威尼斯造船、海上通道及结论部分，并检查返回的参考文献；未逐页校影像，也未称全篇已读。

“British–French rivalry 1809–1814”节，注45邻近段落：
> As would be obvious later, the shipyard programme of the French was too slow and proved not to be much of a challenge for the British navy.

此处注45引Sondhaus（1989）p.354。论文讨论亚得里亚海造舰到实际部署的阻碍，不是Glover的逐数转载；所见书目没有Glover。本轮不据此裁决法国全国舰队规模，不录入数量模型。可作为以后读Sondhaus的定位线索。
