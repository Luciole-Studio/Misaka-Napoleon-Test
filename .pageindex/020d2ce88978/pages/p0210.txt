DIRECT    TAXES. 

By  the  constitution,  direct  taxes,  when  laid,  are  to  be  apportioned 
among  the  States,  in  the  same  manner,  as  representatives,  including 
three  6fVhs  of  the  slave  population.  This  part  of  the  constitution  waa 
a  compromise  between  the  slave  holding  and  non  slave  holding  states; 
the  former,  agreeing  to  pay  direct  taxes,  according  to  the  ratio  of 
Uieir  representation.  Notwithstanding  this,  four  direct  taxes  only, 
have  been  laid,  from  the  commencement  of  the  government — the 
slave  holding  states,  therefore,  have  enjoyed  the  benefits  of  this 
compromise  without  feeling  much  of  its  burdens.
