CHINA   AND   THE    NORTH    WEST   COAST    OF   AMERICA. 

The  American  commercial  intercourse  with  China,  commenced 
soon  after  the  close  of  the  peace  of  1783.  The  first  vessel,  from  the 
United  States  to  that  country,  sailed  from  the  port  of  New  York*  on 
the  22d  day  of  February,  1784,  John  Greey,  master,  and  Samuel 
Shaw,  supercago ;  and  returned  on  the  11th  of  May,  1785.
