It having been stated that the Funds at the disposal of the Supracargoes, 
amounted to £. 31,375,413 and that of those Funds £. 27,583,054 had been 
employed in the purchase of Investments consigned to England, and in sundry. 
payments to be accounted for by the Concern in England, and that £. 2,179,968 was. 
disbursed on account of India and Saint Helena, Your Committee will pursue their 
enquiry into the disposal of the remainder, amounting to £. 1,612,391. 


It appears that the direct and established Charges of the Factory are uniformly 
added to the Invoice price of the Investments consigned to England, and that other 
Charges were incurred, and Losses experienced in the course of the management of the 
business, which were not so added. By an Account, which is inserted in the 
Appendix, it appears too that the amount of the Charges and Losses of the descrip- 
tion now alluded to, was £.995,243. The Loss in this instance is however only 
apparent as applied to the entire concerns of the Company, because the calculation 
is made after crediting England for goods imported from thence at the invoice price 
of them, which is charged with the addition of £. 1,449,767, being 10 per cent. upon 
the real cost. Adjusting this sum with the Loss above shewn, a Profit would be 
exhibited amounting to £.454,524. : 


The Balance of Stock in China in favour of the Company, in January 1792, was 
£- 687,906. In March 1809 it was £.1,306,606; being an increase amounting to 
£618,700; adding this sum to the Charges and Losses, the total will be & 1,613,043, 
which exceeds the Amount to be accounted for by £. 1,552} @ variation which is not 
of suflicient importance to require particular investigation.

pany. In the’ reign of Charles IL it was granted as a port of refreshment for 
ships engaged in the India ‘Trade, on their homeward-bound voyage, and transferred
