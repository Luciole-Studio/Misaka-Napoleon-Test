The species of Trade denominated in the Statement originally adverted to Neutral Appendix, N° 2& 
and Prize Goods, will not call for remark further thatras it passes through the medium of 
the Company. Its first appearance was in the year 1795-6, in consequence of the new 
state of affairs upon the Continent of Europe. About the latter end of the year 
1794, the French made their irruption into the United Provinces, which was followed by 
the conquest of the country. This event imposed on His Majesty's Governmentnew 
measures relative to the Dutch Trade with India and China, which was then con- 
siderable. These measures apply to this species of Trade, the provisions which 
governed the Indian Trade under the existing Laws. A special provision was at the 
same time made in favor of such Subjects of the United Provinces as maintained 
their allegiance to the Stadtholder and chose to avail themselves of it, by Orders of 
Council dated 16th and 21st January 1795, afterwards confirmed by the Act of the 
35th of the King, cap. 15. and again by another Act in the same year, cap. 80. and 
other Acts since passed.
