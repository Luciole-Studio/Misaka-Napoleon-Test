The value of the Assets on the 1st March 1810 is stated at £. 14,504,944. 
Deduceting from it their Amount on. the 1st March 1793, there will be an Increase in 
the sum of £.4,764,112. The Increase thus shewn, however, calls for some 
observation and adjustment. 


The value of ‘Assets under the head of Goods in. the Warehouse unsold in each 
of the periods, is computed on Estimate governed by the sale price at the time, and 
conscquently includes the Profit after defraying all Charges. As the value of goods at 
the latter period very considerably exceeded at the former, the computed Profit 
on the 1st March 1810 was likewise much greater. The excess amounted to 

Appendix, X° 50. _£. 998,000, and should be deducted from the Increase of the Assets, because, although 
it may fairly be assumed as a future available Profit, it has no immediate reference to 
the Cash proceedings of the period. Credit is likewise taken for the sum of 

Appendix, N°23. £. 960,000 as a claim upon Government for Stores supplied, Advances, &c. This. 
article cannot but be considered as disputable, if not wholly objectionable, if reference 
is had to the Balance settled and paid after the Report of Your Committee in 1808, 
although it may not from its connection with the Disbursements, be excluded in the 
view to be now given. Deducting therefore the increased Amount of the Profits, viz. 
£- 998,000, from the additional Amount of the Assets as above shewn, the Increase in 
the period will be reduced to £.3,766,112. The Increase of the Debts has been 
shewn to amount to £. 2,366,010, consequently an improvement would at first view 
be apparent in the sum of £.1,400,102. The Money raised in the year 1793 upon 

Appendix, N° 22. Capital Stock, to the amount of 2,027,295, though included in the Cash Receipts, is 
not so in the Debts owing by the Company, as now stated. ‘The effect of this Receipt 
will fall more properly under consideration with that of the combined operations of 
the whole Concern upon the Balance of Property generally.
