LIGHTHOUSE DUES. 

A lighthouse having been erected on the bank of Gowcally Creek, to the southward of Kedgeree, for 
the purpose of facilitating the navigation of the river, and of conducing to the safety of. ships which may 
anchor in Kedgeree roads, the public are hereby informed, that a light will be exhibited in the lanthorn 
of the lighthouse on the loth of June, IS 10 ; after which date, a duty will be levied, on account of Govern¬ 
ment, upon all ships, brigs, and sloops, including coasting vessels and denies, which may navigate the River 
Hughley, as follows :— 

I. Upon every vessel, being British property, or which may be owned by natives residing under the 
protection of the British flag, as well as those belonging to the United States of America, 2 annas per ton 
per annum. 

II. Upon every vessel, being the property of foreigners, whether belonging to Europeans or Asiatics, 
4 annas per ton per annum. 

III. The charge for the above duty will be included in the pilotage bill, inwards or outwards, at the 
option of the master attendant.
