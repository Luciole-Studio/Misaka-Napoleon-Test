1780. The Dutch having entered into an offensive and defensive treaty with America, then at war 
with Great Britain, the latter power declared war against Holland, and fitted out a squadron, under 
the command of Commodore Johnstone, to proceed against the Cape of Good Hope; but the French, 
under the command of Suffrein, arriving there before him, frustrated the object of the expedition. He, 
however, surprised five home-ward bound East Indiamen in Saldanha Bay, on the 21st of July, four 
of which he took possession of; the fifth, the Middleburgh, the largest and richest, was burnt.
