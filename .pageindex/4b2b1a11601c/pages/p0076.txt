XVII. If any of His Majesty’s ships are at anchor, it may be taken for granted

arrives or departs from the roads, and salutes, the King’s ships will of course 
answer it; but if they salute a second time, the fort returns it. 

XVIII. Whilst an Admiral, Commodore, or senior Captain of any of His Majesty’s 
ships are in the roads, no ship having a junior Captain, is to be saluted, 
as he cannot answer the salute; but the Captain is to have his proper salute
