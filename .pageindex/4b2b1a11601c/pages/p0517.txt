From the state of European warfare, the Americans, being a neutral nation, have derived great advan¬ 
tages ; they have had access to countries from whence the English were shut out, where they disposed of 
considerable quantities of the commodities of India and China: but the war between Great Britain and 
America has put a stop to this; otherwise, on the return of peace, all these advantages would have ceased, as 
America cannot maintain a successful competition against the Company, either in India or China.
