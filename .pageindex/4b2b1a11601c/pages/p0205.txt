Under the head of sundries paid in England, are included passage of military homewards; sundries for 
the use of the military on the voyage to India; freight and demurrage of ships employed in carrying mili¬ 
tary or stores from port to port in India, or as cartels, depot at the Isle of Wight, and recruiting; cadets 
and military seminary; and various other items connected with the Company’s military establishments, 
amounting in the whole to 6,482,125.
