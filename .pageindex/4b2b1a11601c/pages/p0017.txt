Under the head of various places are comprehended the Maidive and Laccadive Islands, Mosambique, 
and East Coast of Africa, New South Wales, Cape of Good Hope, St. Helena, &e. 

The whole of the commerce of this Presidency, commonly called the country trade, is in the hands of 
individuals, the East India Company never interfering, except for the European market. 

The accounts are made up in Bengal Sicca rupees, at the usual rate of exchange, in order to consoli¬ 
date the commerce of all parts of India under one head hereafter. As the entries of both imports and 
exports are verified by oath, and the duties moderate, the following statements may be considered accurate.
