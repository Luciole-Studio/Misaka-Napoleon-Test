MERCHANT VESSELS. 

The merchant vessels belonging to Madras are in general small; not above four are of the burthen of 
800 tons. Several of the smaller ones have been built at Coringa and Rangoon, and the others at Bombay, 
Bengal, and Pegu. This coast being without a secure port for shipping, they proceed to Bengal during 
the boisterous season*
