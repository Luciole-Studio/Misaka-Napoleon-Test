MOOTAPILLY 

1$ about eight leagues N.N. E. of Gondegam. The town is about half a mile inland, not discernible 
from the offing; but with the assistance of a glass, a pagoda is perceptible. There are some detached palmyra 
trees to the northward of the landing-place, and about a mile to the southward a thick grove of trees, with 
a clump on its southern part, higher than the rest. With the northern extremity of a piece of high land 
in one with a thick grove of trees, you are abreast the proper anchorage in 15° 42' North. Large ships lie 
about a mile from the shore, with Mootapilly pagoda bearing N. W. by N.
