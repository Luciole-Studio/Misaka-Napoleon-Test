The Dutch factory at Canton was not, like all their other settlements and places of trade in India, 
subordinate to the Government of Batavia, but under the management of a special Committee of Direc¬ 
tors in Holland; yet every thing relative to it was communicated to, and transacted in concert with the 
Government of Batavia.
