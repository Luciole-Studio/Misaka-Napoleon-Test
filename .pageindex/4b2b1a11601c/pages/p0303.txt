PORT-CHARGES. 

The import and export charges on a ship, of whatever burthen she may be, and presents to the 
principal men, &c. are as follow: 

Anchorage dues w 30 Ticals 
Sundry charges on clearing out the ship, viz. 

Cups, plates, and soft sugaiv^- 30 ditto 

Fowls, hogs, &c. for breakfast —^ 20 ditto 
Pilotage in and out of Rangoon^—300 ditto 
A boat to take the pilot out-- 25 ditto 

forming a total of 1345 ticals. A new ship built in the river, on proceeding on her first voyage, is exempt 
from the port-charges, but she is afterwards subject to all charges as other ships.
