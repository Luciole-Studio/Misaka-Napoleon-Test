Manilla produces indigo, which is of an inferior quality to that manufactured at Bengal. The tobacco 
is excellent, and esteemed the best in Asia, and used formerly to be exported in considerable quantities. 
Sugar is produced in abundance, and was also an article of export to India. The East India Company's 
ships bound to China have occasionally stopped at Manilla; and when they have arrived in August 
or September, considerable quantities of specie have been sent by them, which would otherwise have been 
sent on China junks.
