

The manufacture of long ells is almost confined to a single comity, but the demand for them has 
greatly increased. The camblets formerly exported by the Company were unsaleable, owing to the 
Dutch having a peculiar mode of preparing that article more suitable to the taste of the Chinese. It was 
not till 17S9 the English manufacturers discovered the secret, which they have done so completely, that their 
goods are preferred to those of the Dutch. These articles the Company reserve to themselves. 

In 1789 the Company entered into an engagement with the proprietors of the tin mines in Cornwall, 
to send annually 800 tons of tin to China at a stipulated price. The increase and decrease of the tin have 
arisen from the circumstance of the County of Cornwall being enabled to obtain a higher price in Europe 
for the commodity than formerly, in consequence of which the stipulated quantity of 800 tons has not 
been regularly delivered to the Company.
