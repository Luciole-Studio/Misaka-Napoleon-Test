BALASORE. 

The entrance of the river is in latitude about 21° 28' North. Balasore was formerly a considerable 
town, but at present is only about a mile long, and half a mile broad in the widest part. It is built along the 
river Beree Bellaun, where the tide commonly rises eight feet, and serves to carry vessels up to the dry docks, 
of which there are many here; but the spring tides rise much higher. The stream is navigable only for 
vessels of 100 tons burthen; and these cannot get over the bar at the mouth of the river, except at high 
water spring tides.
