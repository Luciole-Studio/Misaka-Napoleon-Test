PIECE-GOODS. 


The following is the mode in which the tonnage of piece-goods is ascertained: 

When the letter It is against pieces of 400 to a ton, it shews those goods are to be reduced to a stand¬ 
ard of 16 yards long and 1 broad; when against pieces of 800 to a ton, to 10 yards long and 1 broad. 

Example.— 1000 pieces of 12 yards long and If broad, at 400 pieces to a ton, make 844 pieces, or 
2 tons 44 pieces; and 1000 pieces of 104 long by If, at 800 to a ton, are 1,181 pieces, or 1 ton 381 pieces.
