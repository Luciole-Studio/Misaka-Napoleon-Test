PULO CONDORE, 

The principal island of a small group which goes by this name, about three leagues long from N. E. 
to S.W. and two to four miles broad; the centre is in latitude 8° 40'North, and longitude 106° 42' 
East, and is about 17 leagues S. by E. from the mouth of Cambodia River. This island is the only one 
inhabited; it is a ridge of high mountains, difficult of access, and separating the harbour from the great
