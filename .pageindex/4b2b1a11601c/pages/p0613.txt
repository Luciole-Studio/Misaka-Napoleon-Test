The Company's imports from England consist of provisions and clothing for the troops and inhabit¬ 
ants of the island, which are issued from the stores at an advance on the prime cost, sufficient to cover the 
expences attending their importation, such as freight, insurance, &c. and of the various stores requisite for 
the supply of shipping, on which an advance of 50 per cent, is charged to the owners of the ships. The 
Company also import from China, teas, China-ware, and other commodities, and from their settlements 
in India, piece-goods, &c. During the period above stated, their imports amounted to <£*320,795.
