Fort St George, December 28, 1797. 

The Governor in Council at Bombay having thought fit to alter the period of dispatching the 
monthly overland packet, via Bussorah, to England, from the beginning to the middle of every month, 
has given notice that the alteration will have effect from that of June next ensuing, when the dispatch is 
to take place on the loth instead of the 1st, and to be similarly continued in every succeeding month.-

“ The Right Honourable the Governor in Council having, under orders from the Honourable the Court 
of Directors, adopted a new mode of preparing the overland mails from this Presidency; notice is hereby 
given, that in consequence of this arrangement, only one packet will in future be dispatched monthly from 
Fort St George.—The overland mail will in future be dispatched for Bombay about the 5th or Gth of each 
month.
