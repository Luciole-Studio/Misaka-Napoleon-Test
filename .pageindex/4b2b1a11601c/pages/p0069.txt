The Company's staple article of export is piece-goods. Sugar and saltpetre are sent from Bengal as 
dead weight for such of the Company’s ships as take in their cargoes at Madras; but as few articles of 
trade are procurable here, the homeward-bound Bengal ships reserve sufficient tonnage for such piece-goods 
as may be destined for Europe from this Presidency. 

The commercial charges comprise the salaries and allowances of the Board of Trade, of a description 
strictly commercial, with those of the factories where the investments are provided; and also the charge of 
the import warehouse, and the departments where the goods imported from England, are deposited and sold.
