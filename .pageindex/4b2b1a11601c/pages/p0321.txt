IMPORT DUTIES. 

The following are the established duties to be levied on all imports and exports, viz. 

I. On the invoice price of all European goods imported on British ships and vessels, not 

having previously paid Government duties at any British port in India—— 5 per cent. 

II. On the invoice price of all European goods imported on all foreign ships and vessels, 

with the exception of Americans, who are to pay as British^-8 per cent.
