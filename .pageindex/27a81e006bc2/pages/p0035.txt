Forecasting the (Stability) of Inflation


In a first step, I check whether expected inflation, π e was stable during the suspension
period (for a discussion see Bordo and Kydland, 1995; Rolnick and Weber, 1997). This
amounts to testing whether the same rate of inflation was anticipated for the gold stan-
dard and the suspension period alike. The exact process of expectation formation is
beyond the scope of this article. I, therefore, posit that agents forecast inflation using
information regarding its past evolutions. Besides the simplicity of this approach, simple
back-ward looking forecasts of inflation are in practice hard to beat by more sophisti-
cated ones (Faust and Wright, 2013).
