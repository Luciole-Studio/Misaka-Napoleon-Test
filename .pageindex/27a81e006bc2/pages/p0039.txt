Table 4 displays the R2 for the different segments. During peace times, switching from the
gold standard to the paper pound explains the difference in mean yields well (R2 = 0.61).
Similarly, the difference in the average yields is well captured by a switch from peace to
war times under the gold standard (R2 = 0.61). On the contrary, during the suspension,
little of the variance between average consol yields can be attributed to the transition
from peace to war (R2 = 0.27). The same is true for switching monetary regimes during
war times (R2 = 0.12). The evidence presented in tables 3 and 4 entails the presence of
premia for default and anticipated inflation.

In order to check whether these premia are constant over time, table 5 displays standard
deviations for the four different segments. Standard deviations are significantly differ-
