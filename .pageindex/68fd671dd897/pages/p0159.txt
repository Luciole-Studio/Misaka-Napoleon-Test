37. “Resumen del segundo regimiento,” AGN, Guerra, legajo 21, car. 20. Reille’s report of the battle dated 1 August 1810 listed eight killed and seventy wounded, but said nothing about the Navarrese renegades captured. AAT, C8, 268. The truth probably lies somewhere in between these two partisan accounts. The Navarrese source indicated that the guerrillas wounded 400 enemy troops. This demonstrates how the guerrillas exaggerated enemy losses, particularly the numbers of wounded. Combatants have always “rounded off” or changed body counts, but the Navarrese estimates have to be treated with particular skepticism, since the guerrillas almost never maintained the field (until 1812) even in victory, and the French were usually able to retreat in orderly fashion with their wounded. That battle figures were dispatched to officials hundreds of miles away in Valencia or Cádiz with no means of verification is another good reason to discount the figures given by the guerrillas. In most cases, I have discarded Navarrese figures for French wounded. Usually this results in a close correspondence between the French and Spanish estimates for casualties, and it is probably as near to precision as we will ever get.
