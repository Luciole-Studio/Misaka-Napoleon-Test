 STATEMENT shewing the Quantity and Value of each of 36 articles, the Produce and Manufactures of the United Kingdom,
EXPORTED from thence, to Sweden, Norway, Denmark, and Prussia, in each of the 4 Years 1827- 1830.
   %* The Imports from these Countries, as well as from Russia and France, appear to exceed the Exports thereto, but such a conclusion is
fallacious, a considerable portion of the Exports to Prussia being made by way of Hamburgh to the Havel River, which runs past Berlin and
Potsdam to the Elbe at Havelburgh ; while the bulk of the Imports from Prussia are derived Jrom the Ports in the Baltic, Viz. Memel, Konigsburgh, Dantzig, Sutlin, Elbing, S,c.; a
considerable portion of the Imports from Prussia are absorbed in discharge of the Interest and Sinking Fund on Loans raised for the service of the Prussian Government since 1818 .*
