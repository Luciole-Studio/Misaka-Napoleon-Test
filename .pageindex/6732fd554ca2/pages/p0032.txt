     %* The following page exhibits a continuation of the above Account more amplified and complete.       The Charge for Collection when separated from the Super-
annuations and Allowances, and expences attending the Prevention of Smuggling, and other Payments, is comparatively inconsiderable.          The increasing expence
attending the Prevention of Smuggling ( amounting in 1832 to £384,057, exclusive of £ 18,303 as compensation to Naval Officers in the Coast Guard Service),
deserves in a social and moral rather than in a pecuniary point of view, more than an ordinary share of attention ; no such means us have hitherto
                                                                                                                                                      been adopted’,
whatever expence they may incur, will ever diminish the practice.   If Smuggling is to be suppressed, it must be by those social arrangements with neighbouring
States, which an honest and enlightened mind will have no difficulty either in devising or reducing to practice.
