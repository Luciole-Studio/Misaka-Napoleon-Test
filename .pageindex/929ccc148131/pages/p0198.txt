        The length of serviceof a soldier had a bearingupon the likely efficiency of the

army. The longer soldiersserved,the more likely the army would be able to perform the

tasksset for it, and the more likely it would be that the soldier would break all ties with

civilian society,and embracehis new home- the regiment.As with ages,the serviceof

soldiers between 1807 and 1815 remained remarkably constant,66 with privates

averagingsix yearsand four monthsservice,and the averageNCOs spendingnine and a

half years in the army. It does appear that the average service of a soldier was

lengtheningin the period, and although this is likely during a period when the army
