As will be shown later, the Transport and Victualling Boards were homes to constant improvement and refining of administrative procedures. These went hand-in-hand with a reformation of the entire make-up of naval administration, dissolYing patronage, replacing hereditary positions with a meritocracy and replacing fees with salaries. The implementation of the reforms to the victualling system depended upon these wider ranging changes. The reforms of 1809, in particular, demonstrated that competence and merit had come to rule the respective offices.
