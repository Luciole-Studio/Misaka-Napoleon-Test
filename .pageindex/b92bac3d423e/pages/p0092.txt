

The Victualling System in practice: 1808-1809

In 1808 there were four deliveries of victuals to the Baltic fleet. The Victualling Board was always faced with a dilemma: the more deliveries it made, the fresher the food would be, while spreading the risk of transport loss at sea. They could not very well send out victuallers every few weeks, however, as such an arrangement would be both expensive and an administrative nightmare. The order came from the Admiralty to 'provide with all possible expedition a proportion of Provisions of all species for two months for the squadron of His Majesty's Ships and Vessels in the Baltic reporting to their Lordships Secretary when the said Provisions are ready'.
