

Judging efficiency is a matter of judging wastage against shortage. We know the number of men stationed in each theatre and can therefore calculate the number of rations needed over a year. From the Victualling Board minutes, we can also calculate the number of rations sent out, and compare the two. If there were significantly higher amounts of rations being sent out than were needed, then we can talk of wastage. If there is significantly less being sent out, then it is clear there was a shortage. The Baltic theatre particularly lends itself to this mode of analysis due to its annual status. Since the
