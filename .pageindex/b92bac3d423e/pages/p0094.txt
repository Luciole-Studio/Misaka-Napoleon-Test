Each shipment carried exact amounts of provisions for the fleet for the given number of men on station. Victualling Board officials, knowing the number of men to be provided for, worked out specific amounts for a specific time. The decisions were taken on an informal basis at daily meetings. In the Victualling Board out-letters is a document from 1809, detailing the precise calculations made by that Board for the provisioning of the Baltic fleet, with amounts of each species calculated to last for specific periods:
