restore Polish independence (1846, 1848, 1863–64) carried far more risk
of repression than promise of military honours or social advancement.
In the historical consciousness of future generations the motives of the
earlier volunteers became sanitized; and heroism came to overshadow
the other, more complex, motives that lay behind the decisions by so
many volunteers to join the Polish army in the period of the Duchy of
Warsaw.
