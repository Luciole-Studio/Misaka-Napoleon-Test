      the prefect of this department, indirectly defended his conduct to Lebrun by

      prudence by taking vigorous action in this instance as I did in the Tyrol in

      any blood, I gained the same results which it required severe measures to
      achieve at Oud-Beyerland’. See de Stassart to Lebrun, 28 February 1813, in
      Colenbrander, Gedenkstukken, vol. 6, pp. 984–985.
