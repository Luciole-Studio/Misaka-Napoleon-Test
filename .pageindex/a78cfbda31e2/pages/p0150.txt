  On the 9th of June, in the morning, the British 18-gun brig-
sloop, Kangaroo, Captain George Christopher Bulling, and 14-gun
brig-sloop Speedy, Captain Lord Cochrane, discovered a Spanish
convoy, lying at an anchor under the battery of Oropeso, a small
seaport of Old Castile. The armed vessels protecting it con-
sisted of a xebec of 20 guns, and three gun-boats, and the battery
was a large square tower, which appeared to mount 12 guns.
The two commanders resolved, at once, to attack this force
with their brigs.  Accordingly, at noon, the Kangaroo and
Speedy came to an anchor, within half-gun shot of the enemy,
