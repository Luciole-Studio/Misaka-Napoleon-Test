                    RECAPITULATORY ABSTRACT.

Showing the Number of French, Dutch, Spanish, and Danish Ships of the Line
  and Frigates, Captured, Destroyed, Wrecked, Foundered, and Accidentally
  Burnt, during the War commencing in February, 1793, and ending in
  1801 ; also the Number of Captured Ships added to the British Navy
  during the same period.
