Glossary of Arabic and Turkish terms

usya: a piece of land granted to a tnultazim in remuneration for tax-collecting
  services

wajba: a hospitality tax
wall: a governor of a province
tvaqf: land or other forms of wealth alienated as mortmain for a religious
  bequest, or entailed in favour of one's family members
uiarsha (plur. wurash): a workshop; an office
wurash: see tuarsha

yuzbashi: a captain

zimam: the land or acreage belonging to a village
