

After the beginning of the new ministry., as Fox sought to restore 
harmony to Anglo-American relations without affronting the un- 
ruly brass players under Stephen, a reedy obbligato wafted across 
the Atlantic from the United States. The notes seemed confused, 
for the leading wood winds declined to instruct their colleagues, 
but the central theme of commercial warfare clearly emerged. 
By cutting off Britain's market, perhaps merely by threatening to 
do so, America would compel England to accept her demands. 
This was good Republican doctrine upon which almost all agreed,
