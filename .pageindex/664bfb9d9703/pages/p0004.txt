

In the 1970s the federal government introduced a minimum reserve price scheme that subsidised the industry. By 1990 it was apparent the scheme was economically unviable and, when it was discontinued, the industry stalled.

Slowly the wool industry rebuilt itself. In 2020, although accounting for less than 10 per cent of the world’s sheep population, Australia dominated the global fine wool market, producing more than 50 per cent of the world’s merino wool.
