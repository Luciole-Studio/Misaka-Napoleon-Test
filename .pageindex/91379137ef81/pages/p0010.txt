and sketched out over some excellent Belgian ale in Christianshavn,
Copenhagen in early August 2009, we have incurred many debts. We
would like to thank John Mason and Manuscripts for translating our

Ruth Hemstad and Martin Austnes for very helpful comments and sug-

ing an as yet unpublished article with us. Thanks also to Mette Dahl
Hansen at the Royal Library, Copenhagen for her assistance. The excel-
lent scholars associated with the so-called Norwegian ‘1814 Project’ at
the University of Oslo and the Danish ‘1814 Network’ at the University
of Southern Denmark have provided us with many opportunities for
fruitful discussion, and for this we thank them all. Last, but not least,
we would like to thank Alan Forrest and Rafe Blaufarb for their encour-
agement. While all these people have made invaluable contributions to
the making of this book, all flaws and errors that remain are of course
solely our own.
  This publication has received funding from the Research Council of
Norway.
