## 1. 1807-05-01 Castlereagh 内阁备忘录（印 pp.314–324；txt 行 14802–15200）

### 1.1 政策回顾（对"征服 vs 解放"falsifier 的直接证据）
- 【事实·亲读】"it does not appear that any original purpose prevailed in the Councils of the late Government, to commence operations in South America"（印 p.315，行 14818–14821）→ 1806 年的行动**不是**一项既定征服国策，而是 Popham 自行发动后被追认。
- 【事实·亲读】Windham 训令的口径："any intention of countenancing an immediate change in the constitution of the Settlement is disclaimed; the purposes of the expedition are confined to **a mere military occupation**, and the assurances to be given to the inhabitants limited to a declaration that his Majesty would, upon a peace, be disposed to attend to their interests, as far as possible."（印 p.315）
- 【事实·亲读】时间链：1806-06-24 收到 Popham 已进军的通知；7-10 下令准备一支分遣队；**逾三月未发增援或训令**；同期对西印度群岛司令部就 Miranda 远征"remain in total ignorance of their intentions"，结果是"just enough of co-operation on our part to mix us in the policy and failure of the effort"（印 p.315）。
- 【事实·亲读】转折点是消息而非战略："The popularity of this acquisition, and the disposition of the commercial interest to take advantage of it, appears first to have determined the Government to follow up this object."（印 p.316）→ **驱动英国 1806 年南美政策的是商业利益集团与舆论，不是内阁的帝国设计。**
- 【事实·亲读】三支部队口径：Auchmuty 1806-10-18 带 2,900 人赴拉普拉塔；Craufurd 11 月初带约 4,000 人赴智利"without any precise object of attack in view"；对墨西哥的方案是 8,000 人自牙买加登陆韦拉克鲁斯 + 8,000 人自印度在对岸登陆（Wellesley 指挥）（印 p.316）。
- 【事实·亲读】1807 年 5 月预计 Whitelocke 手上"nearly 10,000 men"，另 Beresford 被俘部队 1,100 人；Auchmuty 认为**有效占领该国需 15,000 人**（印 pp.318–319）。

### 1.2 关键判断（本卡最重引文）
- 【事实·亲读】"whether the value of such an occupancy and such a connexion… is such as to compensate for the drain and incumbrance it must prove upon our other military operations and on our population; and, if not, whether some principle of acting more consonant to the sentiments and interests of the people of South America cannot be taken up, which… may relieve us from **the hopeless task of conquering this extensive country, against the temper of its population**."（印 p.319）
- 【事实·亲读】为何当地人不合作："We give them no assurance of not being surrendered upon a peace to their former masters—an assurance which could alone render it prudent for them to expose themselves to the vengeance of a severe Government."（印 p.319）
- 【事实·亲读】反对扶植独立的两条理由："the probability that any local government which might be established would become **democratic and revolutionary**, and that… we might, in destroying a bad government, leave them without any government at all."（印 p.320）
- 【事实·亲读】触发条件："while the territory of Old Spain is, in fact, a French province, and whilst not only the fleets and armies of Spain, but of France, are put in motion against us by the resources of Spanish America, we are driven to consider whether those resources may not be made the means of creating and supporting an amicable and local government"（印 pp.320–321）→ **英国扶植独立的条件函数里，"西班牙资源为法国所用"是自变量。**
- 【事实·亲读】姿态限定："it seems indispensable that we should not present ourselves in any other light than as **auxiliaries and protectors**… we should be prepared to pursue our object by **a native force, to be created under our countenance**"；英国自认只求两项："the depriving our enemy of one of his chief resources, and the opening to our manufactures the markets of that great Continent."（印 p.321）
- 【事实·亲读】保王体、换执政者的"最小改写"方案："If it were possible, without dissolving the frame of the Government, without destroying the monarchy which now exists, to commit the power and authority of the Sovereign to other hands, and that **the Prince on the throne should live amongst his subjects**… such a transfer of power does not appear impracticable or objectionable"（印 pp.321–322）。
- 【事实·亲读】人选偏好："it ought to be our policy to support the pretensions of **some member of the Bourbon family**… rather than any Prince more immediately connected with our own Crown"；理由是宗教相同、且"Not being too near in the succession to the Crown of France"（印 p.322）。
- 【事实·亲读】对共和的判断："The native Americans might perhaps incline rather to a frame of government more resembling that of North America, in which their numbers might have more influence; but between such a system and the present Spanish authority they could not hesitate."（印 p.322）
- 【事实·亲读】奥尔良公爵方案："were such an enterprising individual as the Duke of Orleans, with their concurrence, to undertake it… It is probable **Miranda** might be induced to follow his fortunes; **Dumouriez** certainly would"（印 p.323）。
- 【事实·亲读】自限："His own mind is by no means settled on this point, and he begs to be understood as not so much submitting opinions to his colleagues, as throwing out such ideas as may serve to provoke discussion."（印 pp.323–324）

【判定（falsifier 直测）】卡片 falsifier 为"1806–08 英国文书示征服为主流→扶植独立下调"。**判负**：最高决策层的成文立场恰好相反——征服被明写为 hopeless，扶植本地政权被写成唯一可持续选项；但同时须限定：①这是"未定之见"（自承 not settled）；②替代方案的首选政体是**波旁王子立宪君主国**而非共和国；③英国自我设定的收益只有"断法资源 + 开市场"两项。

## 2. Castlereagh 无日期〈Memorandum relative to Spanish America〉（印 pp.385–389；txt 行 17811–18010）
- 【事实·亲读】"The liberation of South America must be accomplished through the wishes and exertions of the inhabitants; but the change can only be operated, in the first instance… under the protection and with the support of an **auxiliary British force**."（印 p.385）
- 【事实·亲读】兵力规格：对墨西哥"should not be less than **10,000 men**"，10 月在牙买加集结，12 月 1 日前在 Spanish Main 登陆；另"A small force at Trinidad, to give the impulse at the same time to **the Caraccas**"。
- 【事实·亲读】部队构成：**三分之二英军 + 三分之一黑人西印度团（Black West India Regiment）**，英军须选已适应热带气候者。（印 p.385）
- 【事实·亲读】拼盘表（印 p.386）：直布罗陀 4 团 4,000 + 马德拉 1 团 1,000 + 黑人团 4 个 4,000 + 欧洲团 3 个 3,000 = 12,000，加下马骑兵 1,200 与炮兵 = **13,200**；若对美（美国）战争之虞消失，可再抽 3,000。西印度现有 19,422 人（另牙买加 5,000、自开普在途的 60 团一营），"usual force in that quarter not exceeding 15 or 16,000"。
- 【事实·亲读】本土补法：自国内派 4,000，缺额以"the usual annual draft of fifteen men per company from the Irish Militia"补回，"The force for home defence will then stand as at present."

【推断·中高】该表给出**英国 1807 年可用于西属美洲的"不伤本土防务"上限量级 ≈1.3 万人（可加 3,000）**；与 Auchmuty"有效占领拉普拉塔需 15,000"并置，说明英国的兵力只够**在一个总督区**做"扶植型登陆"，不够同时做两处征服。此为本卡 B/英国能力包络的硬锚点之一。
