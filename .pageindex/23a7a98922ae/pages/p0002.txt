[A convention signed between the allied powers and the French government](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Convention_for_a_suspension_of_hostilities_with_France) on the 23 April 1814 was the precursor of a more comprehensive and specific arrangement; and on 30 May 1814 a definitive treaty of peace between his Britannic majesty and his most Christian majesty Louis XVIII was signed at Paris.
