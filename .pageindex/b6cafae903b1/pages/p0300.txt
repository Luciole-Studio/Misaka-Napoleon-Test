I Letters of 8th and 29th January, 6th February and 15th March 1794 from R.Voute,
  St.Petersburg, to Hope & Co., Amsterdam. According to Voute, Ritter received
  5,000 roubles for his services. A week later, however, he approached the bank with a
  request for the repayment of the sum which he had advanced, at the rate of 10,000
  roubles per week. 'And to think that this was the man who asserted that Hope & Co.
  had made millions out of Russia,' Voute scornfully added.
