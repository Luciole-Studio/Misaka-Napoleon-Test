I Letters of 1st and 8th September 1787 to B.Roosen, Hamburg, P.e. '87,129,136.
  Hope estimated the stock in Hamburg to be between 50 and 100 seroons. Roosen put
  the figure at 20-25, but he was later obliged to revise this figure. This instance shows
  how difficult it was for even a local merchant to accurately assess the quantity
  available.
