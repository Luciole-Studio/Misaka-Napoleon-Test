3 Letters of 15th and 29th December 1801 from J. Williams Hope, London to
  P.C. Labouchere, ...
  In Nantes, family matters awaited Labouchere's attention. In the margin against the
  passage concerning the chartering of a vessel there are notes: in pencil the word 'no,'
  and in ink the instruction 'I positively forbid, no hurry.' In all probability these
  remarks reveal concern on the part of Henry Hope, who did not wish to expose
  Labouchere to risks.
