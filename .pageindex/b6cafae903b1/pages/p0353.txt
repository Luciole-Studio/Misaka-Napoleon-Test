  Simons reached agreement with Hope & Co. that they would come to a settlement
  with him regarding their share of the six months' additional interest. Hope's
  contracts with the entrepreneurs, however, provided for the extra interest, amounting
  to 2i %, to go to the entrepreneurs. Godoy's memoirs make mention of Talleyrand
  receiving 21- % as 'pot-de-vin;' this figure almost corresponds with the extra interest.
  It is conceivable that Simons received t%. If this be so, the question arises where did
  the 2i %for the entrepreneurs come from? Hope received a total commission of 7 %,
  yet paid 5 %+ 2i %= 7i % to the entrepreneurs. There is, however, the point that the
  purchase price of 90 % and the conversion at 100 % of the bonds and vouchers
  surrendered (with the exception of the Echenique bonds, for which the conversion
  rate was 95 %) left an appreciable margin.
