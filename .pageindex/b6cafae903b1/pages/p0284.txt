5 Infra, 192. 'Propositions faites au Ministre du Tresor Public par M.Labouchere,
  associe de la Maison Hope & Cie. d' Amsterdam.' Labouchere received from the
  minister drafts from F.Baring & Co. to a value of 34t million francs. These were
  dated 3rd May 1803 and had been accepted by Hope & Co. In exchange for these,
  Hope was required to furnish: I) drafts issued by the Receveurs Gemiraux and
  totalling 15 million francs, of which 5 million fell due on 30 Fructidor of the year: 12
  and 10 million on 30 Vendemiaire of the year 13; 2) eight bonds involving in all 12
  million francs and falling due at monthly intervals from I st May 1804 to the end of
  that year; these were to be used for credits opened by the French Admiralty; 3) a sum
  of 5,825,000 francs in the form of six bonds from Hope & Co., payable in Paris; these
  payments, however, would not be made until the remaining two-thirds of the bonds
  had been surrendered to Hope, and pending this the Company would pay t %
  interest per month on the sum remaining unpaid. In recognition of the acceleration
  of the payments, Baring and Hope were allowed a discount of 1,675,000 francs.
