  Letter of 24th March 1788 to Bohl Bros. & Co., Cadiz, P.C. '88, 281. Bohl was

  not drop below 75 ducats, preferring to see it rise to 80. On loth April, Hope
  confirmed to Bohl the receipt of the latter's letter of 18th March. Letter of loth April
  1788 to Bohl Bros. & Co., Cadiz, P.e. '88, 335. On lIth April Hope wrote to Baring:
  'By letters from Cadiz we have advice of the arrival of The Confidence from Vera
  Cruz with 295 surons cochineal & that The Mentor & St.Raphael were daily expected
  with about 400 surons more.' (P.e. '88, 339). When a letter dated 18th March refers
  to 'daily expected,' one of 24th March which takes three weeks to reach its destination
  cannot be in time.
