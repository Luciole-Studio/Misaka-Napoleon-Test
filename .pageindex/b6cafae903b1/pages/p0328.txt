  Every Russian merchant who was a member of the First Guild (the association of
  large merchants) was permitted to purchase land on condition that payment was
  made in bonds ofthe new loan. This group, however, was prohibited from buying
  land from private individuals. The purchasers of State land were to enjoy manorial
  rights in respect of the estates so purchased, but remained in their own class and thus
  did not share the rights of the nobility. Payment for manorial assets purchased from
  the State could be effected in five annual instalments, interest being added. This
  provision was probably introduced in order to facilitate the purchase of land by
  noblemen. The first tranche, of 20 million roubles, was to be opened for
  subscription on 15th July ISIO.
