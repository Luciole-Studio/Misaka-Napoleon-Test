3 Letter of 23rd September 1803 from Jos. Morison, Riga, to Hope & Co., Amsterdam.
  Morison received an advance of 50,000 albertustalers against a contract for masts
  valued at a far greater sum. Morison was not permitted to draw until shipment was
  effected, after which the 50,000 albertustalers would be deducted from the sum of
  the final shipments. In an attempt to tum this situation to good account, Hope
  proposed to Porter Brown & Co. in St.Petersburg that they should remit to the
  Company in the event of Farmbacher's purchases leading to drafts being drawn on
  Paris, and thus to a rise in exchange rates in Russia.
