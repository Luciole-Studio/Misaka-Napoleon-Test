  Letter of 8th/ 18th November 18 I 4 to the widow of W.Borski, Matthiessen & Sillem
  and Bethmann Bros., A XXVIII, 605. The market revived following the sale of the
  200,000 poods of iron. Sillem put this down to commercial jealousy. A more
