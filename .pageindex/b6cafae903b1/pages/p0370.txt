I Letters of 21st and 24th May 1804 to J.F.Bandeira, Lisbon, 208 et seq. Bandeira
  was not 0 bliged to take more than two million pounds annually. The royal
  storehouse in Lisbon contained 600,000 pounds, and supplies in 1804 were expected
  to total 1,700,000 pounds. Labouchere took the view that if such a quantity were to
  materialize in 1804, imports in the ensuing year would have to be limited to 1-1.2
  million pounds if a fall in prices was to be avoided.
