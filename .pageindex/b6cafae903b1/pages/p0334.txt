  1812. The former had also placed a sum ofRo. 29,120 at the disposal of Sillem upon

  iron, the net proceeds totalling Ro. 171,505.89, and 141 bales, or 420,000 arshins, of
  crash, which netted Ro. 75,065. Ofthe stock of metal, 17,345 poods of Demidov's
  common bar old sable iron, 37,134 poods ofYakovlev's broad iron, 200,884 poods of
  P.S.F. common bar old sable iron and 1.438 poods of copper then remained. Sillem
  received a commission of 2 %on the sums remitted to Holland since his arrival in
  Russia.
