  Helmund & Son shipped the timber which they purchased in 12 vessels, of which
  9 sailed to Emden under the Prussian flag, and 3 to Marseilles under the Swedish flag.
  Justus Blanckenhagen shipped hemp to a value of cf 901,716.5.0., which represented
  46 % of the total shipments of this commodity. He employed 20 vessels for the
  purpose, 5 of which sailed to Morlaix, 5 to Emden, 3 to La Rochelle, 3 to Nantes and
  I to Bordeaux. The destinations of the remaining three are unknown, as are the
  nationalities of all the vessels which he employed.
