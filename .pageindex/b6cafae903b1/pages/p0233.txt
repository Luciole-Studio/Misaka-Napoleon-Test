  Whether or not all came right in the end, the letterbooks of the day do
not reveal. The last letter concerning the cochineal speculation bears the
date 16th April 1788, and later surviving correspondence bears no refer-
ence to the subject. We are, however, in possession of details of the
'Cochineal Account' covering the years 1788 to 1793 and this shows that
the speculation fared badly. At the end of 1793, 3,368 seroons and 124
sobernales, which up to that moment had cost cf 6,128,34148, including
