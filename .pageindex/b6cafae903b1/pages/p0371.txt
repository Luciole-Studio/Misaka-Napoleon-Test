3 Letter of 20th February 1809 from G.Kieckhoefer, Falmouth, to Hope & Co.,
  Amsterdam. Kieckhoefer wanted to export Silesian linen to Brazil. This could only
  be done via England, where the linen was subject to a heavy tax imposed under the
  Orders in Council, the British answer to Napoleon's Continental System. The
  Portuguese envoy promised to support the measures taken by Kieckhoefer to obtain
  exemption from the tax.
