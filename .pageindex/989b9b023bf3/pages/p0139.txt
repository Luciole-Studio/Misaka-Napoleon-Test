Among  the  journalists  of  the  day  with  whom  M. 
Biernacki  corresponded  was  Leigh  Hunt,  who  was 
at  the  time  still  confined  in  Horsemonger  Lane 
gaol  on  account  of  his  libel  on  the  Prince  Regent. 
M.  Biernacki  describes  him  as  '  a  young  man  full 
of  talent  and  learning,  romantic  and  ardent.  His 
paper  is  one  of  the  most  popular  in  England  ;  it  has  a 
sale  of  8000  copies  a  day,  which,  according  to  the  usual 
calculation  in  such  cases,  would  mean  that  it  is  read 
by  40,000  people.'  The  following  is  a  copy  of  Leigh
