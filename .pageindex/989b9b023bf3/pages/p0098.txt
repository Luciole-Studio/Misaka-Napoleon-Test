A  MEMEL,  ce  23  Juin  1807. 

One  of  the  chief  articles  of  the  Treaty  of  Tilsit 
was  the  creation  of  the  Duchy  of  Warsaw,  under  the 
rule  of  the  King  of  Saxony,  out  of  the  Polish  pro- 
vinces annexed  by  Prussia  in  the  various  partitions. 
This  event  produced  some  characteristic  manifesta- 
tions of  Polish  patriotism.  Several  persons  gave 
up  the  whole  of  their  property  for  the  mainten- 
ance of  the  Polish  army  ;  others  raised  and  equipped 
entire  regiments  at  their  own  expense.  Six  regiments 
—one  of  artillery,  two  of  cavalry,  and  three  of 
infantry — were  raised  and  placed  on  a  war  footing 
entirely  by  four  individuals  in  a  few  weeks.  Those
