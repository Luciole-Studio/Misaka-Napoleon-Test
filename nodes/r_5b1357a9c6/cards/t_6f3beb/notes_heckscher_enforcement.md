## 一、执行机器的规模与"不对称战争"

- 印p.193 / PDF209【转引Mollien, *Mémoires*, vol. III, p. 10】：
  > "In one passage Mollien speaks of the futility of the efforts of 20,000 customs officials, whose posts were known, to guard a frontier threatened by more than 100,000 smugglers, who were supposed to have good connexions in Paris and were favoured by the population besides."
  口径警告：20,000 与 100,000 都是Mollien的**修辞性量级**，Heckscher紧接着对6,000一数说"can make no higher claims than those of Mollien to express anything more than a general notion"。→ 不可当编制表或人口普查用；可当**数量级锚点**（执法者 vs 违法者 ≈ 1:5）。
- 印p.193–194 / PDF209–210【转引Bourrienne】："there were no fewer than 6,000 smugglers in Hamburg alone"。
- 印p.191 / PDF207：走私地理范围 "from Gothenburg in the north-west around all the coasts of Europe to Saloniki in the south-east, without any great variation in the methods"；巴尔干用200公斤以下小箱由驮畜经波斯尼亚、塞尔维亚、匈牙利运往维也纳。
- 印p.191 / PDF207："France proper was undoubtedly the most closely guarded country, but even there ... smuggling flourished to a very large extent."

## 二、走私的价格：**执法强度可观测的唯一连续指标**（本卡核心数据）

- 印p.194 / PDF210：
  > "In Strassburg there were 'insurers' of different grades, the chief of which charged a commission of from 4[0] to 50 per cent.; in 1809 it was considered that the expenses of passing the frontier of France were, as a rule, 30 per cent., while the above-mentioned new customs line between Rees and Bremen could be broken through for 6 or 8 per cent.; and at about the same rate it was possible to smuggle any commodity whatever from Holstein into Hamburg."
  （OCR"44)to 50"，按上下文读作40–50%；本卡记为"40–50%（OCR存疑）"。）
- 印p.194 / PDF210：枫丹白露敕令的走私业分层——`entrepreneurs / assureurs / intéressés / chefs de bande, directeurs et conducteurs de réunions de fraudeurs / simples porteurs`，Heckscher评为"a complete hierarchy ranging downwards from the directors of the smuggling enterprises through the capitalists and officials to the unskilled workers"。→ 走私不是偶发越界，是**有保险、有佣金、有分层劳动力的产业**。
- 印p.195 / PDF211：商品差别——英国工业品"came in on a large scale ... principally yarn"但被各附庸政府以保护主义理由抵制；殖民品则"all people, from the crowned ruler down to the day labourer, were of one mind"，走私"met with nothing but assistance and support"。→ **执行能力对两类禁品完全不同**，是本卡"执行上限"分类的关键。

## 三、腐败：执行机器的内生漏率

- 印p.195 / PDF211（不来梅）：原产地证明书等规费"increased tenfold during the first six quarters after the issue of the Berlin decree"。
- 印p.196 / PDF212：日内瓦"eighty customs officials had to be dismissed in seven months for complicity in malversation"；莱茵边境"the director of customs and his relations directly helped the illicit trade in the smuggling centre of Strassburg"，"the customs lieutenants on the Rhine lived on bribes when they had no British pension"。
- 印p.196 / PDF212：拿破仑1811年初估算Bourrienne在汉堡获利"seven or eight million francs"；Bourrienne最终被禁签原产地证明并去职。
- 印p.203–204 / PDF219–220：拿破仑把贿赂**收归己有**——柯尼斯堡领事Clérembault放行14艘英船（货值2,800,000法郎），一案得800,000法郎、合计1,500,000–1,600,000；皇帝令其向外交部上交，拟令Bourrienne缴2,000,000、Lachevardière缴500,000给偿债基金；Clérembault已先自付500,000入皇帝私库(caisse de l'extraordinaire)。致Champagny 1811-01-01信结语：
  > "You will see that I shall get the money for a really handsome palace which will cost me nothing."
  【转引 *Lettres inédites* nos. 733, 748；*Correspondance* no. 17,267】
- 印p.197 / PDF213：Davout是"very few persons who from the beginning to the end really made the resolute execution of the Continental System the lodestar of all his conduct"，因此在汉堡被称 Marshal "Wuth"（Fury）。→ **严格执行者是稀缺品，且其稀缺性本身是体系的结构特征**。

## 四、财政转向（fiscalism）：从"禁"到"抽租"

- 印p.197 / PDF213 法国本国关税收入（法郎）：1806 = 51,200,000；1807 = 60,600,000；1808 = 18,600,000（"less than one-third"）；1809 = 11,600,000。
  → 与F4卡 Marion IV p.304–306 的净额序列（1808=18.559m、1809=11.552m）在同一量级、几乎同数：**两者很可能同源于官方序列，不构成独立互证**。
- 印p.197–198 / PDF213–214：Collin de Sussy（法国海关总监）与Montalivet（稍后内政部长）的方案——"the state might be able to enter into what was literally a competition with the smugglers ... the importation of the hitherto forbidden goods was to be permitted, but only on payment of a duty that exactly corresponded to an amount which ... the smuggling business had previously cost. In that case no more goods would come into the country than had been the case beforehand, but the profit would fall to the state instead of to the smugglers."
  → **这是特里亚农体系的官方经济学：关税=走私成本的国有化**。本卡"租金再分配"的一手理论依据。
- 印p.198 / PDF214 Heckscher的判词："fiscalism had definitively gotten the upper hand over the Continental System, at least in one-half of its range. The object was no longer to exclude goods, but to make an income by receiving them instead; and no sophistry in the world could make the latter compatible with the former."
- 印p.198 / PDF214【转引 *Correspondance* no. 16,983，1810-10-03致热罗姆】：新制既增收入，又使"the continental customers of the English merchants will not be able to pay for them (the goods), and the consumption of colonial goods, which will be rendered dear in this way, will be diminished."
- 印p.199 / PDF215 **缉获品法令（1810-01-12）**：禁止进口之货（除某些棉织品与针织品外），若来自军舰或持照私掠船捕获之敌方战利品，缴**48%**关税即可入境，称"origines permises"。【*Bulletin des lois* 4e sér., bull. 260, no. 5,122】
- 印p.200 / PDF216 **荷兰并入法令（1810-07-09）第10条**：荷兰境内大量殖民品存货按**价值50%**（另一说按申报时间40%或50%）纳税入帝国；凡距法国边界**四日行程以内**的殖民品存货一律视为损法而须受法军检查，"French troops were actually employed for the purpose"；关税**可以实物缴纳**（并可用期票），Heckscher说没有这一让步"it is certain that in many cases such large amounts could not have been gathered in"。
- 印p.200 / PDF216：为防止货物逃往他国，要求全大陆**同时**施行，故令欧仁暂时保密。
- 印p.201 / PDF217 **特里亚农税则（1810-08-05）**：按重量（每100公斤）从量征收，非从价；战利品与许可证货物似仍分别适用40%与50%。税率对比（每100公斤法郎）：

  | 黎凡特棉（海路） | — | 400 |
  | 黎凡特棉（经莱茵海关） | — | 〔OCR"WOO"不可辨，未冻结〕 |
  | 其他棉（那不勒斯除外） | — | 600 |
  | 靛蓝 | 1803=15 | **900**；1813-01再升至 **1,100** |
  | 丁香 | 1806=3 | 600 |
  | 茶（绿） | 3（另某些情形从价10%） | 600；其他茶150 |

  1810-09-27补充税则再加约三十个税目。
- 印p.202–203 / PDF218–219 **枫丹白露敕令（1810-10-18，档案作10-19）**：走私首领十年苦役＋烙印；下层工具可减为轻罪刑＋5–10年警察监视；殖民品走私四年苦役；"simple smuggling"（无协议、无承保义务者）不入苦役。货物处置：殖民品照旧没收、每六个月拍卖一次；**禁品（工业制品）公开焚毁或销毁**，事先列单标价。为此设**cours prévôtales des douanes**（海关军法法庭），"the operations of which have stood out to later generations as the culmination of the oppression involved in the Continental System"。

- 印p.203 / PDF219：焚货先例来自**英国十七世纪立法**，至乔治三世初年仍重复（3 Geo. III, c. 21）；1810-12-09《Moniteur》即以英国近例作文章。→ 焚货不是拿破仑独创的野蛮，而是重商主义惩罚传统的重启。
- 印p.204 / PDF220：特里亚农与枫丹白露之后"we find proportionally a still larger number of examples of bribery and embezzlement ... than before"；拿破仑的应对是"demand a share of the bribes ... convert them into sponges with which to soak up revenue from the illicit trade"。

## 五、走私的"常态化"与官方共谋

- 印p.192 / PDF208：法方用英文借词"smoggler"；Boucher de Perthes（1811–12布洛涅海关副监察）称其为"contrabandists of their (the British) nation, who are attached to our police and who at the same time carry on a traffic in prisoners of war and guineas"。
- 印p.192–193 / PDF208–209【*Correspondance* nos. 13,718、16,508；*Lettres inédites* nos. 874, 877】1808-04-03致荷兰王路易："If you need to sell your gin, the English need to buy it. Settle the points where the English smugglers are to come and fetch it, and make them pay in money but never in commodities."；1810-05-29致Gaudin："My intention is to favour the export of foodstuffs from France and the import of money from abroad ... I should be very much inclined to let the smugglers in only at Dunkirk, unless current practice required that they should also be received at Flushing."
  Heckscher评：走私者"were necessary for the prosperity of Dunkirk and made that town exempt from the general crippling of economic life in the ports"。（1808年信真伪Heckscher自注"not altogether above suspicion"。）
- 印p.190 / PDF206【转引Rist, *Lebenserinnerungen* II, pp.106 ff.】汉堡"filtration"：海关官员放任妇孺"Träger"分散携货，"If the officials seized every third or fourth 'bearer' ... they derived a fine income from it; but the traffic was not at all disturbed by this, for losses were part of the business"。
  → **缉获率 1/3–1/4 是"被容忍的税率"而非执法成效**；本卡缉获率参数的唯一同时代叙述性锚点。

## 六、许可证制度（印pp.205–220 / PDF221–236）

- 印p.215 / PDF231【*Correspondance* no. 16,930，1810-09-19致欧仁】拿破仑亲自下的定义：
  > "A licence is a permission, accorded to a vessel that fulfils the conditions exacted by the said licence, to import or export a certain kind of merchandise specified in that licence. **For those vessels the Berlin and Milan decrees are null and void.**"
- 印p.215 / PDF231 **许可证敕令（1810-07-25）**【Martens, *Nouveau recueil* I, p. 512】：自1810-08-01起，凡驶往外国港口的船只非有**拿破仑亲笔签署**的许可证不得离开法国港口；驶往帝国内港或地中海沿岸贸易者需 acquit-à-caution 及保结，须凭抵港证明才解除。理由：没有任何 grand commerce 可以不停靠英国港口或不受英方检查，而后者按米兰敕令即构成"denationalization"与没收。→ **许可证制度在法理上是对自己两大基本法（柏林／米兰敕令）的逐船豁免权**。
- 印p.215 / PDF231：拿破仑甚至要求法国船挂中立旗进伦敦，"Under this disguise England receives them, and I make laws for her owing to her pressing need of commercial intercourse." 1812年甚至发过**从伦敦进口大米**的许可证。
- 印p.216 / PDF232 **许可证价格**（本卡租金账核心）：早期 30 或 40 napoleons（600 或 800 法郎）；后期汉萨出口证 40 napoleons（800 法郎）＋小麦每吨30法郎、黑麦每吨15法郎"was regarded as cheap"；**自英国进口殖民品的许可证高达 300 napoleons ＝ 6,000 法郎**，"much higher amounts than the British licences"。拿破仑不讳言其目的是 *un revenu considérable*。
- 印p.216–217 / PDF232–233 **出口义务**：进口须搭配等值（至少同值）的法国／意大利商品回程货；典型组合许可证＝德意志帝国港口谷物→敦刻尔克→英国卸粮→装海军物料回敦刻尔克→再装法国葡萄酒、丝绸、制品运汉堡。制度源头是**1803年法国海关条例**中对可疑船只的惩罚性规定。
- 印p.217–218 / PDF233–234 **出口义务的失败**："the goods were taken on board, of course, but as their importation was prohibited in England, and as, moreover, they were not in a position to compete with British manufactures, there could be no sale."→ 出口商买"rossignols"（滞销垃圾货）凑数，1812年法国商工会议公开承认"the goods were simply thrown into the sea"。Heckscher的一般命题：
  > "purely coercive laws in the sphere of economics have far fewer possibilities of being made effective in a positive direction than in a negative one."
  → **本卡执行能力上限的理论支点：禁止可以执行，强制交易不可以执行。**
- 印p.218–219 / PDF234–235 **法国船运垄断**：1810-08-20致勒布伦（荷兰）信："The article applies to all kinds of vessels, French, neutral or foreign; that is to say, with the exception that I do not grant licences to other than French vessels. In two words, I will not hear of any neutral vessel, and as a matter of fact there is in reality no such thing; for they are all vessels which violate the blockade and pay tribute to England." 致欧仁9-19信更要求船须**在法国建造**。汉萨、但泽、意大利城市可得证但费率"unusually high"；盟国原则上与中立国同样被排除。
- 印p.219 / PDF235 **对丹麦（最忠实盟友）的实际后果**：其船只在拿破仑各港被扣，1812年春释放时"there were still eighty left"，释放条件是出口巨量法国丝绸——"an absolute impossibility"；同时丹麦还须供船运粮往荷兰、供官兵给斯海尔德备战舰队。
- 印p.220 / PDF236 Heckscher总判：体系"had partly shot past him and become a means of promoting the interests of France ... at the expense of her own helpers in the struggle against Great Britain." 引1811年秋丹麦驻巴黎公使Waltersdorff语：法国除对英贸易外别无贸易，自然想独占之。

## 七、特里亚农-枫丹白露体系的实际运行（印pp.221–237 / PDF237–253）

### 7.1 财政成绩（**本卡"许可证版S3"收益上限的硬锚**）
- 印p.221 / PDF237：
  > "Napoleon's customs revenues alone rose to **105,900,000 francs in the period from the Trianon tariff to the close of 1811**, this as compared with only 11,600,000 francs in 1809; and the auctions of confiscated goods, together with the licence fees, brought in far more, to say nothing of what the vassal states contrived to make."
  **口径对账（与F4卡）**：Marion IV p.306 记"08/1810–01/1812累计105.927667"，与此几乎同数但**区间端点不同**（Heckscher止于1811年底，Marion止于1812年1月）→ 两者同源于法国海关账，不作独立互证；引用时须标区间。
- 印p.222 / PDF238【转引Thiers】：1810年剩余数月内**没收货拍卖现金收入近150,000,000法郎**。
- 印p.226 / PDF242【转引Darmstädter】：法兰克福1810-10一役对法国国库产出 **9,000,000 法郎**。
- 印p.226 / PDF242：荷尔斯泰因存货按特里亚农税率放入汉堡，**法国国库仅实物缴纳一项得19,700,000法郎，合计42,500,000法郎**；Rist述通宁(Tönning)公路昼夜车马不绝，"half the peasants of Holstein had deserted their fields"，"Cotton lay all about the fields like snow"。

### 7.2 司法与军事执行机器
- 印p.222–223 / PDF238–239：新海关人员"is represented as a rabble scraped together from different countries"，昼夜入室搜查；Rist述汉堡 prévôtal court 为"a tribunal of blood ... the most frightful tool of fiscal despotism"，*Le Grand Prévôt*"half customs official and half judge"，位阶等同于省长与高等法院院长。
- 印p.223 / PDF239 **可量化执法产出**：1812年汉堡 Grand Prévôt **两周内判出120个六月徒刑**，全部为违反封锁令；汉堡监狱爆满，**100名囚犯转送安特卫普苦役船**；不来梅监狱条件致 **22½% 的囚犯死亡**。另有死刑判决并执行，而枫丹白露敕令本身并无死刑依据。
- 印p.223 / PDF239【转引Bourrienne】：1811年一名家长因在易北省进口**一小块糖块**几被枪决，"possibly at the very moment when Napoleon was signing licences for the importation of a million sugar-loaves"。
- 印p.224 / PDF240 **军事警戒线**：Masséna旧军团（时归Oudinot）自布洛涅沿布拉班特、荷兰海岸布防，最强一师置于埃姆登以与汉萨联络；其次为达武军团——Thiers称"the finest, most reliable, and best organized"、"the invincible third corps"，是**大陆短暂和平期内全军唯一保持战时编制的军**：三个师，每师五个步兵团各四营（合六十营）＋80门炮，另加胸甲骑兵一师、轻骑兵一师、大型攻城列车、河口炮艇队；最东前哨为拉普（Rapp）在但泽的部队。1810-09-28致达武信"expressly declared that the two divisions stationed along the German North Sea coast had as their sole task the prevention of smuggling"。
  → **本卡"执行能力上限"的最硬证据：帝国把和平期唯一的战备军团当缉私队用。**
- 印p.224 / PDF240：1810年末沿海修筑大量工事，此前"a plan to capture Heligoland without maritime forces had had to be abandoned"。

### 7.3 藩属与盟国的执行差异（**执行的政治边界**）
- 印p.225 / PDF241：按法方看法，特里亚农税则起初"remained a dead letter in all the states of the Confederation of the Rhine, **except Baden**"；普鲁士与萨克森试图把原料排除在税则之外；"the somewhat more independent states, such as **Russia, Austria, and Sweden, never, so far as is known, introduced the tariff as a whole**"。Heckscher判断正是1810年8–9月这种消极抵抗促成了10月的枫丹白露敕令。
- 印p.225 / PDF241 **法兰克福事件（1810-10-17/18）**：名义主权邦，两个法国步兵团在大公未获通知的情况下开入，占领城门、广场架炮、张贴敕令、令申报一切殖民品与英货；法国海关人员搜查全部货栈、加封地窖、扣押账簿信件，"the whole of the great trade movement was stopped"；11-08新令准其按特里亚农税率纳税放行。
- 印p.227 / PDF243 **关税的"消费税化"**：各国重复征税对法兰克福等中转邦"evidently fatal"，"gradually an arrangement was made whereby the tariff was generally applied as **a tax on consumption, not as a transit duty, but with freedom for goods that had once paid the duty**"。→ **这是史实中最接近"大陆关税同盟原则"的一步，且它是从财政压力而非制度设计中生出来的**（本卡S5关税同盟化判断的一手入口）。
- 印p.227 / PDF243：普鲁士允许**以市价59.5%的政府债券按面值缴纳**特里亚农税，再以普鲁士完税证明放货过境；1811年春夏普鲁士证明被宣布无效并再次突击查抄。
- 印p.227 / PDF243 **拍卖地理**：没收货拍卖"preferably in towns which lay at some distance from the great smuggling places, because the prices were highest there"——首推**安特卫普**，其次法兰克福、科隆、美因茨、斯特拉斯堡、米兰、威尼斯。拍卖使殖民贸易"given a constant source of supply alongside the smuggled goods and the duty-paid imports"，并创造"a possibility, besides smuggling, of purchasing the goods at a rate lower than the foreign price plus the customs duty"。→ **国家自己成了第三条供给渠道，并在压低自己税则的有效税率**。

### 7.4 焚货（autos-da-fé）的真实成色
- 印p.227–228 / PDF243–244：英国工业品一律销毁，此项"Napoleon never, so far as is known, made an exception"；但Heckscher强调**这一半反而更无效**——"the reason is the total absence of pecuniary interest, public and private, in obedience to the latter regulations"。仪式有军乐与地方显要出席，"the ceremony was just as great whatever was the real value of the goods burnt"。
- 印p.228 / PDF244 **法兰克福实例**：1810-11首次清查，金币卷放进专设抽屉后"the goods became Swiss or Saxon instead of British"；实际付之一炬者价值仅**800,000法郎**，官方估价 **1,800,000**（OCR"1,&00,000"）。1811年春第二次清查，弗里德贝格犹太人Cassella充当替罪羊，仅其英国棉布被焚；市长坦言"When they were spread out, there seemed to be a lot of cloth, and they could give the impression of a great quantity at the burning"，目的是"to ward off unpleasantness from France, not to ruin our own population"。
- 印p.229 / PDF245：Servières（汉萨）与M. Schäfer（不来梅）所载焚货**总值约4,500,000法郎**，但"many burnings took place for which we have no figures"，且"it is very difficult to determine the truth behind the official statements"。焚烧发生于"hundreds of towns from one end to the other of the territory of Napoleon and his allies, **with the sole exception of Denmark**"。Heckscher评其为"a very cunning display of power"，效果是"tremendous though altogether exaggerated impression"。

### 7.5 贸易路线的重排（执行造成的**地理转移**而非消灭）
- 印p.230 / PDF246：许可证使**海路重新合法化**，终结了斯特拉斯堡作为法国进口（合法与非法）集散地的繁荣。
- 印p.230 / PDF246：1811年夏起莱比锡集市上英货"practical cessation"、殖民品亦降至微量；法兰克福反而受损较小。Heckscher解释：萨克森有**真实的守法意愿**（其纺织工业受益于排斥英国竞争），法兰克福无此利益。→ **执行力最强处＝本地产业利益与禁令一致处**，这是本卡反应函数的关键机制。
- 印pp.231–232 / PDF247–248 **Bacher报告（1810-10-02，Schmidt《Le Grand-duché de Berg》印行）**——法国驻莱茵邦联公使的预测：
  > "the Danube will now take the place of the Rhine as the channel through which the states of the Confederation of the Rhine will in future be able to provide themselves."
  并预言：即使萨克森王把关税警戒线从维滕贝格延至波希米亚边界并对原棉课税，"this painful sacrifice, which would reduce the whole of the mountainous part of Saxony [Erzgebirge] to the deepest misery, would be no profit to France. It would only enrich the government and merchants of Austria"；棉纺工人将"be compelled to emigrate from Saxony and Voigtland, and even from Bavaria, Baden, and Switzerland, in order to seek their livelihood in the Austrian factories erected and managed by Englishmen"。
- 印pp.232–233 / PDF248–249：维也纳取代莱比锡成为大陆贸易枢纽；货路两条同至**加利西亚的Brody**——北线经普俄波罗的海港口绕华沙公国，南线先自敖德萨过黑海、法俄战争爆发后改经君士坦丁堡与萨洛尼卡至伦贝格；再经**巴伐利亚免税过境**输往南德、瑞士，并走私入法。
- 印p.233 / PDF249 **运费即关税（转引Tooke, *History of Prices* I, pp.309–310注）**，1809–12 vs 1837：小麦运费每夸特 50先令 vs 4先令6便士；大麻每吨 £30 vs £2 10s；木材每load £30 vs £1；生丝自贝加莫经士麦那／阿尔汉格尔斯克绕行，历时一年／两年；经法国每包费用£100（另加勒阿弗尔至英国运费）。**一艘一百余吨船自加来到伦敦往返的运费＋法国许可证费可达 £50,000**，折靛蓝每英磅运费4先令6便士 vs 1837年1便士（1/54）；一艘总值£4,000的船自波尔多至伦敦往返毛运费 £80,000。
  → **本卡的"走私弹性"可用这一组价格当"执法税率"的上界观测：体系把贸易成本抬高一至两个数量级，却没有把贸易归零。**

### 7.6 波罗的海与哥德堡：执行机器够不到的地方
- 印p.234 / PDF250：1810年夏600艘英国商船（挂各中立旗、货值£8,000,000–9,000,000）滞留哥德堡，9月进入波罗的海驶往瑞典、俄国、普鲁士港口；Champagny称其"wandering about like the fragments of a scattered army"；拿破仑以威胁与分赃利诱各国没收。
- 印pp.234–235 / PDF250–251 **结果**：梅克伦堡驱逐船只；普鲁士让步但柯尼斯堡领事Clérembault使查扣落空；**亚历山大拒绝全面没收**，称到港者不过约60艘（Caulaincourt按俄方说法称9月中以来载货者仅15艘）；1816年英商备忘录称被扣140船货、价值£1,500,000（Heckscher认为"much too high"）。瑞典1811年春在**卡尔斯港(Karlshamn)误扣百余艘**（挂丹麦、普鲁士、汉堡、帕彭堡旗），查明为英国财产（估值£500,000）后以"视为瑞典货＋虚拟回购"处理，"the British here lost nothing"。1811年8月底拿破仑信称已有**150艘挂美国旗的船**进入俄国港口。
- 印p.236 / PDF252 **哥德堡**：1810-09 总督 Axel Pontus von Rosen 描述锚地"such an appearance as it had never had since the Creation, with **19 British men-of-war and 1,124 merchantmen** lying at anchor"；某日风转东，"several hundred vessels sailed away at the same time"。11月给von Rosen的训令：对瑞典臣民所有、挂美旗或其他可接受旗的货船，"His Majesty does not require you to recur to extremities of diligence, but on the contrary to **suppress facts and facilitate traffic** as far as you may do so ... without compromising your position."
- 印p.236 / PDF252 **哥德堡数量**：进口1807→1809翻两番(quadrupled)，1810再增至五倍(quintupled)；原糖出口 **14,500,000磅**（约为上年两倍），咖啡 **4,500,000磅**，尚不计经瑞典本土转运出去的部分。
- 印pp.236–237 / PDF252–253：波罗的海内的 **Hanö 与 Matvik**（布莱金厄省）由瑞典政府训令同时充当英国分舰队基地与殖民品／制品集散地；瑞典整体是英美贸易通往俄国与波罗的海南岸的转运国，因该路线"more secure from French and Danish privateers than the direct route"。
