## A. 转口贸易与农业出口规模（章I "Beleaguered Britain"，行1690–1725，页眉30前后）
> "in 1806 the United States exported $20 million worth of her own goods to Britain and only $2.7 million worth to France. Total American domestic exports topped $41 million in 1806 and reached a peak of nearly $49 million in 1807."

> "That year, 64 million pounds of cotton, 1.2 million bushels of wheat, 62,000 hogsheads of tobacco, and large amounts of hemp, beef, corn, and other commodities were shipped abroad."

> "The reexport trade expanded even more spectacularly, from an infinitesimal amount in 1791 to $60 million in 1806. In 1806, 47 million pounds of coffee, more than 146 million pounds of sugar, and nearly 2 million pounds of cotton imported into the United States were reexported to Europe. The government encouraged this commerce by returning, in the form of drawbacks, almost all customs duties levied at the time of the original importation."

> "This trade permitted goods from French and Spanish colonies in the Caribbean, as well as the lie de France and Bourbon, to reach the home country. It cast the protection of a neutral flag over supplies Napoleon woefully needed, frustrating the Royal Navy's efforts to break up French commerce."

> "Britain scarcely benefited at all, for less than 5 per cent of all reexported commodities went to England and her colonies. Many Englishmen angrily pronounced American merchants and shipowners effectively allied with the Emperor Napoleon."

【卡内判读】①美国是法国殖民地体系与欧洲之间**唯一规模化的中立承运人**；②英国的敌意有物质基础（转口货95%+不进英帝国市场、压低西印度价格）；③"美国商人事实上与拿破仑结盟"是**英国当时的认知**，不是本卡对美国政策的判定。

## B. 强征水手（章VII前后，行4490–4630）
> "that three-quarters of those impressed had been born in America."（对某组样本的主张）
> 行4552：国务卿Monroe"claimed that 6,257 Americans had been impressed"；行4555：该数"arrived at by totaling applications for the release of impressed"（即申请汇总，非独立核实）。
> 行4599–4615：联邦党方面"purported to show that Britain had impressed far less than 800"；另有"approximately 1,000 Americans had been impressed"说；"in 1812 the British released 1,800 seamen who had been impressed"。
【口径冲突登记】强征美国人总数三口径：6,257（Monroe，申请累加）／≈1,000／<800（联邦党辩论口径）；1812释放1,800。→ 本卡不取单值，记区间并注生成机制。

## C. 1810 拿破仑对美政策：Rambouillet 与 Cadore（章VII "America's Humiliation"，行11845–11960，页眉244–245）
> "On March 23, 1810, by the Rambouillet Decree, the French monarch ordered all American ships that entered his ports seized, whether or not they had touched at British [ports]"

> Napoleon（1810-06-25 致内政部长的笔记，转引 Correspondance XX, 501–502）："We will engage ourselves to nothing … but we can … let those goods which we need enter."

> 1810-07-31 拿破仑致Cadore：因Macon法案，"my decrees of Berlin and Milan will not have any effect after November first," 条件是英方撤枢密令或"the Congress of the United States shall fulfil the engagement it has made to reestablish its prohibitions on English commerce."

> 1810-08-05 Cadore致Armstrong正式声明，把"禁止对英贸易"的明确要求替换为较模糊的"conformably with [Macon's Bill] …, shall cause their rights to be respected by the English."

> Perkins裁断："Was it really correct to say that the decrees were 'revoked,' when their effect was not to cease until November and then only if one of two conditions was fulfilled? Quite obviously not"；Cadore本人随后声明"decision of H.M. is … conditional."

【卡内判读·承重】卡多尔信是**附条件的诱饵**而非撤令；但麦迪逊选择据此对英重启不进口。这正是"对法扣船怨恨"与"对英开战"并存的关键机制：法国给了一张**可被美国政府当作外交工具使用的纸**，英国没有给。
